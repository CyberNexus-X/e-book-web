English Speaking Books for Beginners in Hindi – Kaunsi Book, Kab, Aur Kyun

November 6, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: Agar tum bilkul zero se English bolna seekhna chahte ho, to pehle Rapidex English Speaking Course uthao — wo Hindi me samjhata hai aur ek poore course ki tarah step by step le jaata hai. Agar thodi English aati hai aur bas bolte waqt atak jaate ho, to V. Sasikumar aur P.V. Dhamija ki Spoken English se shuru karo — usme roz-marra ki baat-cheet ke dialogue hain. Aur agar tum YouTube se seekhne wale ho, to Aditya Rana ki Spoken English Guru Book tumhare liye hai, kyunki uske saath free video lessons bhi milte hain. Aur usse bhi pehle, ek kaam free me karo: British Council ki LearnEnglish site ya app kholo aur ek lesson sun lo. Book khareedne se pehle bolna shuru kar dena sabse samajhdaari wala kadam hai.

Honestly, English seekhne walon ki sabse badi dikkat book ki kami nahi hai. Dikkaat ye hai ki har list me wahi 20-25 naam hote hain, par koi ye nahi batata ki pehle kaunsi, kis ke liye, aur kaunsi abhi mat uthao. Ek list tumhe seedha Wren & Martin ki grammar pakda degi — jo achhi reference book hai, par pehli book nahi. Doosri list tumhe Word Power Made Easy me dhakel degi, jab tumhe abhi ye bhi nahi pata ki "How are you?" ke baad kya bolna hai. Dono adhoore hain. Is article me 8 books hain, ek seedhe order me: buniyad, phir dialogue, phir grammar aur shabd, phir ek zaroori counterpoint. Aur har ek ke saath ye bhi likha hai ki ye kis ke liye nahi hai.

Ek baat pehle hi saaf kar dete hain, kyunki isme dhoka sabse zyada hai. Koi book, koi course, koi app tumhe ye guarantee nahi de sakta ki 30 din me fluent ho jaoge. Jo "30 din me fluent English" ka dawa kare, usse door raho — chahe uska cover kitna bhi chamakdaar ho. Book tumhe shabd, sentence aur tareeka deti hai. Bolna sirf bolne se aata hai. Is article ka plan isi liye sirf padhne ka plan nahi hai — har hafte me bolne ka kaam bhi hai.

Pehle Ye Samajh Lo: Book Bolna Nahi Sikhati, Bolna Sikhati Hai

Ye baat thodi kadvi hai, par isi se shuruaat karni chahiye. English bolna ek skill hai, jaise cycle chalana ya khana banana. Cycle chalana kitaab padh kar nahi aata — pehle din tum girte ho, phir balance aata hai. English bhi waisi hi hai. Tum grammar ke 500 rule yaad kar lo, phir bhi pehli baar kisi se English me baat karte waqt munh se shabd nahi niklenge. Kyunki padhna aur bolna, dono alag kaam hain.

Ek chhota sa example. Socho tumne ek book se seekha ki dukaan par "How much is this?" bolte hain. Ab tum dukaan gaye, dukaan wale ne jawab me kuch bola jo book me nahi tha, aur tum atak gaye. Iska matlab ye nahi ki book bekaar thi. Iska matlab ye hai ki book ne tumhe pehla sentence diya tha, doosra sentence tumhe khud banana tha. Jo insaan sirf book padhta hai aur kabhi bolta nahi, wo pehle sentence par hi atka rehta hai. Jo roz 10 minute bolta hai — chahe akele me, chahe sheeshe ke saamne — wo doosra, teesra, chautha sentence bhi banana seekh jaata hai.

Isi liye is list ka order aisa hai. Pehle buniyad — Hindi me samajh, chhote sentence. Phir dialogue — roz-marra ki baat-cheet, zor se bol kar practice. Phir grammar aur shabd — jo galti baar-baar ho, usi ko sudhaarna. Aur aakhir me ek counterpoint — ki kabhi-kabhi picture wali book ya sirf sunna, padhne se zyada kaam ka ho sakta hai. Ye aakhri baat bahut se logon ko pasand nahi aati, par isi liye ye list me hai.

Free Me Shuru Karo: British Council Aur YouTube

Book khareedne se pehle ek hafta free material par lagao. British Council ki LearnEnglish website aur app par free lessons, audio aur practice material milta hai — sunna, bolna, grammar, vocabulary, sab kuch, aur wo bhi aise level par jo beginner ke liye bana hai. Uske saath Spoken English Guru ke free YouTube lessons hain, jo Hindi me samjhate hain aur jinhe karodon log dekhte hain. Ye material isliye kaam ka hai, kyunki isme koi kuch bech nahi raha — sirf seekhna hai.

Ek side note, jo kaam aayega. Internet par "spoken english book pdf free download" likh kar jo pehli PDF mile, usko mat uthao. Copyrighted kitaabon ki random PDF aksar illegal hoti hain, aur kabhi-kabhi adhoori ya galat bhi. Free chahiye to official free lo — British Council, YouTube ke free lessons, ya library se. Ye chhota sa check tumhe galat material se bacha lega.

8 Books, Ek Seedha Order

Neeche har book ke saath teen cheezein milengi: ye book kya sikhati hai, ye kis ke liye hai, aur isme dhyan rakhne wali baat kya hai. Order ko ulta mat karo, khaas kar pehli teen books ka. Aur ek baat: neeche jo daam likhe hain, wo October 2026 me check kiye gaye the — daam badalte rehte hain, isliye khareedne se pehle Amazon ya Flipkart par aaj ka daam zaroor dekh lo.

1. Rapidex English Speaking Course — Pustak Mahal

Ye list ki sabse purani, aur isi liye pehli, book hai. Rapidex 1976 se chal rahi hai — matlab tumhare maa-baap ke zamane se bhi pehle se. Ye ek poore course ki tarah likhi gayi hai: pehle basic shabd, phir chhote sentence, phir poori baat-cheet. Aur sabse badi baat: iske alag-alag edition Hindi aur doosri Indian bhashaon me milte hain, isliye tumhe concept samajhne ke liye English se ladna nahi padega.

Kis ke liye? Un logon ke liye jo bilkul zero se shuru kar rahe hain. Hindi medium se aaye ho, English padhna aata hai par bolna nahi, ya ghar par koi English bolne wala nahi hai — agar tumhari kahani ye hai, to ye book tumhari pehli book hai. Lagbhag ₹265 se shuru hoti hai (MRP ₹345), isliye jeb par bhi bhaari nahi padegi.

Dhyan rakhne wali baat: Ye book purani hai, isliye uski kuch sentences aaj ke hisaab se formal ya purani lag sakti hain. Aur ek aur baat: Hindi se translate kar ke seekhne ki aadat agar zyada ho jaaye, to dimaag me pehle Hindi banta hai, phir English — aur bolte waqt waqt lagta hai. Isliye Rapidex se shuruaat karo, par usi par atke mat raho. Doosri book par jaane ka waqt tab hai jab tum chhote sentence bina soche bolne lago.

2. Spoken English Guru Book — Aditya Rana

Ab un logon ki baari jo YouTube se seekhte hain. Aditya Rana ka Spoken English Guru channel Hindi me English sikhane ke sabse bade channels me se ek hai — October 2026 tak 8.2 million se zyada subscribers. Usi channel ki book hai ye: "English Speaking Course Book (Complete Grammar & Spoken Topics)". Grammar ke rules Hindi aur English dono me samjhaye gaye hain, aur aakhir me ek mini dictionary bhi hai. Kul 208 pages.

Kis ke liye best hai? Un Hindi readers ke liye jo pehle se YouTube par English ke video dekhte hain aur ab usi cheez ko book me, ek order me, chahte hain. Agar tum video dekh kar seekhne wale ho, to ye book tumhare liye hai — kyunki ye book akele nahi, video ke saath kaam karti hai. Lagbhag ₹225 se shuru hoti hai (MRP ₹305).

Ek imaandaar baat: Ye book tabhi poori kaam aati hai jab tum uske saath wale video bhi dekhte ho. Sirf book padh li aur video nahi dekha, to aadhi baat reh jaayegi. Aur haan, iski zyadatar samjhaish Hindi me hai, isliye jo Hindi nahi samajhte unke liye ye book kam kaam ki hai.

3. Spoken English: A Self-Learning Guide to Conversation Practice — V. Sasikumar & P.V. Dhamija

Ab asli baat-cheet ki book. Ye book grammar par nahi, dialogue par khadi hai. Bank me kya bolna hai, doctor ke paas kaise bolna hai, railway station par kya poochna hai, interview me kya jawab dena hai — roz-marra ki situation ke dialogue isme hain. Kuch editions ke saath audio CD bhi milti hai, jisse tum sun kar bhi seekh sakte ho.

Kis ke liye? Un logon ke liye jo basic English jaante hain, par bolte waqt hesitation hoti hai. Student ho, job ki taiyaari kar rahe ho, ya interview dene wale ho — agar tumhe lagta hai ki "samajh to aata hai, bol nahi pata", to ye book tumhare liye hai. Lagbhag ₹270 se shuru hoti hai (MRP ₹285, 3rd edition).

Dhyan rakhne wali baat: Ye ek textbook jaisi book hai, isliye iska fayda tabhi hai jab tum dialogue ko zor se padho, ya kisi partner ke saath practice karo. Chup-chaap padh kar aage badh gaye to kuch haath nahi aayega. Har dialogue ko kam se kam do baar zor se bolo — ek baar padh kar, ek baar bina dekhe.

4. Speak English Like a Star — Yogesh Vermani

Ab grammar ki baari, par bolne ke saath. Vermani ki book ka kaam ye hai: grammar ko baat-cheet se jodna. Isme English aur Hindi dono me samjhaish hai, aur Indian learners ki aam galtiyon par khaas dhyan diya gaya hai — wahi galtiyan jo hum sab karte hain, jaise "I am agree" bolna ya tense me atak jaana. Sentence banane ki practice bhi isme hai, sirf rule ratne ka kaam nahi.

Kis ke liye best hai? Un logon ke liye jo ab bol to rahe hain, par galtiyaan baar-baar ho rahi hain. Student, job seeker, professional, homemaker — jise bhi lagta hai ki "bol to leta hoon, par sahi nahi bolta", uske liye ye book hai. Lagbhag ₹450 se shuru hoti hai (MRP ₹600).

Ek imaandaar baat: Ye book Rapidex se kam famous hai, isliye khareedne se pehle latest edition aur reader reviews check kar lo. Aur iske kuch hisse grammar-heavy hain — unko padh kar chhod do, roz bolne ki practice ke saath-saath unko wapas kholo.

5. English Conversation Practice — Grant Taylor

Ab intermediate level ki book. Taylor ki book me 27 units hain, aur har unit ek roz-marra ki situation ke aas-paas bana hai. Har unit me ek taraf practice ke sawaal hain, doosri taraf model jawab — matlab tum ghar par akele bhi practice kar sakte ho. Interview wala unit bhi isme hai, jo job ki taiyaari walon ke liye khaas kaam ka hai.

Ek chhota sa test: agar tum kisi se English me baat karte waqt sirf "yes", "no", "okay" bol kar ruk jaate ho, to tumhe naye shabd nahi, naye sentence chahiye. Aur naye sentence sirf tab aayenge jab tum purane sentence ko zor se bol-bol kar apna banao ge. Ye book usi kaam ke liye hai.

Kis ke liye nahi? Absolute beginners ke liye nahi. Agar tum abhi chhote sentence bhi nahi bana paate, to pehle upar ki kitaabon se buniyad pakki karo, phir is par aao. Ye book unke liye hai jo samajhte hain, thoda bolte hain, aur ab fluent banna chahte hain. Lagbhag ₹237 se shuru hoti hai (MRP ₹450).

6. High School English Grammar & Composition — Wren & Martin (Grammar Companion)

Ye book is list me isliye hai, taaki tum ek taraf ki kahani na suno. Wren & Martin Indian schoolon ki sabse mashhoor grammar book hai — isse saalon se padhaya ja raha hai, aur iska kaam grammar ko ek jagah, saaf tareeke se rakhna hai. Par dhyan rahe: ye bolne ki book nahi hai, ye reference book hai.

Isse kaise istemal karein? Isko pehli book mat banao. Isko side me rakho. Jab bolte waqt koi rule baar-baar galat ho — jaise tense, ya article (a/an/the) — tab isko kholo, wo rule padho, aur phir bolne me usko istemal karo. Grammar ko pehle khatam karne baithe to bolna kabhi shuru hi nahi hoga. Ye book tumhari dictionary jaisi hai: jab zaroorat ho, tab kholo.

Dhyan rakhne wali baat: Is book ko cover-to-cover padhna zaroori nahi hai. Jo log isko pehle number par padhte hain, wo aksar beech me chhod dete hain, kyunki ye dheemi aur bhaari book hai. Isko chhathe number par rakho, jab tumhe bolne ki aadat ho jaaye.

7. Word Power Made Easy — Norman Lewis (1949) (Vocab Companion)

Ab shabd ki book. Lewis ki book 1949 me aayi thi, aur aaj bhi isliye chalti hai kyunki iska tareeka nahi badla: shabd ko root se samjhao, phir usko exercise se apna banao. Agar bolte waqt sahi shabd nahi milta aur tum wahi ghise-pite shabd dohrate ho, to ye book tumhare liye hai. Isme 500 se zyada pages hain, isliye isko ek baar me khatam karne ki koshish mat karo — roz ek session karo.

Kis ke liye best hai? Students ke liye, interview ki taiyaari karne walon ke liye, aur un logon ke liye jo English me bolte waqt atak jaate hain kyunki shabd hi nahi milte. Lagbhag ₹108 se shuru hoti hai (MRP ₹199) — is list ki sabse sasti book.

Ek imaandaar baat: Ye vocabulary ki book hai, speaking course nahi. Isliye isko akele mat uthao — isko upar ki kisi speaking book ke saath rakho. Aur iske kuch shabd absolute beginners ke liye advanced hain, isliye isko pehli book mat banao. Shabd par detail me hamare communication skills guide me bhi likha hai, wahan is book ko aur gehrai se samjhaya gaya hai.

8. English for Everyone: Everyday English — DK (Counterpoint)

Aakhri book is list ki sabse alag hai, kyunki ye un logon ke liye hai jo dekh kar seekhte hain. DK ki book me har unit me dialogue aur exercise hain, saaf visual layout hai, aur 1,500 se zyada roz ke phrases aur expressions hain. English for Everyone app aur website par native speakers ki free audio bhi milti hai — sun kar bolna seekhne walon ke liye ye bahut kaam ki cheez hai.

Par ek baat saaf rahe: ye imported book hai, isliye mehngi hai — lagbhag ₹665 se shuru hoti hai, aur kuch format me ₹1,199 tak jaati hai. Aur isme Hindi support nahi hai, isliye absolute beginners ko ye mushkil lagegi. Ye book is list me isliye hai, taaki tum ek taraf ki kahani na suno: kuch log padh kar seekhte hain, kuch dekh aur sun kar. Agar tum doosre wale ho, to ye book tumhare liye hai — par pehli book ke taur par nahi, doosri ya teesri ke taur par.

Ye Books Abhi Mat Lo — Aur Kyun

Ab teen galtiyan jo har beginner karta hai, aur jinse ye list tumhe bachana chahti hai.

Pehli: pehle din se bhaari grammar ki book. Wren & Martin, ya koi bhi 500-page grammar book, pehli book nahi hai. Grammar se bolna nahi aata, bolne se grammar samajh me aati hai. Pehle chhote sentence bolo, phir jab galti baar-baar ho, tab grammar kholo.

Doosri: pehle din se advanced shabd ki book. Word Power Made Easy achhi book hai, par pehli book nahi. Agar tum abhi "How are you?" ke baad atak jaate ho, to tumhe "magnanimous" jaise shabd nahi, "I am doing well, what about you?" jaise sentence chahiye.

Teesri: "30 din me fluent" ka dawa karne wale paid course aur kitaabein. Fluency ka koi shortcut nahi hai. Jo course ye dawa kare, wo tumhari jaldi ko bech raha hai, tumhari English nahi sudhaar raha. Book lo, roz bolo, aur 4 hafte ka plan follow karo — wahi asli shortcut hai.

4 Hafte Ka Seedha Plan

Hafta 1: British Council LearnEnglish se ek lesson + Rapidex ya Spoken English Guru Book se pehle 5-6 lessons. Maqsad: dar khatam karna, chhote sentence bolna shuru karna. Roz 10 minute zor se bolo — akele me, sheeshe ke saamne, koi baat nahi.

Hafta 2: Sasikumar & Dhamija ki Spoken English se roz ek dialogue. Maqsad: roz-marra ki situation — dukaan, bank, doctor — ke sentence bina soche bolna. Har dialogue do baar zor se bolo: ek baar padh kar, ek baar bina dekhe.

Hafta 3: Speak English Like a Star se wo grammar rule jo baar-baar galat hota hai, aur Word Power Made Easy se roz ek session. Maqsad: apni galti ko pehchanna aur shabd badhana. Is hafte me Atomic Habits ka ek idea kaam aayega — roz ek hi waqt par 15 minute, bina chhode.

Hafta 4: Grant Taylor ki English Conversation Practice se roz ek unit, aur kisi dost ya parivaar wale se English me 5 minute ki baat-cheet. Maqsad: asli baat-cheet. Interview ki taiyaari kar rahe ho to usi hafte interview wale dialogue bhi kar lo.

Is plan ke baad DK ki visual book apne aap sahi jagah par aa jaayegi, kyunki tab tumhe pata hoga ki tum kis level par ho aur tumhe kya chahiye.

Hindi Me English Speaking Books Kaise Padhein — Seedha Rasta

Hindi reader ke liye sabse seedha rasta ye hai: Rapidex ya Spoken English Guru Book se shuru karo, aur classic kitaabon ke Hindi edition tab uthao jab tumhe buniyad aa jaaye. Khareedne se pehle hamesha edition check karo — naam ek jaise hote hain, edition alag-alag hota hai. Aur agar kisi book ka Hindi edition na mile, to usko chhod kar aisi book uthao jo Hindi me saaf mile, bajaye iske ki tum English se ladte raho aur book adhoori chhod do.

To aaj ka kaam bahut chhota hai. Aaj British Council ki LearnEnglish site kholo, aur sirf ek lesson sun lo. Bas. Na koi book khareedni hai, na koi course lena hai. Kal phir wahi karo, agle lesson ke saath. Ek hafte tak ye aadat chalao. Bas itna hi kaafi hai shuruaat ke liye.
