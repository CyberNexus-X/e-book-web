Karmic & Evolutionary Astrology Books: Pluto, Moon's Nodes Aur Past-Life Karma Sikhne Ki Best Kitaabein — Beginner Guide

November 12, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: pehli kitaab Steven Forrest ki The Inner Sky (1984) hai. Karmic angle ke liye uske baad Forrest ki hi Yesterday's Sky: Astrology and Reincarnation (2008) padho, aur nodes ke liye Martin Schulman ki Karmic Astrology, Vol I — The Moon's Nodes and Reincarnation (1975). Gehrai ke liye Jeffrey Wolf Green ki Pluto: The Evolutionary Journey of the Soul (1985) hai — par wo pehli kitaab nahi hai, teesri ya chauthe number ki hai. Teenon alag kaam ki hain, isliye ek hi kitaab se sab kuch nahi milega. Aur dono — karmic aur evolutionary — se pehle ek shart hai: birth chart ki buniyad pakki honi chahiye. Lagna, 12 bhav aur grah samajh lo, phir ye kitaabein uthao. Seedha inse shuru karoge, to Pluto bhi ek daraawna naam lagega aur nodes bhi ek paheli.

Honestly, "karmic" aur "evolutionary" — ye do shabd online aise use hote hain jaise ek hi cheez ho. Hain nahi. Karmic astrology ek broad lens hai: koi bhi astrology jo chart ko past-life karma se jod kar padhe. Evolutionary astrology ek specific school hai — ek naam, ek method, ek itihaas, jo 1970s-80s me America me bana. Is article ka pehla kaam yehi hai: dono ko alag karna. Kyunki jab tak tumhe farak nahi pata, tum galat kitaab uthate rahoge — Green ki Pluto ko beginner kitaab samajh kar, ya Schulman ko evolutionary samajh kar. Dono galtiyan aam hain. Aur dono se bacha ja sakta hai.

Ek baat aur, pehle hi saaf kar doon. Ye article past-life reading nahi deta, aur na hi ye tumhe batayega ki tum pichle janm me kaun the. Ye kitaabon ka guide hai — kaunsi kitaab kab uthani hai, kis order me, aur kin se bachna hai. Reincarnation aur past-life karma ek vishwas hai, ek adhyayan ka framework hai — scientifically proven fact nahi. Steven Forrest khud kehte hain ki unka framework past-life patterns ko symbolic taur par bhi padha ja sakta hai — tumhe punarjanm me yakeen ho ya na ho, chart se pattern samajh aata hai. Isi imandari ke saath aage padho.

Karmic vs Evolutionary — Farak Kya Hai

Karmic astrology wo hai jo chart ko karma aur punarjanm ki kahani se padhti hai — tumhari aaj ki zindagi, pichli zindagiyon ke patterns se judi hui. Isme koi ek method nahi hai. Judy Hall karmic astrology karti thin, Martin Schulman karte the, Stephen Arroyo karte hain, Alan Oken karte hain — sabka tareeka alag hai, par sawaal ek hai: is chart me purani kahani kya hai, aur is janm me uska matlab kya hai?

Evolutionary astrology isse zyada specific hai. Ye ek school hai, jiska naam Raymond Merriman ne apni 1977 ki limited-edition kitaab Evolutionary Astrology me pehli baar diya tha, aur jise Jeffrey Wolf Green ne apni 1985 ki kitaab Pluto: The Evolutionary Journey of the Soul se mashhoor kiya. Steven Forrest ne ye term apni 1984 ki kitaab The Inner Sky me use kiya. Is school ka sawaal seedha hai: soul is janm me kya seekhne aayi hai? Aur iska jawab do cheezon se nikala jata hai — Pluto (soul ka evolutionary intent) aur Moon's Nodes (South Node = peeche chhoota pattern, North Node = is janm ki disha).

To farak ye hai: har evolutionary astrology karmic hai, par har karmic astrology evolutionary nahi. Schulman karmic hain, evolutionary nahi. Judy Hall karmic hain, evolutionary nahi. Green aur Forrest dono hain — aur dono ke beech bhi farak hai, jo aage aayega. Ye farak samajhna zaroori hai, kyunki isi se tumhari kitaabon ka order banta hai: pehle broad karmic lens (Forrest, Schulman, Hall), phir specific evolutionary school (Green).

Aur ek farak jo beginner ko sabse zyada kaam aata hai — Forrest aur Green ke beech ka. Forrest ka system zyada psychological-developmental hai: past-life patterns asli hain, par unhe symbolic taur par bhi padha ja sakta hai — framework tab bhi kaam karta hai, jab tum punarjanm me literally yakeen na karo. Green ka system zyada metaphysically committed hai: literal past lives, Hindu sense me dharma aur karma. Dono sahi hain, apni jagah. Par tumhe chunna hai ki tum kis taraf se shuru karna chahte ho — Forrest ki taraf se, jo aasan hai, ya Green ki taraf se, jo gehri hai. Is article ki salah: Forrest se shuru karo.

Framework: 5 Signals — Kitaab Se Pehle Ye Samjho

Kitaab uthane se pehle paanch cheezein samajh lo. Ye paanchon is article ki har kitaab me baar-baar aayengi.

Pehla: Pluto. Evolutionary astrology me Pluto sabse important grah hai — iski rashi aur bhav batate hain ki soul is janm me kis cheez ko transform karne aayi hai. Pluto jis bhav me hai — astrology houses me har bhav zindagi ka ek area hota hai — us area me zindagi ki sabse gehri seekh hoti hai. Par ek baat yaad rakho: Pluto dar ka grah nahi hai. Bahut se log Pluto dekh kar ghabra jate hain — "Pluto 8th house me hai, to khatra hai." Aisa nahi hai. Forrest ke hisaab se Pluto batata hai ki kahan purana pattern chhoot raha hai aur kahan naya ban raha hai. Dar nahi, kaam hai.

Doosra: South Node. Ye tumhara peeche chhoota pattern hai — wo aadat, wo talent, wo darr, jo tum pichli zindagiyon se lekar aaye ho. South Node tumhari taakat bhi hai aur tumhari jaal bhi: jo cheez tumhe aasani se aati hai, wahi cheez tumhe aage badhne se rok bhi sakti hai. Schulman kehte hain ki South Node tumhara "Achilles Heel" hai — tumhare peeche chhoote kadmon ka nishan. Ise samajhna, karmic astrology ka pehla kadam hai.

Teesra: North Node. Ye is janm ki disha hai — wo naya experience, jo tumne abhi tak try nahi kiya. North Node hamesha South Node ke bilkul opposite hota hai — opposite rashi, opposite bhav. Matlab, tumhari growth hamesha tumhari comfort zone ke opposite hai. Ye baat samajh lo, to North Node ka matlab samajh aayega: ye "achha" nahi hai aur South Node "bura" nahi hai — dono ek hi kahani ke do hisse hain.

Chauthe: Saturn (Shani). Saturn ko zodiac ka teacher kehte hain — ye karma aur discipline ka grah hai. Saturn jis bhav me hai, wahan tumhe mehnat karni padti hai, aur wahi tumhari sabse badi seekh hoti hai. Karmic astrology me Saturn wo jagah batata hai jahan tumhe "kaam karna hai" — na ki jahan tumhe "saza mil rahi hai". Ye farak samajhna zaroori hai, kyunki bahut se log Saturn ko saza samajh lete hain.

Paanchva: Retrograde grah. Jab koi grah retrograde (vakri) hota hai, to uska matlab hota hai: is area me dobara dekhna, review karna, aur peeche chhooti hui baat ko poora karna. Schulman ki Vol II poori isi par hai — retrograde grah karmic review ka sign hain. Retrograde ka matlab "kharab" nahi hai — matlab hai "dobara".

Vedic bridge — Jyotish se kaise jode. Ab ye paanchon signals tumhe Vedic Jyotish me bhi milenge, bas naam alag hain. Moon's Nodes hi Rahu-Ketu hain — wahi axis, wahi kahani: Ketu = South Node (peeche chhoota pattern), Rahu = North Node (is janm ki disha). Saturn hi Shani hai — karma karaka. Aur bhavon me: 5th bhav poorva punya (past-life merit), 9th bhav dharma, 12th bhav moksha aur unseen cheezon ka hai. Matlab, Western kitaab padhne se pehle tumhe apna birth chart samajhna zaroori hai — iske liye hamara Birth chart reading guide padh lo, jisme Lagna, 12 bhav aur dasha detail me hain. Uske bina Pluto aur nodes ki kitaab uthana, bina map ke sheher me ghumne jaisa hai.

Level 1: Pehli Kitaabein — Forrest, Schulman, Judy Hall

Level 1 ki chaar kitaabein hain — yehi woh karmic astrology books hain jinse ek beginner ko shuru karna chahiye. Inhe is order me padho.

1. The Inner Sky — Steven Forrest (1984). Ye pehli kitaab hai, aur isiliye kyunki ye sabse aasan entry hai. Forrest ne ye kitaab Bantam Books ke liye likhi thi — ek aam reader ke liye. Isme rashi, bhav, grah aur aspects ki buniyad hai, par evolutionary bhasha me: har grah ek seekh hai, har bhav ek area hai jahan soul kaam kar rahi hai. Jo log pehli baar astrology padh rahe hain, unke liye bhi ye kitaab hai — aur jo basic Jyotish jaante hain, unke liye ye Western bhasha ka best bridge hai. Is kitaab se Forrest ki awaaz samajh aayegi — seedhi, psychological, aur free will par zor dene wali. Ye kitaab khatam karo, phir aage badho.

2. Yesterday's Sky: Astrology and Reincarnation — Steven Forrest (2008). Ye doosri kitaab hai, aur karmic angle ki seedhi kitaab hai. Forrest ne is par das saal research kiya tha, aur ye unki past-life astrology par sabse comprehensive kitaab hai — isme specific karmic themes ko pehchanne ke tareeke hain, aur unke current-life manifestations ko samajhne ke tareeke hain. The Inner Sky ne tumhe bhasha sikhai thi; Yesterday's Sky usi bhasha me past-life ki kahani padhna sikhati hai. Agar tumhe sirf ek karmic kitaab padhni ho, to ye wahi hai.

3. Karmic Astrology, Vol I — The Moon's Nodes and Reincarnation — Martin Schulman (1975). Ye teesri kitaab hai, aur nodes ki sabse seedhi kitaab hai. Schulman ne pehle reincarnation aur karma par intro chapters likhe, phir nodes ko rashi aur bhav ke hisaab se poora delineate kiya — har combination ka matlab, nodes par aspects ka chapter, sample delineations, aur 1850 se 2000 tak nodal positions ka appendix. Ye kitaab sirf 133 pages ki hai — chhoti, par ghani. Iska fayda ye hai: isse tum apne South Node aur North Node ko rashi aur bhav se seedha padh sakte ho, bina kisi aur kitaab ke. Is series ke baaki volumes — Vol II (Retrogrades and Reincarnation), Vol III (Joy and the Part of Fortune), Vol IV (The Karma of the Now) — Level 2 me aate hain.

4. Past Life Astrology: Use Your Birthchart to Understand Your Karma — Judy Hall. Chauthe number par Judy Hall ki kitaab hai — aur iski khaasiyat ye hai ki ye sabse compassionate hai. Hall past-life therapy bhi karti thin, aur unki kitaab case histories se bhari hai — un logon ki kahani, jinki karmic history wo apne past-life kaam se jaanti thin, aur famous charts bhi (Kennedys, Marilyn Monroe, Lenin, Martin Luther King). Unki doosri kitaab The Karmic Journey: The Birthchart, Karma and Reincarnation (Penguin, 1991, 368 pages) bhi yahi kaam karti hai — famous case histories se karmic patterns ko dikhana. Hall ki kitaab tab padho jab tumhe nodes aur Pluto ki technique samajh aa gayi ho — ye kitaab technique nahi, insaan ki kahani sikhati hai. Aur unki The Astrology Bible ek reference kitaab hai, pehli kitaab nahi.

Level 2: Gehrai — Green, The Book of Pluto, Schulman Vol II–IV

Jab Level 1 ki chaar kitaabein haath me aa jayein, tab gehrai shuru hoti hai. Inhe bhi order me hi padhna.

5. Pluto: The Evolutionary Journey of the Soul, Volume 1 — Jeffrey Wolf Green (1985). Ye evolutionary astrology ki founding text hai — is school ki pehli aur sabse zaroori kitaab. Green isme dikhate hain ki Pluto ki position se soul ke evolutionary aur karmic lessons kaise samjhe jayein, aur past lives ko Pluto ke through kaise padha jaye. Par seedhi baat: ye kitaab bhaari hai. Ye pehli kitaab nahi hai — ye tab padho jab Forrest ki do kitaabein khatam ho chuki hon. Agar Green ka method pehle se hi aasan bhasha me samajhna ho, to Ari Moshe ki Evolutionary Astrology: A Beginner's Guide pehle padh lo — ye Green ke paradigm ki beginner entry hai, aur iske baad Pluto Vol 1 aasan lagegi.

6. Pluto, Volume II: The Soul's Evolution Through Relationships — Jeffrey Wolf Green (1997). Doosra volume relationships par hai — do logon ki evolutionary aur karmic progression, past-life dynamics ke saath. Isme Venus aur Mars ka kaam, relationship types aur needs, aur composite chart me Pluto ka role samjhaya gaya hai. Ye kitaab tab uthao jab Vol 1 khatam ho — aur tab, jab tumhe relationships ko karmic lens se dekhna ho. Agar relationships tumhara vishay nahi hai, to ise baad ke liye chhod sakte ho.

7. The Book of Pluto: Finding Wisdom in Darkness with Astrology — Steven Forrest (1995). Ye Forrest ki Pluto par kitaab hai — Green ki Pluto se alag, aur isiliye dono padhni chahiye. Green Pluto ko soul ke evolutionary intent ki tarah padhte hain; Forrest Pluto ko darkness me wisdom dhoondhne ki tarah — shadow, transformation, aur woh jagah jahan purana pattern chhoot raha hai. Dono ek hi grah ko do taraf se dekhte hain. Forrest ki kitaab Green se halki hai, isliye ise Green se pehle bhi padh sakte ho — par dono ko padhne ke baad hi Pluto poora samajh aayega.

8. Measuring the Night: Evolutionary Astrology and the Keys to the Soul, Vols 1–2 — Steven Forrest aur Jeffrey Wolf Green (2000/2001). Ye kitaab dono ki lectures par based hai — Forrest aur Green ne ek saath lectures diye, aur unhi se ye do volumes bane. Iska fayda ye hai: tum ek hi jagah par dono systems ka farak dekh sakte ho — Forrest ka psychological tareeka, Green ka metaphysical tareeka — aur samajh sakte ho ki dono kahan milte hain aur kahan alag hote hain. Forrest aur Green ko "The Pluto Brothers" bhi kaha jata hai, aur ye kitaab unki dosti aur unke farak — dono — ki gawah hai. Ye Level 2 ke ant me padho, pehle nahi.

Schulman Vol II–IV aur Arroyo, Oken

Schulman Vol II — Retrogrades and Reincarnation. Retrograde grahon par ek radical, unorthodox interpretation — traditional "good/bad" wali baatein chhod kar, ek esoteric tareeka. Ye kitaab unke liye hai jo nodes samajh chuke hain aur ab retrograde grahon ko karmic review ki tarah padhna chahte hain. Vol III — Joy and the Part of Fortune aur Vol IV — The Karma of the Now series ko poora karte hain — Vol IV khaas taur par vartaman par hai: karma sirf pichle janm ki kahani nahi, abhi ka kaam bhi hai.

Astrology, Karma and Transformation — Stephen Arroyo. Ye kitaab kai lists me "wide-ranging" aur "highly recommended" kahi jati hai — aur wajah saaf hai: ye karma aur transformation ko psychological depth ke saath jodti hai. Arroyo ki kitaab unke liye hai jo Forrest se aage badh kar, karma ko psychology se jodna chahte hain. Alan Oken ki Soul-Centered Astrology aur Karmic Astrology bhi isi shelf ki kitaabein hain — soul-centred approach, jo karmic astrology ko spiritual framework se jodta hai. Dono ko Level 2 ke baad uthao — pehle Forrest aur Schulman, phir ye.

Level 3: Gehrai — Fernandez, Mark Jones, Deva Green

Level 3 unke liye hai jo ab is vishay me serious hain — aur jinhe pata hai ki ek kitaab se kaam nahi chalega.

Astrology and the Evolution of Consciousness, Volume 1 — Maurice Fernandez (2009). Fernandez Green ke sabse zyada published student hain, aur unki kitaab Green ke method ko aage badhati hai — consciousness ke evolution ko chart se kaise padha jaye. Ye kitaab tab padho jab Green ki Pluto Vol 1 khatam ho — ye usi method ki gehrai hai, naya method nahi.

Healing the Soul: Pluto, Uranus and the Lunar Nodes — Mark Jones (2011). Jones ki kitaab is branch ki sabse zyada cited text hai — isme Pluto ke saath Uranus aur lunar nodes ko healing ki lens se padha gaya hai. Ye kitaab unke liye hai jo karmic astrology ko counselling aur healing se jodna chahte hain — par ek baat yaad rakho: ye kitaab padhna, counsellor banna nahi hai. Chart se healing ki baat karna ek baat hai, kisi ki therapy karna doosri — aur doosri wali galti kabhi mat karna.

Evolutionary Astrology: Pluto and Your Karmic Mission — Deva Green. Green ki beti Deva Green ne apne pita ki teachings ko compile kiya hai — ye kitaab Green ke method ka ek aur raasta hai, unke apne shabdon me. Patricia Walsh ki Understanding Karmic Complexes: EA and Regression Therapy bhi yahin aati hai — Walsh Green ke School of Evolutionary Astrology ki graduate hain aur professional past-life regressionist bhi — unki kitaab evolutionary astrology aur regression therapy ke beech ka bridge hai. Regression ek serious kaam hai; is kitaab ko padhna, regression karna nahi hai.

Aur do extra options, jo shelf par rakhne layak hain: Isabel M. Hickey ki Astrology, A Cosmic Science — ek beginner's guide, par karma aur spiritualism ki taraf jhuka hua — aur Lisa Wagner ki Karmic Astrology — 30 saal se zyada ke experience wali astrologer ki comprehensive guide, jisme numerology aur past-life experiences bhi hain. Dono achhi hain, par dono "extra" hain — pehle upar wali kitaabein, phir ye.

Vedic Side: Jyotish Me Karma Kaise Padha Jata Hai

Ab wo sawaal jo har Hindi reader ke dimaag me hai: ye sab Western kitaabein to theek hain, par Vedic Jyotish me karma kaise padha jata hai? Jawab seedha hai — upar wale framework me jo paanch signals the, wo Jyotish me bhi hain, bas naam alag hain.

Jyotish me karma Rahu-Ketu se padha jata hai — wahi lunar nodes, wahi axis: Ketu peeche chhoota pattern hai, Rahu is janm ki disha hai. Shani karma karaka hai — jis bhav me Shani hai, wahan mehnat aur seekh hai. Aur bhavon me: 5th bhav poorva punya hai (past-life merit), 9th bhav dharma hai, 12th bhav moksha aur unseen cheezon ka hai. Ye framework hamare Birth chart guide me detail me hai — usi ko dobara padho, phir Western kitaabein samajh aayengi.

Par ek imandari wali baat yahan bhi. Western karmic/evolutionary astrology ka ek canon hai — Forrest, Green, Schulman, Hall — ek list, ek order. Vedic Jyotish me aisa koi single canonical "karmic astrology" book nahi hai, jo English ya Hindi me aise hi list ho. Karma Jyotish me har jagah hai — har classic text me, har bhav me — par ek kitaab nahi hai jise "Vedic karmic astrology ki pehli kitaab" kaha ja sake. Isliye is article me Vedic side ke liye koi nakli list nahi banayi gayi hai. Sahi raasta ye hai: pehle hamara Astrology books guide aur Jyotish learning-order article padho, phir KP aur Nadi guide — kyunki KP aur Nadi bhi predictive systems hain, aur unki kitaabon me bhi karma aur past-life ka angle aata hai. Western kitaabein tumhe framework dengi; Vedic buniyad tumhe apni kundli se jodegi.

Abhi Mat Uthao — Pirated PDF, Guaranteed Regression Aur Ek Placement Ka Jaal

Ek cheez jo har beginner karta hai: Google par "karmic astrology books pdf" ya "evolutionary astrology books pdf" search karna. Free PDF mil bhi jaye, to uske teen nuksan hain. Pehla, wo aam taur par pirated hoti hai — lekhak ki mehnat ki chori. Doosra, wo purani ya adhoori hoti hai — beech ke pages gayab, ya scan itna kharab ki nodes ka chart padha hi nahi jata. Teesra, aisi sites par malware bhi milta hai. Free me padhna hai, to sahi raasta hai: astrolium.com ka free Evolutionary Astrology guide, School of Evolutionary Astrology ki apni books page, aur hamara birth-chart guide — teeno free, legal, aur current hain. Aur agar koi kitaab genuinely out of copyright hai, to archive.org par uska legal scan mil sakta hai — pirated PDF sites se behtar.

Doosri cheez jo abhi mat karo: kisi "past-life regression guaranteed" centre ko advance dena. Regression ek serious kaam hai — aur koi bhi centre jo "pichla janm pakka dikhayenge" keh kar pehle bade paise maange, wo centre nahi, dukaan hai. Koi kitaab, koi regression, koi reading past life ki guarantee nahi deti — ye baat tum ab jaante ho. Pehle kitaab se framework seekho, phir — agar karna hi ho — to ek trained, reputed practitioner ko chunno, bina guarantee wale vaade ke.

Teesri cheez: ek placement se poori kahani banana. "Pluto 8th house me hai, to ye vyakti aisa hai" — aisa nahi hai. Ek placement ek signal hai; kahani paanch signals, bhav, rashi, aspects aur dasha — sab mil kar banti hai. Jo vyakti ek placement se faisla deta hai, wo karmic astrology nahi kar raha — wo andaza laga raha hai. Aur chauthe: Pluto se darna. Pluto transformation ka grah hai, saza ka nahi. Pluto ko dekh kar darna, kitaab ko galat padhna hai.

Aur agar tumhe best books to learn astrology ki poori list chahiye — Jyotish ki kitaabon ki buniyad — to hamara Astrology books guide agla achha kadam hai, aur kitaabon ko PDF me padhne ke baare me astrology books PDF guide bhi dekh sakte ho.

Ye 5 Galtiyan Mat Karo

Ab wo galtiyan, jo karmic aur evolutionary astrology me sabse zyada hoti hain. Inse bach gaye, to aadha kaam ho gaya.

Galti 1: Ek placement se poora faisla. "South Node Scorpio me hai, to ye vyakti aisa hai" — aisa nahi hai. Ek placement ek signal hai. Kahani Pluto, nodes, Saturn, retrograde, bhav, rashi, aspects aur dasha — sab mil kar banti hai. Ek rule se jawab dena karmic astrology nahi, andaza hai.

Galti 2: North Node ko "good" aur South Node ko "bad" samajhna. Ye sabse aam galti hai. South Node tumhara peeche chhoota pattern hai — usme tumhari taakat bhi hai, tumhara talent bhi. North Node tumhari disha hai — par wo mushkil bhi hai, naya bhi hai. Dono ko achha-bura me baantna, poori kahani ko aadha karna hai.

Galti 3: Regression centre ko pehle advance dena. "Past life guaranteed" kehne wale ko paise dene se pehle ye yaad rakho: koi guarantee nahi hoti. Pehle kitaab se seekho, practitioner baad me — aur tab bhi bina process samjhe advance mat do.

Galti 4: Kitaab ko bhavishyavani samajhna. Karmic aur evolutionary astrology ek adhyayan hai — pattern samajhne ka framework. Isse kisi ki zindagi ka faisla lena — shaadi, naukri, ilaaj — galat hai. Chart se pattern samjho, par faisla insaan ki zindagi aur uski marzi se lo.

Galti 5: Kitaab khareed kar padhna nahi. Ye sabse aam galti hai — Forrest khareed lo, Green khareed lo, Schulman khareed lo, aur shelf par saja do. Kitaab khareedna seekhna nahi hai. Har kitaab ke saath ek kundli uthao — apni ya kisi jaani-pehchani — us par Pluto aur nodes lagao, aur galtiyan note karo. Jo practice nahi karta, wo karmic astrology — kuch bhi nahi seekhta.

4 Hafte Ka Seedha Plan

Hafta 1: Framework pakka karo. Pluto, South Node, North Node, Saturn aur retrograde ko ek kagaz par likh lo — har ek ka matlab, ek line me. Hamara Birth chart guide dobara padho — Lagna, bhav aur dasha revise karo. Saath me astrolium.com ka free Evolutionary Astrology guide aur School of Evolutionary Astrology ki books page padhna shuru karo. Maqsad: kitaab kholne se pehle bhasha samajhna.

Hafta 2: Forrest ki The Inner Sky shuru karo — pehle chapters, buniyad. Saath me Schulman ki Vol I se apne South Node aur North Node ko rashi aur bhav se padho. Maqsad: dono kitaabon ki buniyad haath se karna.

Hafta 3: Yesterday's Sky — past-life angle. Saath me apne chart par Pluto aur nodes ki practice — har din ek placement, ek kundli. Paper par ek chhoti si diary banao: kaunsa signal dekha, kya pattern nikla, kya sahi laga. Maqsad: signals ko haath se lagana, sirf padhna nahi.

Hafta 4: Ab ek school chuno — Forrest ya Green. Ek ko chuno, dono ko nahi. Forrest chuno to The Book of Pluto ki taraf badho; Green chuno to Ari Moshe ki Beginner's Guide se Pluto Vol 1 ki taraf. Aur agar Hindi me padhna ho to hamare Astrology books guide se Vedic buniyad aur pakki karo. Maqsad: ek raasta chunna aur usi par chalna.

Is plan ke baad tumhe khud pata chal jayega ki tumhe aage kya chahiye — Green ki Pluto, ya Schulman ki Vol II, ya Judy Hall ki case histories — aur tabhi aage ki kitaab chunna. Aur agar tumhe predictive systems me aur gehrai chahiye, to hamara KP aur Nadi books guide bhi dekh sakte ho — wahan bhi karma aur timing ka wahi sawaal, doosre tareeke se, aata hai.
