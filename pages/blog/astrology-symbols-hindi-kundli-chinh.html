Astrology Symbols: Kundli ke Chinh, Glyphs Aur Unka Matlab Samjhe

November 15, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: kundli aur birth chart me dikhne wale chinh (symbols/glyphs) grahon, rashiyon aur unke beech ke rishton ki shorthand hain — jaise ♈ Mesha hai, ☉ Surya hai, aur ♃ Guru hai. Inhe isiliye banaya gaya tha taaki poora aasmaan ek page par aa jaye: har grah ka naam likhne ki jagah ek chhota sa chinh kaafi ho. Chaar groups hain — grah chinh, rashi chinh, aspect chinh, aur chhote nishan (jaise vakri ℞ aur Lagna). Bas ye chaar groups yaad kar lo, to koi bhi chart tumhe hieroglyphics jaisa nahi lagega.

Meri pehli baar kisi chart ko dekhne ki yaad abhi bhi taza hai. Ek gol chakkar, usme chhote-chhote ajeeb chinh, beech me rang-birangi lakeerein. Kuch samajh nahi aaya — bas ye laga ki ye kisi secret code jaisa hai. Aur honestly, yehi wo jagah hai jahan zyada tar beginners atak jate hain. Rashiyan seekh li, bhav samajh liye — par jab chart kholo, to wo ♃ aur ♄ ek jaise lagte hain, ♍ aur ♏ me farak nahi dikhta, aur beech wali lakeerein kya keh rahi hain, ye koi nahi batata. Is article ka kaam yehi hai — wo secret code kholna. Ek-ek chinh, uski picture, uska matlab, aur apne chart par use pehchanne ka tareeka.

Ek imandari wali baat pehle hi saaf kar doon. Astrology ek sanskritik parampara aur vishwas ka framework hai — studies me is baat ka koi scientific saboot nahi mila ki chinh, grah ya unki position personality ya events predict karte hain. Is article me jo matlab likhe hain, wo parampara ke hisaab se hain — ek starting sketch, khud ko samajhne ke liye. Inhe apni pehchan ka rulebook mat banao, aur inke basis par shaadi, naukri, paisa ya sehat jaise faisle kabhi mat lo. Theek hai? Ab shuru karte hain.

Chinh Kya Hote Hain — Chart Ki Legend

Ek map socho. Map par sheher ke naam likhe hote hain, par sath me chhote-chhote chinh bhi hote hain — ek triangle pahad dikhata hai, ek neeli lakeer nadi, ek chhota sa hawai jahaz airport. Bina legend ke wo chinh bekaar hain; legend ke sath wo hi map bolne lagta hai. Astrology ke chinh wahi legend hain. Chart ek map hai — janm ke samay aasmaan ka map — aur chinh us map ki legend.

Ye chinh kab se hain? Pehle jaane gaye grah-chinh Byzantine manuscripts me lagbhag 9th century CE me milte hain, aur 15th century ke woodcuts — jaise Johannes Engel ke Astrolabium (1488) — me ye aaj ke roop ke bahut kareeb dikhte hain. Matlab ye chinh koi nayi invention nahi hain; inhe banne aur settle hone me sau saal lage hain. Pluto ka chinh to 1930 me uski khoj ke baad banaya gaya — P aur L, Percival Lowell ke naam ke initials, us astronomer ke, jisne uski khoj ki prediction ki thi.

Aur ek baat jo in chinhon ko yaad rakhna aasan bana deti hai: ye random doodles nahi hain. Almost har grah ka chinh sirf teen basic shapes se banta hai. Ek baar ye teen shapes samajh lo, to har chinh khud hi khulne lagta hai.

ShapeKya dikhata haiKahan dikhta hai
Circle (○)Spirit — aatma, anant, poornataSurya ☉ poora circle hai; Venus ♀ me circle upar hai
Crescent (☾)Soul — mann, grahan-sheelta, receptivityChandra ☽ poora crescent hai; Mercury ☿ me crescent sabse upar hai

Ye teen shapes ka order hi poori kahani hai. Mercury ☿ me crescent sabse upar hai, phir circle, phir cross — mann, aatma se upar, matter se upar. Sochne wale grah ke liye perfect picture hai, hai na? Venus ♀ me circle cross ke upar hai — spirit, matter se upar — pyaar aur values ka grah. Mars ♂ me circle se teer bahar nikal raha hai — energy, bahar ki taraf. Ye koi coincidence nahi hai. Jin logo ne ye chinh banaye, unhone soch kar banaye the.

Ek chhoti si baat aur: rashi chinh alag tareeke se bante hain. Wo pictograms hain — matlab wo cheez ki tasveer hain jise wo dikhate hain. Mesha ka chinh ♈ medhe ke seeng hain. Vrishabha ♉ bail ka sir aur seeng. Mithuna ♊ Roman numeral II hai — judwa. Samajh gaye? Grah chinh philosophy se bante hain, rashi chinh tasveer se. Dono ko alag-alag yaad rakho, to confusion aadha ho jayega.

9 Grah Chinh — Vedic Chart Ke 9 Grah

Vedic astrology me 9 grah hote hain — Surya, Chandra, Mangal, Budha, Guru, Shukra, Shani, aur do chhaya grah, Rahu aur Ketu. Yehi 9 chinh hain jo tumhe sabse zyada dikhenge. Western astrology me inke alawa Uranus ♅, Neptune ♆ aur Pluto ♇ bhi use hote hain — par Vedic chart me wo nahi aate. Isiliye is table me pehle 9 hi hain; baaki teen ka zikr aage ek line me hai.

Vedic astrology me inhe chhaya grah kehte hain. Kundli me ye hamesha ek doosre ke bilkul saamne hote hain — 180 degree par. Agar tumhare chart me Rahu 1st bhav me hai, to Ketu pakka 7th me hoga. Ye counting ka free point hai.

Doosri baat: Uranus, Neptune aur Pluto ka chinh bhi hota hai — ♅, ♆ (trishul), ♇ — par ye sirf Western charts me use hote hain. Agar tum Vedic kundli padh rahe ho, to inhe dekh kar confuse mat ho. Tumhare chart me ye nahi honge, aur hone bhi nahi chahiye. Kuch apps dono systems ko mix karke dikhate hain — tab ye chinh dikh sakte hain. Bas ye yaad rakho ki Vedic reading me inki koi jagah nahi hai.

Farak sirf aakhri stroke me hai: Kanya ka stroke andar band hota hai, Vrishchika ka bahar teer ki tarah nikalta hai. Bichhu ki poonchh — yaad rakho, bichhu ka dank bahar ki taraf hota hai. Shukra aur Mangal me bhi simple farak hai: Shukra me circle upar hai aur cross neeche (sheesha), Mangal me circle se teer bahar ja raha hai. Ek baar ye picture dimag me baith jaye, to dobara galti nahi hogi.

Ek aur baat, jo internet par baar-baar poochhi jati hai: Ophiuchus, "13th sign", kya hai? Seedha jawab — paramparik zodiac me iski koi jagah nahi hai. Ophiuchus ek nakshatra hai jo Vrishchika aur Dhanu ke beech aata hai, par 12-rashi system me use kabhi include nahi kiya gaya. Tumhare chart me 12 hi rashiyan hain, 13 nahi. Koi app ya website 13th sign dikhaye, to use ignore karo — wo purane system ko badal nahi raha, sirf confusion badha raha hai.

Aspect Chinh — Grahon Ke Beech Ki Lakeerein

Chart ke beech me rang-birangi lakeerein dikhti hain na? Wo aspects hain — do grahon ke beech ka angle. Har aspect ka apna chinh hota hai. Western astrology me ye angles degree se nape jate hain — 0°, 60°, 90°, 120°, 180°. Har angle ka apna matlab mana jata hai.

△Trine120°Flow aur aasani — natural support

Ab yahan ek important farak samajh lo, kyunki ye dono systems ko mix karne ki sabse badi jagah hai. Vedic astrology me aspects ko drishti kehte hain, aur wo angle se nahi, bhav se gine jate hain. Har grah ki apni drishti hoti hai: Guru 5th, 7th aur 9th bhav ko dekhta hai; Shani 3rd, 7th aur 10th ko; Mangal 4th, 7th aur 8th ko. Ye Western angles se alag system hai. Isiliye jab tum kisi Western chart app me ☌ ya △ dekho, to use Vedic drishti se mix mat karo. Dono alag tareeke hain, dono ki apni logic hai. Beginner ke liye sirf itna yaad rakho: lakeerein batati hain ki do grah ek doosre ko dekh rahe hain ya nahi — aur wo dekhna support hai ya tension. Detail me jaana ho, to hamare Birth chart reading guide me drishti ka section hai.

Ek chhota sa note: sextile ka chinh ✶ kabhi-kabhi alag fonts me alag dikhta hai — 6-pointed star ya asterisk jaisa. Agar tumhare app me ye thoda alag dikhe, to ghabrana nahi. Chinh ka matlab wahi rehta hai.

Chhote Chinh — Lagna, Vakri, Degrees Aur Nodes

Bade chinh to yaad ho gaye. Ab wo chhote nishan, jo chart ke kone-kone me chhupe hote hain aur jinhe dekh kar beginners sabse zyada confuse hote hain.

Lagna / Ascendant — Asc ya ↑. Ye tumhare chart ka starting point hai — janm ke samay poorab me jo rashi ug rahi thi. Western wheel chart me ye left side par "Asc" likha hota hai, ya ek upar ki taraf teer. Vedic kundli me Lagna wali rashi pehla bhav hoti hai — hamare Houses guide me iski poori counting hai. Lagna ke bina chart adhura hai — isiliye janm ka exact samay itna important hai.

Vakri — ℞ ya R. Kisi grah ke chinh ke paas ℞ dikhe, to matlab hai wo grah vakri (retrograde) tha — dharti se dekhne par wo peeche ki taraf chalta hua lag raha tha. Ye asliyat me peeche chalna nahi hai, sirf ek optical effect hai — jaise train me baith kar paas wali train peeche jati hui lagti hai. Parampara me vakri grah ko "andar ki taraf" ya "dobara sochne wala" mana jata hai. Ye hamesha bura nahi hota. Kabhi-kabhi iska matlab sirf ye hai ki us area me tum cheezon ko gehrai se, doosre angle se dekhte ho. Isse darne ki koi baat nahi hai — aur na hi isse koi badi prediction karni chahiye.

Degrees — °. Har grah ke saath ek number dikhta hai, jaise 14° ya 27°. Ye batata hai ki grah us rashi ke andar kitna aage hai — rashi 0° se 30° tak hoti hai. 0° ke kareeb ho, to grah rashi me abhi-abhi aaya hai; 29° ke kareeb ho, to rashi badalne wala hai. Beginner ke liye degree ka exact matlab yaad rakhna zaroori nahi — bas itna samjho ki ye grah ki "position within the sign" hai.

Rahu-Ketu nodes — ☊ aur ☋. Ye dono ek jaise dikhte hain, bas ek ulta hai. ☊ Rahu (North Node) hai, ☋ Ketu (South Node). Inhe aksar chart me alag se dikhaya jata hai kyunki ye grah nahi, points hain. Aur ek baat — ye hamesha ek doosre ke saamne hote hain. Ek ko dekh lo, to doosra apne aap pata chal jata hai.

Tatva chinh. Kuch charts me chaar tatvon ke alag chinh bhi dikhte hain — Agni 🜂, Prithvi 🜃, Vayu 🜁, Jal 🜄. Ye alchemical symbols hain, aur har chart me nahi dikhte. Agar dikhen, to bas itna samjho: ye batate hain ki rashi kis tatva ki hai. Hamare zodiac guide me har rashi ka tatva pehle se hai.

North Indian Kundli vs Western Wheel — Sabse Bada Farak

Ab wo section, jo is guide ko sirf ek translated English list se alag karta hai. Agar tumne kabhi apni kundli banayi hai — AstroSage ya kisi aur free tool se — to tumne dekha hoga ki wo gol wheel nahi hoti. Wo ek heere (diamond) jaisi aakriti hoti hai, jisme 12 dibbe bane hote hain. Ye North Indian style hai. Aur agar tumne kisi Western app me chart banaya, to wo gol wheel hoti hai, jisme glyphs bhare hote hain. Dono ek hi aasmaan ko dikhate hain — bas tareeka alag hai.

Ye farak kyun important hai? Kyunki agar tum sirf glyphs seekh lo aur phir apni North Indian kundli kholo, to tumhe wahan ek bhi ♈ ya ☉ nahi dikhega — wahan Su, Mo, Ma likha hoga, ya Devanagari akshar. Aur tum sochoge ki tumne kuch galat seekha. Nahi — tumne sahi seekha, bas doosre system ka. Glyphs seekhna isiliye kaam ka hai kyunki bahut saari apps aur websites — aur bahut saari English kitaabein — glyphs use karti hain. Dono notations aani chahiye: Vedic kundli ke akshar, aur Western chart ke glyphs. Ek ko jaan lo, to doosra translate karna aasan hai.

Apne Chart Par Chinh Padhna — 5 Steps, Worked Example Ke Saath

Ab practical baat. Chinh yaad kar liye — ab unhe apne chart par kaise padhe? Paanch steps hain. Inhe yaad kar lo, to tum kisi bhi chart ko khol kar kam se kam ye bata sakte ho: kaun kahan baitha hai.

Step 1: Lagna dhoondho. Western wheel me "Asc" left side par hota hai; North Indian kundli me upar wale bade dibbe me jo rashi hai, wahi Lagna hai. Ye tumhara starting point hai — iske bina baaki sab bekaar hai.

Step 2: Har bhav me kaunsi rashi hai, wo gino. Lagna wali rashi pehla bhav, uske baad ki doosra, aur aise hi 12 tak. Hamare houses guide me ye counting detail me hai.

Step 3: Har grah ka chinh dhoondho. ☉ kahan hai? ☽ kahan hai? Har grah ko uski rashi aur uske bhav ke sath note karo. Ek simple sentence banao: "Surya Mesha me, pehle bhav me." Bas. Isi ko teen cheezon ka combo kehte hain — grah + rashi + bhav.

Step 4: Chhote nishan check karo. Koi grah vakri ℞ to nahi hai? Rahu-Ketu kahan hain? Degrees kya hain? Ye chhote nishan badi picture badal dete hain.

Step 5: Lakeerein dekho — kaun kisko dekh raha hai. Koi grah kisi doosre grah ko aspect kar raha hai? Vedic me drishti check karo — Guru 5th/7th/9th, Shani 3rd/7th/10th, Mangal 4th/7th/8th. Ye batata hai ki grah sirf apne bhav me nahi, doosre bhavon ko bhi affect kar raha hai.

Ab ek worked example — sirf counting dikhane ke liye, prediction nahi. Maan lo kisi ka Lagna Mesha hai. Chart kholo: pehle bhav me Mesha ♈ hai. Ab grah dhoondho — maan lo ☉ (Surya) pehle bhav me hai, ♃ (Guru) 9th bhav Dhanu ♐ me hai, aur ☊ (Rahu) 7th bhav Tula ♎ me hai. Ab teen simple sentences banao: "Surya Mesha me, pehle bhav me — pehchan aur sehat se juda." "Guru Dhanu me, nauve bhav me — bhagya aur guru se juda." "Rahu Tula me, saatve bhav me — partnerships se juda." Bas. Tumne abhi ek chart ke teen chinh padh liye. Yehi kundli padhna hai — ek chinh, ek sentence, ek area. Baaki sab isi par banta hai.

Ye 5 Galtiyan Mat Karo

Ab wo galtiyan, jo chinhon ko lekar sabse zyada hoti hain. Inse bach gaye, to tum aam readers se ek kadam aage ho.

Galti 1: Ek jaise chinhon ko mix karna. ♀ aur ♂, ♃ aur ♄, ♍ aur ♏ — ye teen jode sabse zyada confuse karte hain. Solution upar wali memory aids hain: Venus = sheesha, Mars = teer; Jupiter = 4, Saturn = 5; Virgo ka M andar band, Scorpio ka M bahar teer. Chart padhne se pehle ek baar in teeno jodon ko dekh lo, phir shuru karo.

Galti 2: Sirf chinh dekh kar matlab nikalna. "Mera ☉ Mesha me hai, isliye main aisa hoon" — aisa nahi hai. Chinh sirf batata hai kaun kahan hai. Poori kahani grah + rashi + bhav, teeno mil kar banti hai. Ek chinh se poora faisla lena, ek shabd se poori kitaab samajhne jaisa hai.

Galti 3: Sun sign ko poora chart samajhna. "Meri rashi Simha hai" — iska matlab sirf ye hai ki tumhare janm ke samay Surya Simha me tha. Tumhara Chandra kahan hai, tumhara Lagna kya hai, tumhare bhav kaise hain — ye sab bhi utna hi important hai. Sun sign sirf ek chinh hai, poora chart nahi.

Galti 4: Vakri ko hamesha bura samajhna. ℞ dekh kar darna sabse aam galti hai. Vakri ka matlab sirf ye hai ki grah peeche ki taraf chalta hua lag raha tha. Parampara me iska matlab "andar ki taraf" ya "dobara sochne wala" bhi mana jata hai. Ye koi kharab nishan nahi hai — aur isse koi badi prediction kabhi mat karo.

Galti 5: Do systems ko mix karna. Western aspect angles (0°/60°/90°/120°/180°) aur Vedic drishti (bhav-based) ko ek saath mat lagao. Dono alag systems hain. Ek chart ko ek system se padho. Mix karoge, to chinh to dikhenge, par matlab galat niklega.

Final Words

Chinh sikhne ki sabse badi seekh ye hai: chart koi secret code nahi hai — wo ek map hai, aur chinh uski legend hain. Teen shapes — circle, crescent, cross — se 9 grah bante hain. Tasveeron se 12 rashiyan banti hain. Angles se aspects bante hain. Aur chhote nishan — vakri, degrees, nodes — badi picture ko poora karte hain. Ye sab yaad kar lo, to koi bhi chart tumhe dara nahi sakta.

Bas itna hi kaafi hai shuruaat ke liye.
