KP & Nadi Astrology Books: Krishnamurti Paddhati Aur Palm-Leaf Nadi Sikhne Ki Best Kitaabein — Beginner Guide

November 11, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: KP sikhne ki pehli kitaab K.S. Krishnamurti ka Krishnamurti Padhdhati Reader I — Casting the Horoscope hai, aur Nadi sikhne ki pehli kitaab R.G. Rao ki Bhrigu Nandi Nadi hai. Dono alag systems hain, dono ki kitaabein alag hain, aur dono ka order alag hai — isliye ye article dono ko ek saath isliye la raha hai, kyunki beginner dono ko ek hi jagah par milta hai: basic Jyotish khatam karne ke baad, "ab aage kya?" wale mod par. Par dono se pehle ek shart hai: birth chart ki buniyad pakki honi chahiye. Lagna, 12 bhav aur grah samajh lo, phir ye kitaabein uthao. Seedha inse shuru karoge, to KP ke sub-lord bhi bhaari lagenge aur Nadi ke yog bhi.

Honestly, KP aur Nadi dono ke baare me sabse zyada galat baatein phaili hain. KP ke baare me kehte hain ki ye "shortcut" hai — ek sawal, ek jawab, bas. Nadi ke baare me kehte hain ki taad-patra par tumhara poora jeevan likha hai, bas centre jao aur leaf nikal lo. Dono baatein aadhi sach hain, isliye khatarnak hain. KP shortcut nahi hai — ye ek poora system hai, jisme kundli banane se lekar timing nikalne tak chhe kitaabein lagti hain. Aur Nadi leaf milna guarantee nahi hai — jaisa tum aage padhoge, uski sambhavna lagbhag 10–15% mani jati hai. Is article me wahi imandari hai: har kitaab ke saath ye bhi likha hai ki wo kis kaam ki hai — aur kis kaam ki nahi.

Ek baat aur, pehle hi saaf kar doon. Ye article prediction nahi deta. Ye kitaabon ka guide hai — kaunsi kitaab kab uthani hai, kis order me, aur kin se bachna hai. KP aur Nadi dono paramparik predictive systems hain, ek adhyayan hain, scientifically proven forecasting method nahi. Inse seekhna ek baat hai, in par kisi ki zindagi ka faisla lena doosri — aur doosri wali galti tum kabhi mat karna.

KP Kya Hai, Nadi Kya Hai — Aur Dono Ek Article Me Kyun

KP — Krishnamurti Paddhati — beeswi sadi ka ek system hai, jo Madras ke K.S. Krishnamurti ne banaya tha. Ye Vedic astrology ki buniyad par khada hai: nau grah, baarah rashi aur Vimshottari Dasha, sab wahi. Par isme ek bada badlav hai — KP poore rashi wale bhavon ki jagah Placidus cusps use karta hai, aur har faladesh nakshatra, sub-lord aur cuspal interlinks se nikala jata hai. Matlab, KP ka sawaal ye nahi hai ki "tumhara swabhav kaisa hai" — KP ka sawaal ye hai ki "ye event kab hoga, aur hoga ya nahi?" Ye system event timing aur haan/na wale sawaalon ke liye bana hai.

Nadi astrology isse bilkul alag duniya hai. Parampara kehti hai ki prachin rishiyon — Agastya, Shukra aur doosron — ne hazaron logon ke jeevan ki bhavishyavani taad-patron (palm leaves) par likhi thi. Ye patre khaas taur par Tamil Nadu me rakhe hain — Vaitheeswaran Koil ka collection sabse mashhoor hai. Ek reading me pehle tumhare angoothe ke nishan se tumhara patra dhoondha jata hai, phir us par likhi baatein padhi jati hain. Tamil me "Nadi" ka matlab hai "talash me" — apne ateet, vartaman aur bhavishya ki talash. Isi liye Nadi ko "search" wali astrology bhi kehte hain.

Aur kitaabon wali Nadi ek teesri cheez hai — Bhrigu Nandi Nadi jaise granth, jisme patra dhoondhne ki jagah, grah-yogon se faladesh nikalne ka tareeka likha hai. Ye article dono ko cover karta hai: granth se Nadi sikhna, aur patra wali parampara ki sachchai — kyunki beginner ko dono ke baare me jaanna chahiye, warna wo centre par jaa kar thaga jayega.

To dono ek article me kyun? Kyunki dono ek hi jagah par milte hain: basic Jyotish ke baad. Jo vyakti Lagna aur bhav samajh chuka hai, uske saamne do raaste khulte hain — KP ka systematic raasta, aur Nadi ka granth wala raasta. Dono predictive hain. Dono ki kitaabein alag hain. Aur dono me ek hi tarah ki galtiyan hoti hain — shortcut dhoondhna, aur bina buniyad ke aage badhna. Is article me dono ke alag-alag sections hain, alag reading order hai, aur ant me ek saaf salah hai: pehle ek ko chuno, dono ko ek saath nahi.

Framework: KP Ke Sub-Lord Aur Nadi Ke Teen Rules — Kitaab Se Pehle Ye Samjho

Kitaab uthane se pehle teen cheezein samajh lo. Ye teenon is article ki kitaabon me baar-baar aayengi.

Pehla: KP ka sub-lord. KP me har nakshatra ko chhote hisson — subs — me baanta gaya hai. Krishnamurti ne taaron ke 108 hisson ko 249 hisson me baanta tha, aur yahi sub-division KP ki jaan hai. Har grah ek nakshatra me hota hai, aur us nakshatra ka swami "star lord" kehlata hai; uske andar ka chhota hissa "sub-lord" hota hai. KP me faladesh isi se nikalta hai — grah kis bhav ka significator hai, iska faisla star lord aur sub-lord mil kar karte hain. Iske saath cusps aate hain — bhavon ki exact shuruaat, jo Placidus system se nikali jati hai — aur ruling planets, jo event ki timing pakadne me madad karte hain. Ye teenon — star lord, sub-lord, cusps — KP ki bhasha hain. Inke bina Reader III ek paheli lagegi.

Doosra: Nadi ke teen rules. R.G. Rao ki Bhrigu Nandi Nadi ki poori method sirf teen rules par khadi hai — aur yahi iski khubsurti hai. Karaka: har grah ka ek fixed matlab hota hai, chahe wo kisi bhi rashi me ho — Surya pita aur adhikar, Shukra vivah, Shani karma aur vyavsay, Budh shiksha, Guru santan. Dispositor: jis rashi me grah baitha hai, us rashi ka swami us grah ke result ka director hota hai. Conjunction: ek hi rashi me baithe grah apni kahaniyan mila dete hain — aur sabse kam degree wala grah us combination par haavi rehta hai. Bas. In teen rules se Rao ne 500 se zyada kundliyon par faladesh nikala hai. Nadi granthon me Lagna ko utni ahmiyat nahi di jati jitni classical Jyotish me — ye baat Rao khud apni kitaab ki prastavana me likhte hain.

Teesra: palm-leaf wali sachchai. Ye wo baat hai jo har Nadi centre tumhe nahi batayega. Exotic India Art ke guide ke hisaab se, apna Nadi leaf milne ki sambhavna lagbhag 10–15% hoti hai. Matlab, har 10 me se 8-9 logon ko unka patra milta hi nahi. Bharat me genuine Nadi centres ka network sabse bada hai, par commercial centres bhi utne hi hain. Isliye is article ka niyam yaad rakho: kitaab se pehle Nadi seekho, centre baad me. Aur agar koi centre "leaf milna guaranteed" keh kar pehle bada advance maange, to samajh jao ki wahan se bhaagna hai.

Ab jab ye teenon dimaag me hain, to kitaabein samajh aayengi. Aur agar ye teenon abhi bhi naye lag rahe hain, to pehle hamara Birth chart reading guide padh lo — usme Lagna, 12 bhav aur dasha detail me hain. Uske bina KP ya Nadi ki kitaab uthana, bina map ke sheher me ghumne jaisa hai.

Level 1 KP: Pehli Kitaab — Krishnamurti Padhdhati Readers, Order Me

KP sikhne me sabse badi galti ye hai ki log beech se shuru karte hain — koi YouTube se "sub-lord" sunta hai, koi dost se "ruling planet" ka rule sunta hai, aur seedha prediction karne lagta hai. KP aise nahi seekha jata. Krishnamurti ne apna system chhe Readers me likha tha, ek order me — aur usi order me padhna sahi raasta hai. Ye order galti se bhi mat badlo: horary (Reader VI) pehle nahi aata, transit (Reader V) teesre number par nahi aata.

Reader I — Casting the Horoscope. Ye pehli kitaab hai, aur iska kaam hai kundli banana sikhana — KP ke hisaab se. Bhav kaise nikale, cusps kaise banaye, Placidus system kya hai — ye sab yahin se shuru hota hai. Agar tumne pehle kabhi kundli haath se nahi banayi, to ye kitaab tumhe wahi sikhayegi. Bina iske aage badhna, bina neev ke makaan banana hai.

Reader II — Fundamental Principles of Astrology. Doosri kitaab me system ki buniyad hai — KP kya manta hai, kya nahi, grah aur rashi KP me kaise kaam karte hain, aur Vimshottari Dasha ka KP me kya role hai. Ye kitaab boring lag sakti hai. Par yehi woh kitaab hai jo tumhe "KP kyun alag hai" samjhati hai. Ise skip karoge, to aage ke Readers me har rule aadha lagega.

Reader III — Predictive Stellar Astrology. Teesri kitaab asli prediction shuru karti hai — nakshatra, star lord, sub-lord aur significators ka poora method. Ye KP ki sabse zaroori kitaab hai, aur isiliye iska Hindi edition bhi milta hai — "Krishnamurti Paddhati Nakshatra Phalit Jyotish" (Krishnamurti Publications). Agar Hindi me padhna ho, to isi edition se shuru karo. Par ek baat yaad rakho: Hindi edition mile ya English, order wahi rahega — pehle I, phir II, phir III.

Ek aasan entry — Astrology for Beginners: Encyclopedia of Astrology (Six Volumes). Agar Readers ka purana English bhaari lage, to ek aasan raasta hai. Ye chhe volumes ka set Hindu, Western aur KP stellar methods ko ek saath lekar chalta hai, aur K.S. Krishnamurti ko samarpit hai. Iska apna preface saaf kehta hai: ye kitaab beginners ke liye hai, isme cusps, sub-lords aur punarphoo dosha bhi hain — par ye paanch original Readers ki jagah nahi le sakti. Matlab, ye shuruaat ke liye achhi hai, par aakhri kitaab nahi. Ise ek map ki tarah padho: pehle isse KP ka naksha samjho, phir Readers me gehrai me jao.

Hindi editions kahan milenge? Krishnamurti Publications ne Readers ke Hindi editions nikale hain — Reader 1 Hindi, Reader 3 Hindi (Nakshatra Phalit Jyotish), Reader 4 Hindi ("Vivaha, Vivahit Jeevan aur Bachhe") aur Reader 6 Hindi ("Prashna Jyotish Biggan"). Par ye kitaabein stock me aati-jati rehti hain, isliye khareedne se pehle current availability aur price check karo. Arun Kumar Bansal ki Krishnamurti Paddhati (Hindi) bhi ek vikalp hai. Aur agar Hindi edition na mile, to English Reader se shuru karne me koi harj nahi — KP ki bhasha numbers aur rules ki hai, angrezi ki nahi.

Level 2 KP: Gehrai — Readers IV–VI Aur Case Studies

Jab pehle teen Readers haath me aa jayein, tab aage ke teen khulte hain. Inhe bhi order me hi padhna.

Reader IV — Marriage, Married Life and Children. Chauthe Reader me KP ke rules vivah, vaivahik jeevan aur santan par lagaye gaye hain. Ye woh kitaab hai jisme KP ki taakat dikhti hai — ek hi event, vivah, ko kitne tareekon se timing ke saath padha ja sakta hai. Iska Hindi edition bhi hai — "Vivaha, Vivahit Jeevan aur Bachhe". Agar tumhe KP ka ek hi vishay gehrai me padhna ho, to ye kitaab woh vishay hai.

Reader V — Transits (Gocharapala Nirnayam). Paanchva Reader gochar par hai — grahon ke transit ka KP me kya matlab hai. Isme Western aur Hindu transit systems dono hain, phir har grah — Surya, Chandra, Mangal, Budh, Guru, Shukra, Shani aur nodes — ka transit alag-alag samjhaya gaya hai, aur dasha, bhukti, antra ke saath transit ko jodne ka systematic tareeka diya gaya hai. Ye kitaab tab uthao jab prediction ki buniyad pakki ho — transit pehli kitaab nahi hai.

Reader VI — Horary Astrology. Chhatha aur aakhri Reader prashna kundli par hai — jab koi sawaal lekar aaye, to usi pal ki kundli se jawab kaise nikale. Iska Hindi edition "Prashna Jyotish Biggan" naam se milta hai. Horary KP ka sabse teekha hissa hai — aur sabse zyada galat use hone wala bhi. Ise tabhi uthao jab pehle paanch Readers poore ho chuke hon. Jo vyakti Reader VI se shuru karta hai, wo KP nahi seekh raha — wo shortcut dhoondh raha hai.

Case studies se seekhne ke liye — Hariharan, Khullar, Prabhakar

Readers theory hain. Theory ke baad case studies chahiye — asli kundliyan, asli events. Uske liye ye teen lekhak hain.

Prof. K. Hariharan — Timing of Events (KP), How to Judge Nativity, Rectification of Birth Time. Hariharan KP ke sabse zyada padhe jaane wale aaj ke lekhakon me se ek hain. Unki Timing of Events — KP (Parts I–III, combined edition 2025) me events ki timing par 51 case studies hain — ye kitaab tab padho jab Reader III khatam ho gaya ho, kyunki ye usi method ko asli kundliyon par lagati hai. Unki How to Judge Nativity me 53 case studies hain, aur Rectification of Birth Time ek rare kitaab hai — janm ka samay galat ho to use KP se kaise sudharein. Rectification advanced kaam hai; beginner ke taur par isse sirf padho, practice baad me.

S.P. Khullar — True Astrology: Kalamsa and Cuspal Interlinks Theory. Khullar ne KP me cuspal interlinks par apna research likha hai — bhavon ke cusp ek doosre se kaise judte hain, aur isse faladesh kaise nikalta hai. Ye kitaab unke liye hai jo KP ke method me gehrai chahte hain, khaas taur par medical aur complex cases me. Beginner isse Level 2 ke ant me uthaye — pehle Readers, phir Hariharan, phir Khullar.

Dr. Arastu Prabhakar — Horary Astrology: A Novel Approach to Predictions, aur New Dimensions of K.P. Astrology. Prabhakar ki Horary Astrology unki Hindi kitaab Prasna Jyotish ka English translation hai — KP aur traditional astrology ka blending, live examples ke saath. Unki New Dimensions of K.P. Astrology Krishnamurti ke research ka extension hai — KP ko traditional siddhanton ke saath jod kar aur bharosemand banane ki koshish. Dono kitaabein unke liye hain jo horary me gehrai chahte hain aur jinhe KP ko classical Jyotish ke saath milakar padhna hai.

Level 1 Nadi: Pehli Kitaab — Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, R.G. Rao

Ab Nadi ki taraf. Agar KP me order sab kuch hai, to Nadi me pehli kitaab sab kuch hai — kyunki Nadi ki duniya me ek kitaab aisi hai jiske bina aage badhna hi nahi chahiye.

Bhrigu Nandi Nadi — R.G. Rao. Ye kitaab Nadi technique par likha gaya classical granth hai. Rao ne isme Nandi Nadi aur Bhrigu Nadi ke hisson ko lekar, unhe ek teachable system me badla — aur iska nateeja ye hua ki aaj Bhrigu Nandi Nadi (BNN) Vedic astrology ka sabse specific predictive system mana jata hai. Kitaab me 500 se zyada kundliyan examples ke saath hain — har yog ka faladesh ek asli chart par dikhaya gaya hai. Iska tareeka wahi teen rules hain jo upar likhe hain: karaka, dispositor, conjunction. Rao ki khaasiyat ye hai ki wo Parashari astrology ki complexity — house lords, divisional charts, Shadbala, yogas — ko hatakar, sirf teen rules se faladesh nikalte hain. Aur isi liye ye kitaab beginner ke liye hai: kam rules, zyada practice.

Ek example se samjho. Rao kehte hain: Shani vyavsay ka karaka hai. Agar Shani Budh ke saath baitha hai, aur Budh kala ka karaka hai, to vyakti kala se juda vyavsay kar sakta hai — jaise shiksha sansthan me engineering padhana. Ye faladesh teen rules se nikla: karaka (Shani = vyavsay, Budh = kala), conjunction (dono ek saath), aur dispositor (kis rashi me baithe hain). Aisa ek example nahi — kitaab me 500 se zyada hain. Isi liye is kitaab ko padhne ka tareeka ye hai: ek yog padho, phir apni ya kisi jaani-pehchani kundli par lagakar dekho. Sirf padhne se Nadi nahi aayegi; lagane se aayegi.

Ye kitaab Hindi me hai ya English me? Sach ye hai: R.G. Rao ki kitaabein English me hain, aur Nadi ki achhi kitaabein Hindi me bahut kam hain. Jo log "nadi astrology books in hindi pdf" search karte hain, unhe ye baat pehle se jaan leni chahiye — Hindi PDF ke naam par jo free files milti hain, wo aam taur par pirated, adhoori ya galat hoti hain. Sahi raasta ye hai: Rao ki kitaab English me padho — iski bhasha seedhi hai, rules teen hi hain — aur Hindi me samajhne ke liye free explainers ka sahara lo. Rao ki Bhrigu Nandi Nadi archive.org par full text me bhi available hai — ye ek legal free route hai, pirated PDF sites se behtar.

Rao ki doosri kitaabein — isi order me

Pehli kitaab ke baad Rao ki hi kitaabein aati hain, isi order me.

Chandra Kala Nadi aur Deva Keralam — dono R.G. Rao ki kitaabein hain, aur dono Nadi ke alag-alag hisson ko cover karti hain. Inhe tab uthao jab Bhrigu Nandi Nadi ke teen rules haath me aa jayein — ye kitaabein unhi rules ko naye yogon par lagati hain.

Bhrigu Prashna Nadi (Sagar Publications, pehli baar 1994 me) — ye ek alag tarah ki kitaab hai: isme 107 predictions hain, aur apni prediction dhoondhne ka tareeka ye hai ki apni umar ko 107 me se ghata do — jo number bache, wahi prediction tumhari hai. Ye prashna system hai, faladesh ka shortcut nahi. Ise ek reference kitaab ki tarah padho, pehli kitaab ki tarah nahi.

Level 2 Nadi: Gehrai — Anthvichar, Practical Nadi Aur BSP

Rao ki kitaabein khatam karne ke baad, Nadi me gehrai ke liye ye kitaabein hain.

Nadi Anthvichar — A.V. Sundaram. Ye kitaab Nadi ke vichar — anthvichar — par hai: ek yog ko dekh kar uske peeche ka logic kaise samjhe. Rao ki kitaab tumhe yog batati hai; Sundaram ki kitaab tumhe yog ke peeche sochna sikhati hai. Ye farak chhota lagta hai, par yehi farak ek achhe astrologer aur ek rata-rataya faladesh karne wale me hota hai.

Practical Nadi Astrology (Saptarishis Astrology). Exotic India Art ke buying guide me is kitaab ko "a real Gem" kaha gaya hai — aur wajah saaf hai: ye kitaab theory se zyada practice par hai. Isme Nadi ke rules ko seedhe examples par lagaya gaya hai, aur isiliye ye unke liye hai jo Rao ko padh chuke hain aur ab practice chahte hain.

Bhrigu Saral Paddathi — BSP (Saptarishis Astrology). BSP ek simplified Nadi method hai — Bhrigu Saral Paddathi, matlab Bhrigu ka seedha tareeka. Ye unke liye hai jo Nadi ke yogon ko aur seedhi bhasha me, aur tezi se, lagana chahte hain. Ise Rao ke baad uthao, Rao ki jagah nahi.

Roots of Nadi Astrology: A Comprehensive Study — Satyanarayana Naik. Ye kitaab Nadi ki jadon par hai — is parampara ka itihaas, iske granth, aur iske tareeke kaise bane. Ye unke liye hai jo Nadi ko sirf technique ki tarah nahi, ek parampara ki tarah samajhna chahte hain. Iske saath Agasthiya Maha Siva Nadi aur Ram Babu Sao ki Perfect Knowledge of Nadi Astrology bhi padhi jati hain.

Ek imandari wali baat yahan bhi. Exotic India Art ke guide me saaf likha hai: Bhrigu Nandi Nadi sikhne ka sabse achha tareeka ek knowledgeable guru ke saath classes lena hai. Kitaabein buniyad hain, guru gehrai hai. Agar tumhare sheher me ya online koi achha Nadi teacher mile, to kitaabon ke saath unse seekhna behtar hai — par teacher chunne se pehle ye article dobara padh lo, taaki "guaranteed prediction" wale teacher ko pehchan sako.

Dono Ko Saath Kaise Padhe — Pehle Ek, Phir Doosra

Ab sabse zaroori sawaal: KP aur Nadi dono ko ek saath padhe ya ek ke baad ek? Seedha jawab: ek ke baad ek.

Meri salah ye hai: pehle KP. Kyunki KP systematic hai — chhe Readers, ek order, ek method. KP se tumhe hisaab-kitab ki discipline milegi: cusps nikalna, sub-lord dekhna, significators jodna. Ye discipline Nadi me bhi kaam aayegi. KP khatam karne ke baad Nadi uthao — tab Rao ke teen rules tumhe aasan lagenge, kyunki tum grah aur bhav ki bhasha pehle se jaante hoge.

Agar tumhara dil Nadi ki taraf zyada khinchta hai, to ulta bhi kar sakte ho — pehle Rao, phir KP. Par dono ko ek saath mat shuru karo. Dono ke tareeke alag hain — KP cusp aur sub-lord se chalta hai, Nadi karaka aur conjunction se. Dono ko ek saath padhoge, to dono ke rules dimaag me mil jayenge, aur faladesh galat hoga. Ek system ko itna pakka karo ki uske rules bina soche haath me aane lagein — phir doosra shuru karo.

Aur ek baat: dono systems ko classical Vedic astrology se alag mat samjho. Dono usi ki shaakhaen hain. KP Vimshottari Dasha aur nau grah use karta hai; Nadi bhi grah aur rashi se hi chalta hai. Isliye dono ke liye buniyad wahi hai — hamara Astrology books guide aur Birth chart guide. Buniyad kamzor hogi, to KP bhi nahi aayega, Nadi bhi nahi.

Ye 5 Galtiyan Mat Karo

Ab wo galtiyan, jo KP aur Nadi me sabse zyada hoti hain. Inse bach gaye, to aadha kaam ho gaya.

Galti 1: KP ko shortcut samajhna. "Ek sub-lord dekh lo, bas" — aisa nahi hai. KP me ek event ka faisla significators, cusps, star lord, sub-lord, dasha aur ruling planets — sab mil kar karte hain. Ek rule se jawab dena KP nahi, andaza hai.

Galti 2: Readers ko beech se padhna. Reader VI se shuru karna, ya Reader III ko pehle uthana — ye KP ka order todna hai. Order wahi rahega: I, II, III, IV, V, VI. Har Reader agle ki buniyad hai.

Galti 3: Nadi centre ko pehle advance dena. "Leaf guaranteed" kehne wale ko paise dene se pehle ye yaad rakho: leaf milne ki sambhavna 10–15% hai. Pehle kitaab se seekho, centre baad me — aur tab bhi bina process samjhe advance mat do.

Galti 4: Leaf na mile to "dosha" ke naam par kharcha karna. Agar tumhara patra na mile, to iska matlab ye nahi hai ki tum par koi dosha hai jise mehenga upaay karke hatana hai.
