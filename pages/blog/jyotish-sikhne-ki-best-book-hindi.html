Jyotish Sikhne Ki Best Book in Hindi – Beginners Ke Liye Sahi Order

October 30, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: Jyotish sikhne ki koi ek best book nahi hai. Sahi order ye hai — pehle B.V. Raman ki Astrology for Beginners jaisi simple intro book se buniyad samjho, phir Dr. K.S. Charak aur Light on Life se kundli padhna practice karo, aur uske baad hi Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra ke Hindi translation par jao. Jo log seedha classical text se shuru karte hain, wo aksar 20 page ke baad hi ruk jaate hain. Order galat ho, to book achhi hone ke baad bhi kaam nahi aati.

Ye article un logon ke liye hai jo Hindi me jyotish seekhna chahte hain, par samajh nahi pa rahe ki pehli book kaunsi lein. Yahan koi lambi theory nahi milegi. Sirf ek clear rasta milega — kaunsi book kab, kis liye, aur kis book se abhi door rehna hai. Agar tum best astrology books for beginners in Hindi dhoondh rahe ho, to yehi order tumhare kaam aayega.

Jyotish Sikhne Se Pehle Ye Ek Sawaal Khud Se Poochho

Tum jyotish kyun seekhna chahte ho? Jawab se hi book badal jaati hai.

Agar tum apni kundli khud samajhna chahte ho, to tumhe pehle grah, rashi aur bhav samajhne hain. Prediction ki jaldi mat karo. Agar tum doosron ki kundli dekhna chahte ho, to tumhe practice aur dasha system par zyada waqt dena hoga. Aur agar tum sirf curiosity ke liye padh rahe ho, to ek halki, kahani wali book kaafi hai. Teenon ke liye ek hi list thopna galat hoga.

Ek aur baat. Jyotish ek paramparik vidya hai. Is article me tumhe seekhne ka rasta milega, future ki pakki guarantee nahi. Koi bhi book ye dawa nahi kar sakti ki usse har prediction sahi ho jaayega. Jo book aisa dawa kare, usse thoda savdhaan rehna.

Beginners Ki 3 Sabse Badi Galtiyan

Pehli galti: ek saath 5-6 books kharid lena. Shelf bhar jaati hai, padhai shuru nahi hoti. Ek book khatam karo, phir doosri lo.

Doosri galti: seedha Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra se shuru karna. Ye Vedic jyotish ka sabse bada classical text hai, par ye beginners ki book nahi hai. Isme sutra chhote hote hain aur matlab gehra hota hai. Bina buniyad ke ye kitaab bojh lagti hai.

Teesri galti: sirf yog aur prediction par kood jaana. Jab tak tumhe ye nahi pata ki Surya kis bhav me kya karta hai, tab tak yog ratne ka koi fayda nahi. Pehle neev, phir imarat.

Step 1: Buniyad Samjho (Pehle 2-3 Hafte)

Shuruaat ke liye tumhe ek aisi book chahiye jo Hindi ya bahut simple English me ho, aur jo grah, rashi, bhav aur nakshatra ko aaram se samjhaye.

1. Astrology for Beginners — B.V. Raman

B.V. Raman India ke sabse respected Vedic astrologers me se ek the. Unki ye book isliye beginners ke liye sahi hai, kyunki ye seedhi bhasha me likhi gayi hai. Isme tumhe 9 grah, 12 rashiyan aur 12 bhav ka basic matlab milega. Koi bhaari ganit nahi, koi darane wali terminology nahi.

Is book ko kis liye padhna hai? Taaki jab koi kahe “Mangal chauthe bhav me hai”, to tumhe turant samajh aaye ki baat kis cheez ki ho rahi hai. Is book ko kis liye nahi padhna hai? Prediction seekhne ke liye. Ye buniyad ki book hai, prediction ki nahi.

Agar tum Hindi me padhna chahte ho, to isi level ki ek simple Hindi intro book lo — jaise Jyotish Shastra: Ek Parichay type ki kitaab, jo grah, rashi aur bhav ko aam bhasha me samjhaye. Naam se zyada zaroori ye hai ki bhasha tumhari apni lage. Agar Hindi edition me anuvaad uljha hua lage, to wo book tumhare liye nahi hai, chahe uska naam kitna bhi bada kyun na ho.

Step 2: Kundli Padhna Seekho (Agle 1-2 Mahine)

Buniyad samajhne ke baad asli kaam shuru hota hai — kundli dekhna. Kitab padhna aur kundli padhna, dono alag cheezein hain.

2. Elements of Vedic Astrology — Dr. K.S. Charak

Ye do volumes ki kitaab beginners se intermediate level tak le jaati hai. Isme chart banana, grah ki sthiti dekhna aur Vimshottari dasha ko samajhna step by step diya gaya hai. Is book ki khaasiyat ye hai ki ye theory ke saath practical taraf bhi le jaati hai.

Is stage par tumhe ek kaam roz karna hai: apni ya apne parivaar ki ek kundli kholo, aur sirf ek cheez dekho. Aaj Surya kis bhav me hai? Kal Chandra kis rashi me hai? Roz ek grah, ek bhav. Das din me tumhe pattern dikhne lagenge.

3. Light on Life — Hart de Fouw aur Robert Svoboda

Ye book un logon ke liye hai jo English me comfortable hain aur Vedic astrology ko modern, samajhne layak bhasha me padhna chahte hain. Isme theory aur practical ka balance achha hai. Isliye beginners ki list me iska naam baar-baar aata hai.

Ek chhota sa example samjho. Maan lo kisi ki kundli me Guru paanchve bhav me hai. Sirf ye jaanna kaafi nahi ki Guru achha grah hai. Tumhe ye dekhna hoga ki Guru kis rashi me hai, kis grah ki drishti us par hai, aur wo kis bhav ka swami hai. Yehi farq hai ratne aur samajhne me. Step 2 ki books tumhe yehi farq sikhati hain.

Is stage par Bepin Behari ki Fundamentals of Vedic Astrology bhi kaam aati hai. Ye book jyotish ko sirf ganit nahi, ek soch ke roop me samjhati hai. Agar tumhe sirf formula wali kitaabein boring lagti hain, to ye book tumhara interest banaye rakhegi.

Step 3: Classical Texts Par Jao (Jab Buniyad Pakki Ho)

Ab tum classical texts ke liye tayyar ho. Ab ye kitaabein bojh nahi lagengi, kyunki tumhe pehle se pata hoga ki baat kis cheez ki ho rahi hai.

4. Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra — Hindi Translation

Ye Maharishi Parashar ko samarpit Vedic jyotish ka sabse authoritative text mana jata hai. Isme grah, rashi, bhav, yog aur dasha system — sab kuch detail me hai. Hindi translation, jaise Dr. Sureshchandra Mishra ki vyakhya wali edition, isko aam reader ke liye thoda aasaan banati hai.

Par ek sach ye bhi hai: Hindi translation hone ka matlab ye nahi ki ye aasaan ho gayi. Sutra abhi bhi sutra hain. Isliye is book ko reference ki tarah padho — poori kitaab ek baar me khatam karne ki zid mat karo. Jab Step 2 me koi concept atke, tab is book me uska mool dekh lo.

5. Phaladeepika — Mantreswara

Ye kitaab prediction ki taraf ka kadam hai. Isme grah combinations, yog aur unke phal systematic tareeke se diye gaye hain. Dasha analysis aur Ashtakvarga jaisi cheezein bhi yahan milti hain. Is book ko tab uthao jab tum kundli me grah aur bhav ko aaram se padh lete ho.

Yahan ek zaroori baat samajh lo. Classical text padhna aur usko aaj ki kundli par lagana, dono me farq hai. Purani kitaab me jo yog likha hai, wo har kundli me ek jaise phal nahi dega. Desh, kaal aur patra — matlab samay, jagah aur vyakti ki sthiti — sab farq daalte hain. Jo ye baat samajh jaata hai, wo andha prediction nahi karta.

Step 4: Specialized Branches — Kab, Aur Kab Nahi?

Ab tumhare saamne do raaste aayenge: KP System aur Lal Kitab. Dono apni jagah sahi hain, par dono shuruaat ke liye nahi hain.

KP System, jo Prof. K.S. Krishnamurti ka horary aur sub-lord method hai, prediction me precision ke liye jaana jaata hai. Par isko samajhne ke liye pehle basic kundli, bhav aur dasha pakke hone chahiye. Bina buniyad ke KP sirf formula lagta hai.

Lal Kitab alag hi duniya hai. Isme upay aur remedies par zyada zor hai. Agar tumhe Lal Kitab me interest hai, to pehle ye tay karo ki tum seekhna chahte ho ya upay dhoondhna chahte ho. Seekhne ke liye pehle Parashari buniyad zaroori hai, warna upay ka matlab samajh nahi aayega.

Seedhi salah: Step 1 se Step 3 tak poora kiye bina in branches me mat koodo. Jaldi karne se sirf confusion badhta hai.

Hindi Me Book Kaise Chunen? Ek Chhoti Checklist

Hindi me jyotish ki kitaabein bahut hain, par sab ek jaise quality ki nahi hoti. Book kharidne se pehle ye 4 cheezein dekh lo:

  • Anuvaadak ka naam clear hai ya nahi? Agar translator ka naam aur edition saaf nahi hai, to wo edition risky hai.
  • Shuruaat ke 10 page padh kar dekho. Agar pehle 10 page me hi bhasha ulajh jaaye, to aage aur mushkil hogi.
  • Udaharan hain ya sirf sutra? Beginners ke liye udaharan wali book zyada kaam ki hai.
  • Book kis level ki hai? Cover par “advanced” likha ho, to usko baad ke liye rakh do, abhi mat lo.

Ye checklist tumhe galat khareed se bacha legi. Aur haan, “jyotish ebooks” search karte waqt har free PDF legal nahi hoti. Copyright wali kitaab ka pirated PDF download karna sahi rasta nahi hai. Legal source se lo, chahe thoda waqt lage.

30 Din Ka Simple Practice Plan

Book kharidna aasaan hai. Roz padhna mushkil hai. Isliye ek chhota plan bana lo:

  1. Din 1-7: Roz ek grah. Uska matlab, uski rashi, uska bhav — bas itna.
  2. Din 8-14: Roz ek kundli kholo aur sirf lagna aur Chandra rashi dekho.
  3. Din 15-21: Roz ek bhav par focus karo. Pehle bhav se baarahve bhav tak.
  4. Din 22-30: Ek poori kundli ko 10 kadam me dekhne ki koshish karo — lagna, grah, bhav, drishti, dasha. Galti hogi, wo theek hai.

30 din me tum expert nahi ban jaoge. Par tum wo stage cross kar loge jahan sab kuch uljha hua lagta hai. Aur wahi asli shuruaat hai.

Kya Sirf Books Kaafi Hain?

Seedha jawab: nahi. Books tumhe gyaan dengi, par kundli dekhne ka confidence practice se aayega. Ek experienced teacher, ek study group, ya roz ki gayi practice — inke bina theory adhoori reh jaati hai.

Isliye books ke saath ek kaam aur karo: roz ek kundli par 15 minute lagao. Chahe galti ho, chahe samajh adhoori rahe. Jo roz karta hai, wo aage badhta hai. Jo sirf kitaabein jama karta hai, wo wahi ka wahi rehta hai.

Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

Jyotish sikhne ki best book kaunsi hai?

Ek nahi, ek order hai. Pehle B.V. Raman ki beginner book, phir Charak aur Light on Life se practice, aur aakhir me Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra ka Hindi translation.

Jyotish sikhne ke liye best book in Hindi kaunsi hai?

Hindi me simple intro book se shuru karo, phir classical texts ke Hindi translation par jao. Edition chunne se pehle translator ka naam aur pehle 10 page zaroor check karo.

Bina guru ke jyotish seekha ja sakta hai?

Buniyad aur kundli padhna books aur practice se seekha ja sakta hai. Par gehri aur advanced cheezon ke liye ek experienced guide bahut madad karta hai. Sirf books par poora bharosa mat karo.

Jyotish seekhne me kitna waqt lagta hai?

Buniyad samajhne me 2-3 mahine, kundli aaram se padhne me 6-12 mahine, aur gehri samajh me saalon lagte hain. Jo 30 din me expert banne ka dawa kare, usse savdhaan rehna.

Lal Kitab aur KP System kab seekhen?

Jab Parashari buniyad, bhav, grah aur dasha pakke ho jaayein, tab. Shuru me ye branches sirf confusion badhati hain.
