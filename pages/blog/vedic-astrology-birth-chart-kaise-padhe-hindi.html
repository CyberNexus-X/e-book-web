Vedic Birth Chart Kaise Padhe – Apni Janma Kundli Khud Samajhne Ka Seedha Rasta

November 8, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: Apni Vedic birth chart padhne ke chaar kadam hain, aur inhi ke order me padhna hai — pehle Lagna, phir Chandra ki rashi aur nakshatra, phir 12 bhav, aur aakhir me Vimshottari dasha. Isse pehle ek kaam free me kar lo: AstroSage ya Astro-Seek par apni Janma Kundli banao — sirf janm ki tareekh, sahi janm samay aur janm sthan chahiye. Kitaab baad me, pehle apni chart haath me lo. Kyunki chart padhna seekhna, chart ko dekh kar hi aata hai — sirf theory padh kar nahi.

Honestly, kundli dekhne walon ki sabse badi dikkat ye nahi hai ki unke paas chart nahi hai. Chart to aaj free me do minute me ban jaata hai. Dikkaat ye hai ki chart khulne ke baad samajh nahi aata ki pehle kya dekhein. Koi kehta hai "rashi dekho", koi kehta hai "Shani dekho", koi ek yog ka naam sun kar darata hai. Aur beech me tum — ek diamond jaisi aakriti, usme 12 khane, unme grahon ke naam, aur koi raasta nahi.

Is article me wahi raasta hai. Chaar kadam, ek seedha order, aur har kadam par ye bhi ki wo kyun zaroori hai. Saath me 12 bhavon ka matlab, grah padhne ka tareeka, dasha ka 120 saal ka chakra, aur wo 5 galtiyan jo har beginner karta hai. Aur aakhir me kitaabein — par sirf do, kyunki poori list to hamare Astrology books guide me pehle se hai.

Ek baat pehle hi saaf kar dete hain, kyunki isme dhoka sabse zyada hai. Jyotish ek paramparik adhyayan pranali hai — isse grah, rashi, bhav aur dasha ka dhancha samajh sakte ho, apne faislon par chintan kar sakte ho. Par ye vigyan se siddh bhavishyavani nahi hai. Koi kitaab, koi upay, koi ratna bhavishya ki guarantee nahi de sakta. Jo ye dawa kare, usse door raho — chahe wo free chart de raha ho ya ₹5,000 ki report bech raha ho.

Pehle Ye Samajh Lo: Kundli Kya Hai, Kya Nahi Hai

Janma Kundli, ya Janam Kundali, tumhare janm ke sahi pal aur sthan par aasmaan ka naksha hai. Us pal Surya, Chandra aur baaki saat grah kis rashi me, kis bhav me the — wahi is naksh me likha hota hai. Isi liye isko banane ke liye teen cheezein chahiye: janm ki tareekh, sahi janm samay, aur janm sthan. Inme se ek bhi galat ho, to chart bhi galat.

Ek chhota sa example. Socho do log ek hi din, ek hi sheher me paida hue — ek subah 6 baje, doosra dopahar 2 baje. Dono ki rashi aksar ek jaisi hogi, kyunki Chandra ek rashi me lagbhag dhaaee din rehta hai. Par unka Lagna alag hoga, kyunki Lagna har lagbhag do ghante me badalta hai. Aur Lagna badla, to poora house system badal gaya. Isi liye janm samay sabse nazuk cheez hai — 30-40 minute ki galti bhi Lagna ko agli rashi me dhakel sakti hai.

Aur ek farak jo bahut log nahi jaante. Akhbaar wali rashi aam taur par Western (tropical) Sun sign hoti hai. Vedic Jyotish sidereal hai — wo taaron ke hisaab se chalta hai, aur equinox ke precession ko dhyan me rakhta hai. Isi liye tumhara Vedic Surya ya Chandra sign akhbaar wali rashi se alag ho sakta hai. Ye koi galti nahi hai — do alag system hain. Is article me hum sirf Vedic system ki baat kar rahe hain.

To kundli kya nahi hai? Ye koi faisla nahi hai. Ye ek naksha hai — jaise sheher ka map tumhe raaste dikhata hai, par tum kaunsa raasta chuno, ye tum tay karte ho. Isi nazariye se padho, to chart kaam ka hai. Dar ya lalach se padho, to wahi chart tumhe uljha dega.

Free Me Apni Kundli Banao: Pehla Kadam, Paise Baad Me

Chart padhna seekhne se pehle chart banao. Aaj iske liye kisi pandit ke paas jaane ki zaroorat nahi — free tools hain, aur achhe hain.

AstroSage ki free Janam Kundli sabse seedha raasta hai — naam, gender, janm tareekh, janm samay aur janm sthan bhara, aur chart saamne. Hindi me bhi milti hai, isliye ye Hindi readers ke liye khaas taur par aasan hai. Astro-Seek doosra achha option hai — wahan chart banate waqt "Vedic" ya "Jyotish" chuno aur Lahiri ayanamsha rakho, jo standard setting hai.

Lahiri ayanamsha kya hai? Seedhe shabdon me: Vedic aur Western ganana me ek chhota sa antar hota hai, jise ayanamsa kehte hain. Bharat me zyadatar jyotishi Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa istemal karte hain — yahi Bharat Sarkar ke Rashtriya Panchang me bhi hai. Isliye jab bhi chart banao, ye setting check kar lo. Galat ayanamsa matlab grah galat jagah — aur phir poora padhna galat.

Aur haan, chart banne ke baad usko PDF me save kar lo. Har baar dobara banana padega, to waqt zaya hoga. Ek baar bani hui chart ko saamne rakh kar hi is article ke kadam follow karo — padhte jaao, apni chart me dekhte jaao.

Ek side note, jo kaam aayega. Agar janm samay bilkul nahi pata — na certificate par, na ghar par kisi ko yaad — to chart mat banao, aise hi andaza mat lagao. Bina sahi samay ke Lagna aur bhav bharosemand nahi hote. Par Chandra ki rashi aur nakshatra aksar phir bhi sahi rehte hain, isliye paramparik tareeke se Chandra-lagna chart se kaam chalaya jata hai. Janm samay ke liye janm certificate ya hospital record sabse bharosemand hai.

North Indian Chart Kaise Dekhte Hain

Tumhari chart khulne par pehli cheez jo dikhegi, wo ek diamond jaisi aakriti hai — ye North Indian style hai, jo Bharat me sabse zyada istemal hoti hai. Isme rashiyan anti-clockwise chalti hain, aur Lagna wali rashi pehla bhav hoti hai.

Chart me "Lg" ya "As" likha milega — ye Lagna ka nishan hai. Jis khane me ye ho, wo tumhara pehla bhav hai. Uske baad ki rashi doosra bhav, phir teesra — aise hi 12 bhav poore hote hain. Har khane me grahon ke naam likhe honge — jaise Su (Surya), Ch (Chandra), Ma (Mangal), Bu (Budha), Gu (Guru), Sh (Shukra), Sa (Shani), Ra (Rahu), Ke (Ketu).

Ek aur chart bhi milegi — Navamsa, ya D9 chart. Ye D1 (Rasi) chart ki saathi hai. D1 poori zindagi ka main chart hai; D9 vivah/partnership aur grahon ki asli taakat samajhne ke liye istemal hoti hai. Beginner ke taur par pehle sirf D1 par dhyan do — D9 baad me, jab D1 haath me aa jaye.

Ab chaar kadam. Order mat badalna — yehi order parampara me istemal hota hai, aur isi order me chart khulti hai.

Kadam 1: Lagna — Sabse Pehle, Kyunki Yahi Se Sab Shuru Hota Hai

Lagna wo rashi hai jo tumhare janm ke pal poorab (east) me ug rahi thi. Vedic chart me ye sabse mahatvapurna point maana jata hai. Kyun? Kyunki Lagna se poora house system banta hai. Lagna wali rashi pehla bhav, uske baad wali doosra, aur aise hi aage.

Lagna tumhare shareer, swabhav aur poori zindagi ki disha ko rang deta hai. Jaise — Mesha (Aries) Lagna wale aksar seedhe, tez aur pehal karne wale hote hain; Karka (Cancer) Lagna wale zyada bhavuk aur ghar se jude hote hain. Ye koi niyam nahi, ek jhukaav hai — poori tasveer to baaki grahon se banti hai.

Apni chart me pehle ye dekho: Lagna kis rashi me hai, aur Lagna ka swami (us rashi ka grah) kahan baitha hai. Lagna ka swami jahan baitha hai, wo bhav tumhari zindagi me khaas taur par sakriya rehta hai. Jaise, Vrishabh (Taurus) Lagna ka swami Shukra hai — agar Shukra 10th bhav me hai, to career aur naam ka mamla tumhari pehchan se jud jayega.

Ye kadam isliye pehle hai, kyunki bina Lagna ke bhav ka koi matlab nahi. Grah kahan hai, ye tabhi samajh aayega jab pata ho ki pehla bhav kahan se shuru hota hai.

Kadam 2: Chandra — Rashi Aur Nakshatra, Mann Ka Naksha

Lagna ke baad doosra anchor hai Chandra — Moon. Vedic Jyotish me Chandra ki rashi (Rashi) ko Surya se zyada mahatva diya jata hai. Kyun? Kyunki Chandra mann, bhavna aur aadat se judta hai. Surya tumhara core self hai, par Chandra wo hai jo roz-roz tumhe chalata hai.

Chandra har rashi me lagbhag dhaaee din rehta hai, isliye ye janm ke pal ko bahut personal tareeke se pakadta hai. Tumhari Chandra rashi hi wo hai jise aam bol-chaal me "rashi" kaha jata hai — jaise "meri rashi Kanya hai".

Par Chandra ke saath ek aur cheez dekho: nakshatra. Rashi ko 27 nakshatron me baanta gaya hai, har nakshatra 13 degree 20 minute ka. Tumhara janm nakshatra wo hai jisme janm ke samay Chandra tha. Ye isliye bahut mahatvapurna hai, kyunki isi nakshatra se tumhara dasha kram tay hota hai — kaunsi dasha kab aayegi, ye yahin se shuru hota hai. Nakshatra ka naam janm ke samay ke naam-akshar se bhi judta hai, aur vivah milan me bhi istemal hota hai.

To doosre kadam me ye note karo: Chandra kis rashi me hai, kis nakshatra me hai, aur kis bhav me hai. Ye teenon mil kar tumhare mann ka naksha banate hain.

Kadam 3: 12 Bhav — Zindagi Ke 12 Hisse

Ab chart ke 12 khane, ya bhav. Har bhav zindagi ke ek hisse ko dikhata hai. Grah kahan baitha hai, isse pata chalta hai ki uski urja zindagi ke kis hisse me dikhegi.

Bhavon ko teen samuhon me baanta jata hai. Kendra bhav (1, 4, 7, 10) — action wale, zindagi ki chaar dishaen. Trikona bhav (1, 5, 9) — bhagya aur dharma wale. Dusthana bhav (6, 8, 12) — chunauti wale, jahan mehnat aur dhyaan zyada chahiye. Ye samuh yaad rakhna, kyunki ye aage grah padhne me kaam aayenge.

  • 1st bhav (Lagna): Khud, shareer, swabhav, poori zindagi ki disha.
  • 2nd bhav: Dhan, parivar, vaani, khana, bachpan.
  • 3rd bhav: Bhai-behen, himmat, communication, mehnat.
  • 4th bhav: Ghar, maa, sukh, sampatti, mann ki shanti.
  • 5th bhav: Buddhi, creativity, santan, padhai, prem.
  • 6th bhav: Swasthya, seva, karz, pratiyogi, roz ka kaam.
  • 7th bhav: Vivah, partnership, vyavsayik saathi.
  • 8th bhav: Lambi umar, rahasya, achanak badlav, sasural se dhan.
  • 9th bhav: Bhagya, dharma, guru, lambi yatra, uchch shiksha.
  • 10th bhav: Career, kaam, naam, samaj me sthiti.
  • 11th bhav: Laabh, dost, ichha poori hona.
  • 12th bhav: Kharch, videsh, neend, adhyatm, moksha ki taraf jhukaav.

Ab ek baat jo beginners aksar bhool jaate hain: bhav ko sirf usme baithe grah se mat padho. Bhav ka swami kahan hai, ye bhi dekho. Jaise, 10th bhav khaali ho, par uska swami 9th bhav me baitha ho — to career ka fayda bhagya aur shiksha se jud jayega. Khaali bhav ka matlab "kuch nahi" nahi hota. Uska swami hi uski kahani sunata hai.

Kadam 4: Grah Kaise Padhein, Aur Dasha Kya Hai

Ab grah. Vedic Jyotish me 9 grah hain — Navagraha: Surya, Chandra, Mangal, Budha, Guru, Shukra, Shani, Rahu aur Ketu. Har grah ka apna kaam hai: Surya — aatma aur pita; Chandra — mann aur maa; Mangal — himmat aur action; Budha — buddhi aur vyapar; Guru — gyaan aur vistar; Shukra — prem aur dhan; Shani — anushasan aur mehnat; Rahu-Ketu — ichha aur mukti ki taraf kheench.

Grah ko padhne ka tareeka ye hai: pehle dekho wo kis rashi me hai (uski taakat — uchch, neech, mitra rashi ya shatru rashi), phir dekho kis bhav me hai (uski urja kahan dikhegi), phir dekho wo kisko dekhta hai (drishti — grah apne bhav se 7th bhav ko, aur kuch grah khaas bhavon ko bhi dekhte hain). Aur phir dekho wo bhav ka swami kahan hai.

Ek grah se koi faisla nahi hota. Parampara me grah ko bhav ke swami, usme baithe grahon, drishti, aur grah ki sthiti — uchch, neech, ast ya vakri — ke saath mil kar padha jata hai. Yog — jaise Raja yog ya Dhana yog — tabhi mante hain jab unki poori shartein poori hon. Ek naam sun kar khush hona ya darna, dono galat hai.

Ab dasha — ye Vedic Jyotish ki sabse khaas cheez hai. Vimshottari Dasha tumhari poori zindagi (120 saal ka chakra) ko alag-alag grahon ke samay me baant deti hai. Har grah ek nishchit saal tak tumhari zindagi ka "samay" chalata hai: Ketu 7 saal, Shukra 20, Surya 6, Chandra 10, Mangal 7, Rahu 18, Guru 16, Shani 19, Budha 17. Tum kis nakshatra me paida hue, usse tay hota hai ki tum kis grah ki dasha se shuru karte ho.

Har badi dasha (Mahadasha) ke andar chhoti dashayen (Antardasha) bhi hoti hain. Jis grah ki Mahadasha chal rahi hai, wo us samay ki sabse badi urja hoti hai. Isi liye dasha ko samay ka chakra kehte hain — chart batata hai "kya", dasha batata hai "kab".

Ek example se samjho. Socho tumhari chart me Guru 10th bhav me baitha hai — career ke liye achha. Par agar Guru ki Mahadasha tumhari zindagi me 50 saal baad aayegi, to uska poora fayda tabhi dikhega. Aur agar abhi Shani ki Mahadasha chal rahi hai, to samay mehnat aur anushasan ka hai — Guru ka phal intezar karega. Isi liye chart padhne me dasha ko aakhir me nahi, balki sabse zaroori hisson me ginna chahiye.

Ye 5 Galtiyan Mat Karo

Ab wo galtiyan, jo har beginner karta hai. Inse bach gaye, to aadha kaam ho gaya.

Galti 1: Sirf Sun sign padhna. Akhbaar wali rashi se poori kundli nahi padhi jaati. Vedic me Lagna aur Chandra zyada mahatva rakhte hain. Sun sign ek tukda hai, poori tasveer nahi.

Galti 2: Ek grah se faisla karna. "Mangal 7th me hai to vivah kharab" — aisa ek line me kehna galat hai. Mangal kis rashi me hai, uska swami kahan hai, drishti kya hai — ye sab dekhe bina koi faisla nahi banta.

Galti 3: Yog ka naam sun kar khush ya dar jaana. Raja yog, Dhana yog — ye naam sunne me achhe lagte hain. Par yog tabhi mante hain jab unki poori shartein poori hon. Ek grah ki sthiti se yog nahi banta.

Galti 4: Dasha ko ignore karna. Chart bina dasha ke adhoori hai. Achha grah bhi tabhi phal deta hai jab uska samay aaye. Dasha dekhe bina "kab" ka jawab nahi milta.

Galti 5: Paid prediction ko guarantee samajhna. Koi bhi report, chahe ₹500 ki ho ya ₹5,000 ki, bhavishya ki guarantee nahi de sakti. Prediction ko salah ki tarah lo, faisle ki tarah nahi. Career, paise ya sehat ke bade faislon ke liye hamesha kisi qualified professional se bhi baat karo.

4 Hafte Ka Seedha Plan

Hafta 1: AstroSage ya Astro-Seek par apni free kundli banao aur PDF save karo. Lagna aur Chandra ki rashi-nakshatra note karo. Maqsad: apni chart ko pehchan na — ye kya hai, isme kya kya hai.

Hafta 2: 12 bhavon ka matlab yaad karo — apni chart me har bhav me kaunsa grah hai, ye likh lo. Khaali bhavon ke swami kahan hain, ye bhi note karo. Maqsad: chart ka naksha dimaag me bithana.

Hafta 3: Grah padhna seekho — rashi, bhav, drishti. Aur apni Vimshottari dasha dekho: abhi kis grah ki Mahadasha chal rahi hai, aur agle 2-3 saal me kaunsi aayegi. Maqsad: "kya" ke saath "kab" jodna.

Hafta 4: How to Judge a Horoscope ka pehla volume shuru karo, aur apni chart par uske niyam lagao. Apne parivar ki ek-do chart bhi banao aur unme wahi kadam dohrayein. Maqsad: ek chart se doosri chart tak haath saaf karna.
