Numerology Kya Hai? Mulank, Bhagyank aur Naamank Nikalne ka Hindi Guide

November 18, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: Numerology — Hindi me Ank Jyotish — ek paramparik vidya hai jo maanti hai ki tumhari janm tareekh aur tumhare naam ke aksharon se bane ank tumhare swabhav aur jeevan ki disha ke baare me kuch kehte hain. Iska poora kaam ek hi operation par khada hai: ankon ko jodte jao, jab tak ek ank na bache. 27 ho to 2+7 = 9. 1987 ho to 1+9+8+7 = 25, phir 2+5 = 7. Bas. Isse zyada maths isme hai hi nahi — aur isiliye ye vidya itni popular hai: calculator kholne ki zaroorat nahi, kagaz aur pen kaafi hai.

To phir isme seekhne layak kya hai? Teen ank. Sirf teen. Mulank — tumhare janm ke din se. Bhagyank — tumhari poori janm tithi se. Naamank — tumhare naam ke aksharon se. In teeno ko nikalna aaj tum 10 minute me seekh loge. Par inka matlab kya hai, kaunsa system kis table se chalata hai, aur kahan iski limit hai — ye samajhna hi asli kaam hai. Kyunki internet par har doosri site tumhe ek calculator degi aur ek number dekar chhod degi. Ye article calculator nahi hai. Ye tumhe calculation sikhata hai — taaki koi bhi site ya koi bhi vyakti tumhe jo number bataye, tum khud check kar sako ki wo sahi hai ya nahi.

Ek imandari wali baat pehle hi — aur ye is article me aakhri baar nahi aayegi. Numerology ki predictions ka koi peer-reviewed scientific evidence nahi hai. Ye ek belief framework hai, ek self-reflection ka tool — tested science nahi. Is article ko isi angle se padho: ek purani parampara ko samajhne ke liye, apne baare me sochne ke ek naye tareeke ke liye — apni zindagi ke faisle is par mat chhodo. Aur haan, is article me koi "lucky number" nahi becha ja raha. Jo number tumhara hai, wo tum khud nikaloge — free me, yahin.

Mulank Kaise Nikale — Sirf Janm ki Tareekh Se

Mulank — English me Psychic Number ya Root Number, kabhi-kabhi Moolank bhi likha jata hai — numerology ka sabse pehla aur sabse aasan ank hai. Isme sirf ek cheez chahiye: tumhare janm ka din. Mahina nahi, saal nahi. Sirf tareekh.

Method ek line ka hai: janm ki tareekh ke ankon ko jodo, jab tak ek ank na bache.

Teen examples se samjho:

  • 5 tareekh ko janme ho — to Mulank seedha 5 hai. Single digit hai, jodne ki zaroorat hi nahi.
  • 23 tareekh ko janme ho — 2 + 3 = 5. Mulank 5.
  • 29 tareekh ko janme ho — 2 + 9 = 11, aur 11 phir do ankon ka hai, to 1 + 1 = 2. Mulank 2.

Ye teesra example dhyaan se dekho — kyunki yahi wo jagah hai jahan log galti karte hain. 29 ka Mulank 11 nahi hai, 2 hai. Indian parampara me aam taur par 11 aur 22 ko bhi reduce kar diya jata hai. Western numerology me inhe "master numbers" keh kar rok liya jata hai — par Indian Ank Jyotish me ye rule aam taur par nahi chalta. Agar koi site tumhe 29 tareekh ka Mulank 11 bataye, to wo Western system use kar rahi hai, Indian nahi. Dono galat nahi hain — bas alag systems hain. Aur is article me hum Indian system follow kar rahe hain.

Mulank ko tumhare sabse instinctive swabhav se joda jata hai — wo energy jisse tum bina soche react karte ho. Parampara me kehte hain ki Mulank wo hai jo tum ho, aur Bhagyank wo hai jo tumhari zindagi kidhar ja rahi hai. Dono ko alag rakhna — ye farak samajhna hi numerology ki pehli seekh hai.

Bhagyank Kaise Nikale — Poori Janm Tithi Se

Bhagyank — English me Destiny Number ya Life Path Number — Mulank se ek kadam aage hai. Isme tumhari poori janm tithi lagti hai: din + mahina + saal. Sab ke ank jodo, phir reduce karo, jab tak ek ank na bache.

Method wahi hai, input poora hai. Teen worked examples:

Example 1: 23 August 1987. Pehle din ko reduce karo: 23 → 2 + 3 = 5. Mahina: August = 8. Saal: 1987 → 1 + 9 + 8 + 7 = 25, phir 2 + 5 = 7. Ab teeno ko jodo: 5 + 8 + 7 = 20, phir 2 + 0 = 2. Bhagyank 2.

Example 2: 14 August 1992. Saare ank ek saath jodo: 1 + 4 + 0 + 8 + 1 + 9 + 9 + 2 = 34, phir 3 + 4 = 7. Bhagyank 7. Dono tareeke — alag-alag reduce karke jodna ya sab ek saath jodna — aksar ek hi jawab dete hain, kyunki jodne ka order final root ko nahi badalta. Jo tareeka tumhe aasan lage, wahi use karo.

Example 3: 25 November 1985. 2 + 5 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 9 + 8 + 5 = 32, phir 3 + 2 = 5. Bhagyank 5.

Sabse common galti yahan hoti hai: saal ke aakhri do ank lena. 1985 likhne ki jagah log 85 jod dete hain — aur phir poora hisaab galat ho jata hai. Hamesha poore chaar ank likho. Doosri galti: mahine ko naam se jodna. November ka koi number nahi hai — November = 11 → 1 + 1 = 2. Mahina hamesha number me likho, phir reduce karo.

Aur ek baat jo calculator sites nahi batati: Bhagyank tumhara kabhi nahi badalta. Janm tithi fixed hai, to Bhagyank bhi fixed hai. Mulank bhi fixed hai. Jo badalta hai, wo hai Personal Year — us par aage ek poora section hai. In teeno ko mix mat karo: Mulank = din, Bhagyank = poori tithi, Personal Year = is saal ka theme.

Aur haan — lagbhag 9 me se 1 vyakti ka har ank hota hai, kyunki ank sirf tareekh se nikalta hai. Isiliye Mulank ya Bhagyank se koi personal prediction nahi banti. Ye baat yaad rakho, kyunki aage jahan "lucky number" aur "name correction" ki baat aayegi, wahi ye samajh kaam aayegi.

Naamank Kya Hai — Naam Se Number Kaise Nikale

Teenon ankon me sabse interesting — aur sabse zyada confuse karne wala — hai Naamank, yaani Name Number. Mulank aur Bhagyank tareekh se bante hain, jo kabhi nahi badalti. Naamank tumhare naam se banta hai — aur naam, kam se kam spelling, badla ja sakta hai. Isi liye iske aas-paas sabse zyada dukanen khuli hain. Pehle method samjho, phir us dukan ki baat karenge.

Naamank nikalne ke liye har akshar ko ek number diya jata hai, sab ko joda jata hai, aur phir single digit tak reduce kiya jata hai. Sawal ye hai: kaunsa table? Kyunki do tables hain, aur dono se alag jawab aata hai.

India me standard hai Chaldean table — jise Cheiro chart bhi kehte hain. Iska ek khaas rule hai: har akshar ki value 1 se 8 hoti hai. Kisi bhi akshar ko 9 nahi diya jata. Chaldean parampara me 9 ko sacred aur complete mana jata hai — isiliye wo letter table se bahar rakha gaya. 9 sirf total ke roop me aa sakta hai, kisi ek akshar se nahi.

Table dekh kar pehli baat jo dimag me aayegi, wahi sabki aati hai: ye alphabetical order me kyun nahi hai? A, I, J, Q, Y ek saath kyun hain? Iska jawab ye hai ki Chaldean table spelling par nahi, sound par banta hai — parampara ke hisaab se jin aksharon ki dhvani ek jaisi hai, unki value ek hai. Isiliye ise yaad karne ka koi shortcut nahi hai. Dekh kar nikalna padta hai. Aur yahi farak hai Pythagorean table se, jisme A=1, B=2, C=3… I=9, phir J=1 se dobara — pure alphabetical order, yaad karna aasan.

Ab ek worked example — mere apne naam se, taaki tum khud check kar sako:

ARHAN, Chaldean table se: A = 1, R = 2, H = 5, A = 1, N = 5. Total: 1 + 2 + 5 + 1 + 5 = 14, phir 1 + 4 = 5. Naamank 5 — Budha, communication aur adaptability ka ank.

Wahi ARHAN, Pythagorean table se: A = 1, R = 9, H = 8, A = 1, N = 5. Total: 1 + 9 + 8 + 1 + 5 = 24, phir 2 + 4 = 6. Naamank 6 — Shukra, harmony aur care ka ank.

Ek naam, do systems, do alag jawab — 5 aur 6. Dono "sahi" hain, apne-apne system me. Aur yahi wajah hai ki Naamank par sabse zyada jhagde hote hain. Koi site tumhe 5 batayegi, koi 6 — aur dono apni jagah sahi hain. Isiliye rule simple hai: ek system chuno, aur ek mahine tak usi par raho. India me rehte hue, aur Indian kitaabein padhte hue, Chaldean hi natural choice hai — kyunki yahan ki parampara usi par khadi hai.

Ek aur baat jo Cheiro ke system me khaas hai: compound number. Total ko reduce karne se pehle, us total ko bhi padha jata hai. ARHAN ka total 14 hai — pehle 14 ko padha jata hai, phir uska root 5. Cheiro ne compounds 10 se 52 tak ke readings diye the. Matlab, do naam jinka final ank ek ho — jaise dono ka 5 — unke compound alag ho sakte hain, aur parampara me unka flavour alag mana jata hai. Ye detail beginner ke liye zaroori nahi hai, par ye jaanna zaroori hai ki Naamank sirf ek digit nahi hai — uske peeche ek poora total chhupa hota hai.

Ek imandari wali baat: na to koi system scientifically proven hai, na koi doosre se "zyada sahi" hai. Dono symbolic traditions hain. Isiliye chunav is baat par karo ki tum kis parampara ko follow karna chahte ho — Indian kitaabein aur Indian numerologists Chaldean use karte hain, Western kitaabein Pythagorean. Agar tum Hindi guides padh rahe ho, to Chaldean lo. Agar tum Western bestsellers padh rahe ho, to Pythagorean lo. Bas ek ko chuno. Aur jo site tumhe Naamank bataye bina ye bataye ki usne kaunsa table use kiya, uska number interpretable hi nahi hai — pehle table poochho, phir number mano.

History ki ek chhoti si jhalak, kyunki ye jaanna mazedaar hai: Cheiro — asal naam William John Warner — ek Irish palmist the, jinhone 1926 me Cheiro's Book of Numbers likhi aur Chaldean table ko English-speaking duniya me popular banaya. Unke pehle bhi ye parampara thi, par unhi ki kitaab ne ise ghar-ghar pahunchaya. Aur Pythagorean side me Pythagoras ke baad L. Dono systems ki jad purani hai, par jo form tum aaj dekhte ho, wo 1900s ke writers ki mehnat hai.

Personal Year Number — 2026 Tumhara Kaunsa Saal Hai?

Mulank aur Bhagyank kabhi nahi badalte. Par ek number hai jo har saal badalta hai — Personal Year. Ye numerology ka timing tool hai: 9 saal ke cycle me tum is samay kahan khade ho, ye batata hai. 1 se shuruaat, 9 par completion, phir dobara 1.

Formula: janm ka mahina + janm ka din + current year — teeno ko single digit tak reduce karo, phir jodo aur dobara reduce karo. Janm ka saal isme use nahi hota — uski jagah current year aata hai. Yahi farak hai Bhagyank se.

2026 khud ek Universal Year 1 hai — 2 + 0 + 2 + 6 = 10, phir 1 + 0 = 1. Isiliye 2026 ke hisaab examples:

  • 14 August ko janme ho: August = 8, din 14 → 1 + 4 = 5, saal 2026 → 1. Total: 8 + 5 + 1 = 14 → 5. Tumhara 2026 Personal Year 5 hai — change aur freedom ka saal, parampara ke hisaab se.
  • 14 March ko janme ho: March = 3, din 14 → 5, saal → 1. Total: 5 + 3 + 1 = 9. Personal Year 9 — completion aur letting go ka saal.
  • 28 November ko janme ho: November = 11 → 2, din 28 → 2 + 8 = 10 → 1, saal → 1. Total: 2 + 1 + 1 = 4. Personal Year 4 — hard work aur foundation ka saal.

Common convention — aur is article me jo use hua hai — hai 1 January, kyunki calculation calendar year se hoti hai. Aur ek aur baat saaf kar doon: Personal Year events predict nahi karta. Ye saal ka theme batata hai — kya karna chahiye, kahan energy lagani chahiye — ye nahi batata ki kya hoga. 5 ka saal hone ka matlab ye nahi ki tumhari naukri badlegi hi — matlab ye hai ki parampara me is saal ko badlav ke liye shaped mana jata hai.

Numerology vs Jyotish — Dono Me Kya Farak Hai?

Ab wo sawal jo har Indian reader ke dimag me aata hai: "Numerology aur Jyotish ek hi hai kya? Dono me graha to ek hi hain."

Jawab hai: nahi, ek nahi hain. Dono me graha ke naam ek hain — Surya, Chandra, Guru, Shani — par dono ke inputs aur methods bilkul alag hain. Aur sabse badi baat: numerology classical Jyotish ka hissa nahi hai. Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra aur doosre standard Jyotish granth rashiyon, bhavon, nakshatron aur dashaon se kaam karte hain — janm ke din ke ankon se nahi. Aaj ki Indian numerology ek mix hai: graha-ank mapping (jo Jyotish se aayi) aur Cheiro ka Chaldean table (jo early 1900s me West se aaya). Dono jude hue hain, par ek nahi hain.

Numerology (Ank Jyotish)Jyotish (Vedic Astrology)
InputSirf janm tareekh + naamJanm tareekh + exact samay + janm ka sheher
MethodAnkon ko jodna aur reduce karnaGrahon ki position, bhav, nakshatra, dasha
Core numbersMulank, Bhagyank, NaamankLagna, Chandra rashi, bhav, dasha
TimingPersonal Year (9-year cycle)Dasha (Vimshottari), gochar

Practical farak ye hai: numerology ke liye janm ka samay nahi chahiye. Jiska janm ka exact samay pata nahi — aur India me aise log bahut hain — wo kundli nahi bana sakte, par apna Mulank aur Bhagyank 2 minute me nikal sakte hain. Isiliye numerology Jyotish se zyada accessible hai. Par iska ulta bhi sach hai: numerology kundli ki gehrai nahi deti. Kundli me 12 bhav, 27 nakshatra, dasha ka poora system hai — numerology me sirf 9 ank hain. Ek shortcut hai, ek poora system hai. Dono ko apni jagah par rakho.

Jyotish ka poora system samajhna ho, to hamara Vedic astrology kya hai guide padho — usme itihaas, 3 shakhaen, sidereal vs tropical aur dasha sab hai. Aur apni kundli padhni ho, to birth chart reading guide hai. Numerology uske baad ek achha companion hai — pehla kadam nahi, saath wala kadam.

Numerology Sikhne ki Best Books — Reading Order

Ab kitaabein. Kyunki Mulank nikalna to 10 minute ka kaam hai — asli seekhna uske baad shuru hota hai. Aur yahan wahi rule hai jo hamare har book guide me hai: pehle ek kitaab, phir doosri. Paanch kitaabein ek saath mat kharido.

1. Joy Woodward — A Beginner's Guide to Numerology (2019, ~170 pages). Pehli kitaab ke liye sabse aasan choice. Chhoti hai, modern language me hai, aur core profile — naam aur janm tareekh se — ke saath Personal Year, Month aur Day cycles bhi sikhati hai. Agar tumhe sirf ek kitaab leni hai, to ye lo. Ye Pythagorean-style system sikhati hai, isiliye iske saath Chaldean table ko mix mat karo — pehle ek system seekho.

2. Cheiro — Cheiro's Book of Numbers (1926). Chaldean parampara ki foundation kitaab. Isiliye doosre number par hai: ye wohi kitaab hai jisne Chaldean table ko modern duniya me popular banaya. Language apne zamane ki hai — thodi heavy lagegi — par iske liye ek fayda hai: copyright khatm ho chuka hai, ye public domain me hai, isiliye iski legitimate free copies public-domain archives par milti hain, aur printed edition bhi bahut sasti milti hai. Indian system samajhna hai, to ye kitaab padhni hi padegi — par modern primer ke baad.

3. David A. Phillips — The Complete Book of Numerology (1980; Hay House reissue 2005). Structured self-study ke liye best. Iska centre hai Lo Shu birth-chart grid — janm tareekh ke ankon ko 3x3 grid me rakh kar padhna — aur "Arrows", grid ki bhari aur khali lines. Phillips ka system Pythagorean hai, aur wo apne metaphysical framework ke saath aata hai — kuch readers ko ye bahut pasand aata hai, kuch ko sirf method chahiye hota hai. Kharidne se pehle ek chapter skim kar lo.

4. Matthew Oliver Goodwin — Numerology: The Complete Guide, Vol. 1. Jab basic calculations haath me aa jayein, to ye kitaab gehrai ke liye hai — personality reading, master numbers aur deeper interpretation. Ye wohi kitaab hai jo beginners ko "reference" stage me le jati hai.

5. Juno Jordan — Numerology: The Romance in Your Name. Pythagorean classic, khaas taur par naam ke number ke liye. Jordan ne 1930s me Western numerology ko popular banaya tha, aur ye kitaab unki sabse famous hai. Naamank ki gehrai me jana ho — compound numbers, naam ke vowels aur consonants — to ye kitaab wahi stage hai.

Ek optional chhatha naam: Faith Javane aur Dusty Bunker — Numerology and the Divine Triangle. Agar tumhe numbers ko Tarot ya deeper symbolism se jodna hai, to ye kitaab us raaste ki hai. Beginner ke liye zaroori nahi, par shelf par rakhne layak hai.

Aur "abhi mat lo" wali baat: ye paanchon kitaabein ek saath mat kharido. Woodward se shuru karo, use khatm karo, apne aur apne parivaar ke ank nikalo, ek mahina practice karo — phir Cheiro uthao. Jo kitaab tum padhoge nahi, wo shelf par sirf dikhawa hai. Aur haan, kitaabon ki keemat edition aur region ke hisaab se badalti rehti hai, isiliye yahan koi price quote nahi kar raha — kharidne se pehle latest price check kar lo.

Ye 5 Galtiyan Mat Karo

Ab wo galtiyan, jo numerology me sabse zyada hoti hain — aur kuch to paise bhi kha jati hain.

Galti 1: Mulank aur Bhagyank ko ek samajhna. Dono ka input alag hai, matlab alag hai. Mulank sirf din se banta hai, Bhagyank poori tithi se. Koi site ya vyakti tumhe sirf ek number dekar "tumhara numerology number" keh de, to poochho — kaunsa? Mulank, Bhagyank ya Naamank? Teenon alag hain, teenon ka apna kaam hai.

Galti 2: Dono systems ko mix karna. Chaldean se Naamank nikala, phir Pythagorean kitaab se uska matlab padha — ye galat hai. ARHAN ka example yaad rakho: Chaldean se 5, Pythagorean se 6. Agar tum system mix karoge, to tum khud ko 5 bhi samjhoge aur 6 bhi — aur dono ka matlab padh kar confuse hoge. Ek system chuno, ek mahina usi par raho.

Galti 3: Master numbers ko Indian system me zabardasti rakhna. Western kitaabein 11 aur 22 ko "master numbers" keh kar rokti hain. Indian practice me aam taur par inhe reduce kar diya jata hai — 29 tareekh ka Mulank 2 hai, 11 nahi. Agar tum Indian guides padh rahe ho, to Western master-number rule wahan mat lagao. System ka rule, usi system ke andar.

Galti 4: Naam badalne ke liye paisa dena. Ye sabse mehngi galti hai. Kuch log "name correction" ke naam par hazaron rupaye lete hain — ek akshar badlo, kismat badal jayegi. Sach ye hai: naam badalne se number badalta hai, zindagi ki guarantee nahi milti. Naam badalna ek legal aur personal decision hai — passport, bank, documents sab badalte hain. Kisi ank ke liye itna bada kadam uthane se pehle do baar socho. Aur jo vyakti tumhe dara kar naam badalne ko kahe, wo numerologist nahi, salesman hai.

Galti 5: Lucky number ko guarantee samajhna. "Tumhara lucky number 5 hai" — ye sun kar lottery ticket lena, ya koi investment karna, ya shaadi ki tareekh usi par rakhna — ye numerology ka galat use hai. Lucky number, rashifal ke lucky number ki tarah, ek halka sa extra hai — mazaak ke liye, reflection ke liye. Us par faisla mat lo. Bade faisle apni planning aur expert ki salah se lo.

Kya Numerology Sach Hai? — Imandaari Se Jawab

Ab wo section, jo is article ko blind-faith guide se alag karta hai. Sawaal seedha hai: kya numerology sach hai?

Seedha jawab: numerology ki predictions ka koi peer-reviewed scientific evidence nahi hai. Ye koi opinion nahi hai — ye tested position hai. Janm tareekh ya naam ki spelling se outcomes badalne ka koi controlled study me proof nahi mila hai. Doosra hai confirmation bias — jo line sahi nikli, wo yaad rehti hai; jo galat nikli, wo bhool jate hain. Teesra ye hai ki numerology ek achha self-reflection ka tool hai — apne number ko dekh kar tum apne baare me sochte ho, apni strengths aur challenges ko naam dete ho. Wo sochna kaam ka hai. Par wo sochna proof nahi hai.

Numerology ek belief framework hai, tested science nahi. Ankon ka hisaab exact hai — jodna-ghatana kabhi galat nahi hota. Par us hisaab ka matlab parampara ka hai, proof ka nahi. Dono ko alag rakho, to dono se fayda milega.

Frequently Asked Questions

Numerology kya hai, ek line me?

Numerology — Hindi me Ank Jyotish — ek paramparik vidya hai jo maanti hai ki tumhari janm tareekh aur tumhare naam ke aksharon se bane ank tumhare swabhav aur jeevan ki disha ke baare me kuch kehte hain. Isme teen mukhya ank hote hain: Mulank (janm ke din se), Bhagyank (poori janm tithi se) aur Naamank (naam ke aksharon se).

Mulank aur Bhagyank me kya farak hai?

Mulank sirf tumhare janm ke DIN se nikalta hai — jaise 23 tareekh ho to 2+3 = 5 — aur tumhare rozmarra ke swabhav ko dikhata hai. Bhagyank poori janm tithi (din + mahina + saal) se nikalta hai — jaise 23 August 1987 ka Bhagyank 2 hai — aur jeevan ki badi disha se joda jata hai. Dono alag ank hain, dono ko ek mat samjho.

Naamank nikalne ke liye kaunsa table use karein — Chaldean ya Pythagorean?

India me aam taur par Chaldean (Cheiro) table use hota hai — isme har akshar ki value 1 se 8 hoti hai aur kisi akshar ko 9 nahi diya jata. Western kitaabein Pythagorean table use karti hain, jisme A=1 se I=9 tak alphabetical order chalta hai. Dono se ek hi naam ka alag number aata hai, isiliye ek system chuno aur ek mahine tak usi par raho — dono ko mix mat karo.

Personal Year number kaise nikale 2026 ke liye?

Apne janm ka mahina, janm ka din aur current year (2026) — teeno ko single digit tak reduce karo, phir jodo aur dobara reduce karo. 2026 khud 1 me reduce hota hai (2+0+2+6 = 10, phir 1). Jaise 14 August ko janme vyakti ka 2026 Personal Year 5 hai (8+5+1 = 14, phir 5). Janm ka saal isme use nahi hota.

Kya numerology Jyotish ka hissa hai?

Nahi, classical Jyotish ka hissa nahi hai. Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra jaise standard Jyotish granth rashiyon, bhavon, nakshatron aur dashaon se kaam karte hain, janm ke din ke ankon se nahi. Aaj ki Indian numerology graha-ank mapping aur Cheiro ke Chaldean table ka mix hai — Jyotish se judi hui, par uska hissa nahi.

Kya numerology se shaadi, career ya paisa ke faisle lene chahiye?

Nahi. Numerology ki predictions ka koi peer-reviewed scientific evidence nahi hai — ye ek belief aur self-reflection ka framework hai, tested science nahi. Ise apne baare me sochne ka ek tool samjho, zindagi ke bade faislon ka rulebook nahi. Shaadi, naukri aur paisa ke faisle apni planning aur expert ki salah se lo, kisi ank par nahi.

Final Words

Numerology seekhne ki sabse badi seekh ye hai: ye ek mirror hai, rulebook nahi. Teen ank — Mulank, Bhagyank, Naamank — tumhe apne baare me sochne ka ek naya tareeka dete hain. Mulank batata hai tum roz kaise react karte ho. Bhagyank batata hai tumhari zindagi kis disha me jhuk rahi hai. Naamank batata hai tumhara naam kis energy se joda jata hai. Teenon ko jod kar dekho, to ek picture banti hai — par wo picture tumhari kismat nahi hai. Wo picture ek sawal hai: "Kya ye mujh par fit baithta hai?"

Aaj ek kaam karo — apne teenon ank nikalo. Kagaz lo, janm tareekh likho, Mulank nikalo. Poori tithi likho, Bhagyank nikalo. Apna naam likho, Chaldean table se Naamank nikalo. Teenon ko ek jagah likh lo. Phir 1-9 wali table se unke graha aur theme padho — aur sirf ek sawaal poochho: "Isme se kya mujh par sach lagta hai, aur kya nahi?" Jo sach lage, use rakho.
