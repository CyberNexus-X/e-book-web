Tarot Card Kya Hai? 78 Cards, Spreads aur Pehli Reading — Hindi Guide

November 27, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: Tarot 78 tasveeron wala ek card deck hai — 22 Major Arcana aur 56 Minor Arcana. Reading me tum ek sawal soch kar kuch cards nikaalte ho, aur un cards ki tasveeron aur paramparik arthon ko apne sawal se jod kar padhte ho. Poora system sirf teen cheezon par khada hai: deck ki structure, ek achha sawal, aur cards ko ek kahani ki tarah padhna. In teenon ko samajh lo, to tum apni pehli reading aaj hi kar sakte ho. Baaki sab inhi ka detail hai.

Ab ek imandari wali baat, article shuru hone se pehle hi — aur ye is article me aakhri baar nahi aayegi. Tarot ke bhavishya wale daavon ka koi controlled study me scientific proof nahi hai. Ye belief framework hai, tested science nahi. Par iska matlab ye nahi ki article bekaar hai. 78 cards ki tasveerein 600 saal se logo ko apni zindagi par sochne par majboor kar rahi hain — ye sanskriti hai, aur ye psychology bhi hai. Is article ko isi angle se padho: ek purani parampara ko samajhne ke liye, apne sawal ko ek naye tareeke se dekhne ke liye, aur apne faislon par sochne ke liye. Kismat ka faisla is par mat chhodo. Aur haan — is article me koi "card kharab hai, isiliye mehenga upay lo" nahi hai. Jo upay hain, wo hain hi nahi — kyunki Tarot me upay ka concept hi nahi hota, sirf samajh hoti hai.

Ye article hamari astrology series ka ek naya hissa hai. Jyotish vyakti ka system hai — tumhari kundli, tumhare grah. Tarot bhi vyakti ka hi system hai — par iske liye tumhari janm tareekh nahi chahiye, na exact samay, na janm ka sheher, na tumhara haath. Sirf ek deck aur ek sawal chahiye. Isi liye ye Jyotish se zyada accessible hai: kundli ke liye exact samay zaroori hai, hast rekha ke liye tumhara haath, tarot ke liye sirf cards. Jyotish samajhna ho to hamara Vedic astrology kya hai guide padho; apni kundli padhni ho to birth chart reading guide hai. Ye article sirf tarot ki baat karta hai.

Tarot Card Kya Hai — Naam se Shuruaat

"Tarot" shabd kahan se aaya, ye bhi ek kahani hai. 15th century ke Italy me is khel ko trionfi kehte the — matlab "triumphs" — aur baad me tarocchi. French me ye tarot ban gaya, aur wahi naam English aur Hindi me chala aaya. Hindi me iska koi classical naam nahi hai — ye Indian vidya nahi hai, ye European hai. Isiliye Hindi guides me isse "Tarot" hi kehte hain, kabhi-kabhi "टैरो कार्ड" likh kar.

Ek confusion yahin clear kar doon — Tarot aur Oracle cards ek nahi hain. Oracle deck kisi bhi theme par, kisi bhi number ke cards ke saath ban sakta hai — 36 cards, 44 cards, 52 cards, jo creator chahe. Tarot ki structure fixed hai: hamesha 78 cards, hamesha 22 Major aur 56 Minor. Agar deck me 78 cards nahi hain, to wo tarot nahi hai. Ye fixed structure hi tarot ko seekhne layak banati hai — ek baar structure samajh li, to duniya ka koi bhi tarot deck padh sakte ho.

Aur ek baat jo beginners ko turant samajhni chahiye: tarot "bhavishya batane" ka tool nahi hai — wo ek reflection tool hai. Cards tumhe wohi dikhate hain jo tumhare sawal me pehle se hai, bas ek naye angle se. Ek achhi reading ke baad tumhe "aisa hoga" nahi lagta — tumhe "achha, is angle se to maine socha hi nahi tha" lagta hai. Ye farak poora article yaad rakhna. Jo reader tumhe "ye hoga, wo hoga" ki guarantee de, wo tarot nahi padh raha — wo tumhe bech raha hai.

Tarot ka Asli Itihaas — Khel se Oracle Tak

Yahan tarot ki sabse interesting baat hai — aur sabse zyada galat samjhi jaane wali bhi. Aaj zyadatar log mante hain ki tarot hazaaron saal purana hai, Egypt ke mandiron se aaya hai, kisi secret priesthood ne banaya tha. Ye kahani bahut romantic hai. Par ye kahani jhoothi hai. Asli history usse zyada interesting hai — kyunki wo documented hai.

Playing cards khud Europe me 1370s ke aas-paas pahunche, sabse zyada sambhavna hai ki Mamluk duniya (Egypt aur Syria) se trade routes ke through. Uske lagbhag 70 saal baad, 1440s me northern Italy — Milan, Ferrara, Bologna ke courts me — kisi ne ordinary 4-suit pack me ek paanchva suit jod diya: illustrated trionfi, yaani trump cards. Ye cards baaki sab cards ko hara dete the, aur khel ko uski structure dete the. Ye ek trick-taking card game tha. Koi fortune-telling nahi, koi mysticism nahi — ek parlor game, Italian aristocrats ke liye.

Sabse purane surviving decks wahi hain jo Milan ki ruling families ke liye hand-painted bane the — Visconti-Sforza decks, lagbhag 1440s–1450s ke. Gold leaf, family emblems, haath se painted figures — ye luxury items the, divination tools nahi. Aur phir lagbhag 350 saal tak — 1440s se late 1700s tak — tarot sirf ek card game raha. Ye baat yaad rakhna: jis cheez ko aaj log "ancient mystical tarot" kehte hain, wo 350 saal tak sirf ek khel tha.

Mod kab aaya? 1781 me. French clergyman aur scholar Antoine Court de Gébelin ne apni encyclopaedia Le Monde primitif ke volume 8 me ek essay likha: tarot ancient Egyptian sacred book hai — Book of Thoth — jo initiated priests ne card game ke bhesh me centuries tak chhupaya tha. Ye claim poori tarah galat tha. Court de Gébelin ne tarot ko pehli baar ek social gathering me dekha tha aur on the spot convince ho gaya tha. Us samay tak Egyptian hieroglyphs decipher bhi nahi hue the — Jean-François Champollion ne Rosetta Stone se ye kaam 1822 me kiya, Court de Gébelin ke claim ke 40 saal baad. Uske paas Egyptian origin ka koi evidence nahi tha — sirf visual similarities aur wishful thinking.

Par galat claim bhi kabhi-kabhi history badal deta hai. Court de Gébelin ke contemporary Jean-Baptiste Alliette — pen-name Etteilla — ne is theory ko business bana diya. 1780s aur 1790s me usne fortune-telling ke liye specially design kiye gaye pehle decks aur printed guides publish kiye. Phir 19th century me French occultists ne tarot ko aur bada system banaya, aur 1888 me London me Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn ne tarot ko Kabbalah, astrology aur alchemy ke saath ek single magical system me jod diya. Golden Dawn ke members — Arthur Edward Waite aur Aleister Crowley — ne modern tarot ko define karne wale decks banaye. Waite ne artist Pamela Colman Smith ko commission kiya, aur 1909 me Rider-Waite-Smith deck publish hua. Is deck ne woh kiya jo kisi deck ne pehle nahi kiya tha: har card — 56 Minor Arcana cards tak — ko ek poori illustrated scene di. Isi liye aaj beginners ke liye ye sabse best deck hai: tasveer dekh kar hi card ka mood samajh aata hai.

To sach ye hai: tarot ki tasveerein 15th century ke Milan ki Christian aur classical picture-world se aati hain, aur unka occult matlab 18th–19th century me baad me joda gaya. Ye sequence samajhna hi sabse useful hai — kyunki isse pata chalta hai ki cards asal me kya hain: ek purana khel, jise baad me ek oracle ki tarah padha jaane laga. Dono layers real hain. Bas unhe confuse mat karo.

78 Cards ki Structure — Deck ka Naksha

Ab deck ka naksha. Ek standard tarot deck me 78 cards hote hain, do groups me. Ye split samajhna beginner ke liye sabse useful cheez hai — kyunki isse 78 alag-alag cards yaad rakhne ka kaam do chhote groups me bat jata hai.

GroupCardsKya dikhata hai
Major Arcana22 (0 se 21 tak)Zindagi ke bade themes aur turning points — shuruaat, pyaar, badlav, ant, poornata
Minor Arcana56 (4 suits × 14)Roz-marra ki zindagi — kaam, rishte, soch, paisa, chhote faisle

Simple rule: Major Arcana aaye, to maamla bada hai. Minor Arcana aaye, to maamla roz ka hai. Ek reading me zyada Major cards ka matlab hai ki sawal tumhari zindagi ke kisi bade mod se juda hai. Zyada Minor cards ka matlab hai ki sawal detail aur daily life ka hai. Dono ka combination hi poori picture hai.

22 Major Arcana — Bade Themes, Ek Yatra

Major Arcana 0 se 21 tak numbered hain. Inhe ek yatra ki tarah padha jata hai — Fool's Journey. The Fool (0) ek masoom ke roop me nikalta hai, aur The World (21) tak pahunch kar poora hota hai. Seekhne ke liye inhe teen groups me baanto:

Cards 0–7: khud ko banana. Pehchan, skills, rishte, pehle faisle. Cards 8–14: khud ko test karna. Inner work, badlav, fairness, surrender, balance. Cards 15–21: poori picture se milna. Andhera, aasha, sach, poornata. Ye teen groups yaad rakh lo, to 22 cards ka order kabhi nahi bhoolega.

Beginner ke liye ye confusion ka koi matlab nahi hai — bas ye jaan lo ki dono numbering systems hain, aur RWS wali aaj standard hai.

Aur haan, sabse famous myth yahin tod doon: Death card ka matlab maut nahi hoti. Parampara me Death transformation ka card hai — kisi cheez ka ant, jo nayi shuruaat ke liye jagah banata hai. Naukri chhodna, rishta khatm hona, purani aadat todna — ye sab Death card ke themes hain. Jo reader Death card dekh kar "maut" bol de, wo reading nahi kar raha — wo dara raha hai.

56 Minor Arcana — Roz ki Zindagi, 4 Suits

Minor Arcana roz-marra ki zindagi hai. Ye 4 suits me batte hain, har suit me 14 cards — Ace se Ten tak, phir chaar court cards: Page, Knight, Queen, King. Playing cards se comparison karo, to ye familiar lagega: har suit Ace se Ten tak jaata hai, aur phir chaar face cards. Bas tarot me har suit ka ek element aur ek life-area hai. Ye chaar lines yaad kar lo, to 56 cards ka mood pehle hi samajh aayega.

SuitElementLife-areaMood
WandsFire (Agni)Energy, ambition, creativity, kaamSpark — cheezein shuru karna
CupsWater (Jal)Emotions, pyaar, rishte, intuitionDil — kya mehsoos ho raha hai
SwordsAir (Vayu)Soch, communication, conflict, sachDimag — kya socha ja raha hai
PentaclesEarth (Prithvi)Paisa, kaam, sehat, gharHaath — kya practical hai

Phir numbers ka pattern. Numbered cards (Ace–Ten) har suit me wahi kahani batate hain, bas area badal jata hai:

Ace = nayi shuruaat, ek beej. Two = choice, partnership, balance. Three = pehli growth, doosron ke saath kaam. Four = stability, rukna, aaram. Five = test, conflict, mushkil. Six = harmony, aage badhna. Seven = assessment, ruk kar sochna. Eight = movement, mehnat. Nine = almost wahan, aakhri stage. Ten = completion — ya overload.

Do example lo, to pattern samajh aayega. Ace of Cups = shuruaat (Ace) + feelings (Cups) = naya pyaar ya khula dil. Five of Pentacles = test (Five) + paisa-suraksha (Pentacles) = ek lean season, paisa tight hona. Isi tarah Eight of Swords = movement (Eight) + soch (Swords) = apne hi doubts me phansa hua mehsoos karna. Ye formula seekh lo — number + suit — to 40 numbered cards ka matlab derive kar sakte ho, bina ratte hue.

Court cards log ya vyavhaar ke tareeke hain. Page = student ya messenger, nayi energy. Knight = action, movement, kabhi-kabhi jaldi. Queen = suit ki quality ko andar se hold karna. King = suit ki quality ko bahar direct karna. Ye beginners ke liye sabse tricky cards hain — isiliye inhe aakhri me seekhna. Pehle Major Arcana, phir suits aur numbers, phir court cards.

Deck Kaise Chunein — Pehla Deck

Ab practical sawal: pehla deck kaunsa lein? Jawab simple hai — Rider-Waite-Smith deck. 1909 wala, Pamela Colman Smith ke illustrations ke saath. Iske teen reasons hain. Pehla: har card par poori scene hai — Minor Arcana cards par bhi — isiliye tasveer dekh kar hi card ka mood samajh aata hai. Doosra: lagbhag har beginner kitaab isi deck par likhi hai, isiliye kitaab aur deck ek doosre se match karte hain. Teesra: aaj ke zyadatar modern decks isi ki visual template par bane hain, isiliye RWS seekh liya to koi bhi deck padh sakte ho.

Deck kharidte waqt in baaton ka dhyaan rakho. Original RWS lo, koi fancy variant nahi — pehle deck ke liye standard version best hai. Card size check karo — bade cards haath me shuffle karna mushkil hota hai, khaaskar chhote haathon ke liye. Guidebook saath me aata hai ya nahi, ye dekho — RWS ke saath chhoti guidebook aati hai, jo shuruaat ke liye kaafi hai. Aur price ki baat: deck ki keemat edition, seller aur region ke hisaab se badalti rehti hai, isiliye yahan koi price quote nahi kar raha — Amazon, Flipkart ya local bookstore par kharidne se pehle latest price check kar lo. Ek baat aur — koi "gifted" deck ya "special energy" wala deck zaroori nahi hai. Ye purani superstition hai ki deck khud kharidna nahi chahiye, gift me milna chahiye. Aisa kuch nahi hai. Apna deck khud chuno, khud kharido.

Deck mil gaya, to use "prepare" karne ka koi secret ritual nahi hai. Bas cards ko shuffle karo, unhe dekho, har card ko ek minute do. Deck ko saaf kapde me ya box me rakho — cards kharab na hon, isiliye. Baaki sab — moonlight me rakhna, salt se cleanse karna, kisi aur ko chhoone na dena — ye sab personal beliefs hain, rules nahi. Jo tumhe achha lage, wo karo. Jo na lage, wo mat karo.

Pehli Reading ka 5-Step Tareeka

Ab asli kaam. Reading karna koi magic nahi hai — ye ek order hai. Ye paanch steps follow karo, to pehli reading aaj hi kar sakte ho.

  1. Ek clear sawal banao. Sawal hi poori reading ko shape deta hai. "Mera future kya hai?" — ye sawal nahi hai, ye ek khaali cheque hai. "Is naukri ke offer ko le kar main kya miss kar raha hoon?" — ye sawal hai. Achhe sawal open hote hain (kyun, kya, kaise), personal hote hain, aur un cheezon par hote hain jo tumhare control me hain. "Wo mujhse pyaar karta hai kya?" ki jagah "Is rishte me main kya samajh nahi pa raha hoon?" poochho. Yes/no sawalon se bacho — tarot reflection ke liye hai, coin toss ke liye nahi.
  2. Shuffle karo, jab tak mann kare. Shuffle karne ka koi "sahi" tareeka nahi hai. Cards ko haath me lekar sawal ko dimag me rakho, aur tab tak shuffle karo jab tak lage ki bas, ab rukna hai. Kuch log cards ko table par phaila kar mix karte hain, kuch haath me shuffle karte hain — dono theek hain. Jo tareeka tumhare haath ko comfortable lage, wahi sahi hai.
  3. Cards nikalo aur spread me rakho. Pehli reading ke liye 3-card spread se shuru karo (aage detail hai). Cards ko left se right rakho. Har position ka ek matlab hai — isiliye position yaad rakho, sirf card nahi.
  4. Cards ko ek kahani ki tarah padho. Ye sabse important step hai, aur beginners yahin galti karte hain — teen cards ko teen alag statements ki tarah padhte hain. Skill hai inhe jodne me. Example: tumne ek atke hue project ke baare me poochha, aur cards aaye Eight of Swords, Ace of Wands, The Chariot. Left se right padho, to kahani ye banti hai: tum apne hi doubts me phanse hue mehsoos kar rahe ho (Eight of Swords), ek fresh spark of energy abhi yahan hai (Ace of Wands), aur agar use pakad lo, to aage ka raasta determined, steady progress hai (The Chariot). Teen cards, ek sentence. Yahi reading hai.
  5. Journal me likh lo. Ek tarot journal rakho — date, sawal, cards, aur tumhari reading. Ye sabse underrated advice hai. Do hafte baad wapas padhoge, to pata chalega ki tumhari reading kitni sahi thi — aur kahan tumne apni hi wishful thinking ko card ka matlab bana diya tha. Journal bina, tarot sirf entertainment hai. Journal ke saath, ye ek practice hai.

Ek aur baat — pehli reading khud ke liye karo, doosron ke liye nahi. Doosron ki reading tab karo jab tumne apne liye 20–30 readings kar li hon aur journal me unka record ho. Doosre ki zindagi ke faislon par bolna zimmedari hai, aur wo zimmedari practice ke bina mat uthao.

3 Spreads — Ek Card se Celtic Cross Tak

Spread ka matlab hai cards ko kis layout me rakhna. Har position ka ek matlab hota hai. Beginners ke liye sirf teen spreads kaafi hain — baaki sab inhi ke variations hain.

1. One-Card Daily Draw — Roz ka Ek Card

Sabse simple spread, aur seekhne ke liye sabse best. Subah uth kar, ek sawal socho ya bas "aaj ka din kaisa rahega?" — aur ek card nikalo. Din bhar us card ko dimag me rakho. Shaam ko journal me likho: card ne aaj kya dikhaya? Ye practice ek hafte me tumhe 7 cards sikha degi, ek mahine me 30. Koi kitaab itni jaldi nahi sikhati. Aur ek baat — daily draw "aaj kya hoga" predict karne ke liye nahi hai. Ye "aaj kis angle se dekhna hai" ke liye hai. The Tower aaya, to din bhar "kya tootta hai" dekhna — ye ek lens hai, ek warning nahi.

2. Three-Card Spread — Sabse Popular Beginner Spread

Teen cards, ek line me. Classic reading hai past – present – future. Doosre useful versions hain: situation – obstacle – advice (haal, rukawat, salah) aur you – the other person – the relationship (tum, doosra vyakti, rishta). Spread choose karte waqt pehle decide kar lo kaunsa version use kar rahe ho — cards nikalne ke baad version badalna cheating hai, khud se bhi.

Three-card spread hi wo jagah hai jahan "cards ko ek kahani ki tarah padhna" seekha jata hai. Past card batata hai kahan se aaye, present batata hai kahan ho, future batata hai kis direction me ja rahe ho. Future card "ye hoga" nahi batata — wo "is direction me ja rahe ho" batata hai. Direction badli ja sakti hai. Yahi farak hai prediction aur reflection me.

3. Celtic Cross — 10 Cards, Poori Picture

Celtic Cross tarot ka sabse famous spread hai — 10 cards, ek bade sawal ke liye. Beginners ko isse shuru nahi karna chahiye, par iska naam aur structure jaanna chahiye, kyunki har serious guide me ye aayega.

1. PresentAbhi ki sthiti — sawal ka core
2. ChallengeJo cross kar raha hai — rukawat ya tension
3. Past (neeche)Jad — kahan se shuru hua
4. Future (upar)Kis taraf ja raha hai
5. Above (crown)Conscious goal — tum kya chahte ho
6. Below (root)Subconscious — andar kya chal raha hai

Celtic Cross ko tab try karo jab tumne 3-card spread me 15–20 readings kar li hon. Is spread ki galti ye hai ki log position 10 ko "final answer" samajh lete hain. Nahi — outcome ka matlab hai "agar sab aise hi chalta raha, to". Tum position 7 (advice) par action le kar outcome badal sakte ho. Yahi to point hai.

Reversed Cards — Ulta Card Aaye to Kya Karein?

Kabhi-kabhi card ulta nikalta hai — tasveer ulta. Ise reversed card kehte hain. Parampara me iska matlab hai card ki energy ka ulta ya blocked ya internal version. Example: upright The Sun = khushi aur clarity; reversed The Sun = khushi delayed ya andar ki taraf, bahar nahi dikh rahi.

Par yahan beginner ke liye sabse imaandaar advice hai: shuruaat me reversals ko skip kar do. Har card ko upright ki tarah padho, chahe wo ulta nikla ho. 78 cards ke upright meanings hi seekhne me mahine lagte hain — unhe double kar ke 156 mat banao. Jab upright meanings me confidence aa jaye, tab reversals add karna. Aur ek baat — kuch readers reversals ko bilkul use nahi karte, aur wo bhi theek hai. Reversal ek option hai, rule nahi. Deck shuffle karte waqt cards ulte ho jayein, to unhe seedha kar ke bhi padh sakte ho. Koi tarot police nahi hai.

Tarot vs Kundli vs Numerology vs Hast Rekha — Farak Samjho

Ab wo sawal jo har Indian reader ke dimag me aata hai: "Tarot, Kundli, Numerology, Hast Rekha — ye sab ek hi hai kya? Sab me grah, rashi, bhav to hain."

Jawab hai: nahi, ek nahi hain. In sab ki cosmology overlap karti hai — grah, tatva, rashi — par har system ka input, method aur kaam alag hai. Tarot in sab se isiliye alag hai kyunki iske liye tumhare baare me koi data nahi chahiye.

Practical farak ye hai: Tarot ke liye tumhare baare me kuch nahi chahiye. Na tareekh, na samay, na naam, na haath. Isiliye ye sabse accessible hai — aur isiliye ye sabse zyada misuse bhi hota hai. Kundli galat samay par galat ho sakti hai; tarot galat sawal par galat ho sakta hai. Har system ki apni jagah hai. Kundli ka poora system samajhna ho, to hamara Vedic astrology kya hai guide padho. Apni kundli padhni ho, to birth chart guide hai. Ankon wali vidya ke liye hamara Numerology guide hai, haath ke liye Hast Rekha guide hai, aur ghar-disha wali vidya ke liye Vastu guide hai.

Tarot Sikhne ki Best Books — Reading Order

Ab kitaabein. Kyunki cards dekhna to 30 minute ka kaam hai — asli seekhna uske baad shuru hota hai. Aur yahan wahi rule hai jo hamare har book guide me hai: pehle ek kitaab, phir doosri. Paanch kitaabein ek saath mat kharido.

1. Dr Ishwarbhai Joshi — "Ancient Indian Tarot Book for beginners (In Hindi)" (Google Play, Sept 2021, 474 pages, Hindi). Pehli kitaab ke liye sabse aasan Hindi choice. Tarot card kya hai, card meanings, spreads, yes/no readings — sab easy Hindi me. Google Play par 4.6★ rating hai. Agar tumhe sirf ek Hindi kitaab leni hai, to ye lo.

2. Dr Bipin Aarya — "Learn Tarot Cards in Hindi" (Amazon.in, Hindi). Doosri Hindi option — Major aur Minor Arcana ko simple aur clear tareeke se samjhata hai, easy spreads aur examples ke saath. Readers kehte hain ki ye "over-the-top dramatic" nahi hai — seedha aur practical hai. Joshi ke baad ya uski jagah, dono me se ek chuno.

3. Swati Khampariya — "टैरो रीडर बने (अचूक महाउपाय के साथ)" (Hindi). 78 cards ke meanings aur interpretation ke saath intuition develop karne par focus. Un logon ke liye jo tarot ko personal guidance aur self-discovery ke angle se seekhna chahte hain.

4. Joan Bunning — "Learning the Tarot" (English). Hindi basics ke baad, English me sabse best course-style kitaab. Ye ek structured course hai — step by step, lesson by lesson — aur iska online version bhi free available hai. Agar tumhe "kitaab padh kar seekhna" pasand hai, to ye tumhari pehli English kitaab honi chahiye.

5. Rachel Pollack — "Seventy-Eight Degrees of Wisdom" (English). Ye woh kitaab hai jise zyadatar readers tarot par likhi best kitaab mante hain. Pollack har card ko ek-ek kar ke padhati hai — Rider-Waite-Smith ki tasveeron ke symbols, patterns, aur phir spreads. Ye depth ke liye hai, speed ke liye nahi. Bunning ke baad ye uthao.

6. Mary K. Greer — "Tarot for Your Self" (English). Ek workbook — likhna, draw karna, cards ke saath experiment karna. Ye kitaab tumhe cards ke matlab ratne ki jagah, unhe apne experience se samajhne ko kehti hai. Isiliye ye tarot journal ke saath sabse achhi chalti hai. Pollack ke saath ya uske baad, practice ke liye ye lo.

7. Reference stage — A.E. Waite — "The Pictorial Key to the Tarot" (1910, English). Ye primary source hai — RWS deck ke creator ki apni guide. Ise authority ki tarah mat padho, source ki tarah padho. Waite 1910 me likh raha tha; uske kuch ideas aaj outdated hain. Isi stage me Benebell Wen — "Holistic Tarot" bhi aata hai — reference ke liye, jab koi specific sawal ho, to isme dhundo.

Aur "abhi mat lo" wali baat: ye saari kitaabein ek saath mat kharido. Joshi se shuru karo, use khatm karo, ek mahina daily draw karo — phir Bunning ya Aarya uthao. Jo kitaab tum padhoge nahi, wo shelf par sirf dikhawa hai. Aur haan, kitaabon ki keemat edition aur region ke hisaab se badalti rehti hai, isiliye yahan koi price quote nahi kar raha — kharidne se pehle latest price check kar lo.

Ye 5 Galtiyan Mat Karo

Ab wo galtiyan, jo Tarot me sabse zyada hoti hain — aur kuch to faisle bhi kharab kar deti hain.

Galti 1: Har sawal ko yes/no bana dena. "Kya mujhe naukri milegi?" — tarot is sawal ka jawab nahi de sakta. Tarot reflection ka tool hai, coin toss nahi. Sawal ko open banao: "Is naukri ke liye main kya miss kar raha hoon?" — iska jawab cards de sakte hain.

Galti 2: Ek card se poora faisla suna dena. "Death card aaya, isiliye sab khatm" — aisa nahi hota. Ek card poori kahani nahi hai. Spread ko poora dekho — cards ka combination, unki positions, unka order. Ek card ko akele padhna, ek page padh kar poori kitaab ka review likhne jaisa hai.

Galti 3: Roz 10 readings karna. Ek hi sawal par baar-baar cards nikalna — jab tak "achha" answer na aaye — ye reading nahi, khud ko dhokha dena hai. Ek sawal, ek reading, phir journal. Agli reading tab karo jab situation badle ya naya sawal ho. Cards ko slot machine ki tarah mat chalao.

Galti 4: Doosron ki zindagi par bina practice ke bolna. "Tumhari shaadi nahi hogi" ya "ye rishta toot jayega" — aisi baatein doosre ki zindagi badal sakti hain. Apne liye 20–30 readings aur journal record ke bina, doosron ki reading mat karo. Aur kabhi bhi doosre ki sehat, shaadi ya paisa par definitive prediction mat do.

Galti 5: Darr bechne wale reader ko paisa dena. Ye sabse mehngi galti hai. Kuch log "card kharab hai" dikha kar hazaron rupaye ke upay, yantra aur "correction" bechte hain — aur dara kar bechte hain. Sach ye hai: Tarot me upay ka concept hi nahi hota. Ye ek reading system hai, remedy system nahi. Jo vyakti tumhe dara kar mehenga upay beche, wo reader nahi, salesman hai. Aur kabhi bhi — kabhi bhi — shaadi, career, paisa ya sehat jaise faisle ek tarot reading par akele mat lo.

Kya Tarot Sach Hai? — Imandaari Se Jawab

Ab wo section, jo is article ko blind-faith guide se alag karta hai. Sawaal seedha hai: kya Tarot sach hai?

Seedha jawab: tarot ke bhavishya aur personality wale daavon ka koi controlled study me proof nahi mila hai. Ye koi opinion nahi hai — ye tested position hai. Koi controlled study nahi dikha payi hai ki tarot cards future events ya personality ko reliably predict kar sakte hain. Astrology par bhi yahi baat lagu hoti hai — uski detail hamare Vedic astrology kya hai article me hai, Shawn Carlson ke 1985 ke double-blind study ke saath, jisme expert astrologers charts ko personality profiles se sirf chance ke barabar match kar paye.

To phir Tarot kabhi-kabhi sahi kyun lagta hai? Iske peeche wahi psychology hai jo rashifal aur hast rekha ke peeche hai. Sabse bada factor hai Barnum effect — jab koi baat itni general ho ki har koi use apne par fit kar le. "The Star batata hai ki tumhare andar umeed hai" — ye kisko fit nahi hoga? Is effect ko pehli baar 1949 me psychologist Bertram Forer ne experiment se dikhaya tha: usne students ko ek generic personality sketch diya aur poochha ki kitna accurate hai — students ne average 4.26 out of 5 rating di, jabki sab ko wahi ek sketch mila tha. Yehi wajah hai ki generic card meanings hamesha "sahi" lagti hain. Doosra factor hai confirmation bias — jo reading sahi lagi, wo yaad rehti hai; jo galat nikli, wo bhool jate hain. Teesra hai illusory correlation — dimag pattern dhoondhta hai, aur galat pattern dekhna sasta hai, sahi pattern miss karna mehenga.

Tarot ek belief framework hai, tested science nahi. Cards real hain — 78 tasveerein, 600 saal ki history. Par un cards ka matlab parampara ka hai, proof ka nahi. Dono ko alag rakho, to dono se fayda milega.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki Tarot bekaar hai. Iska matlab ye hai ki ise sahi jagah par rakho: apne sawal par sochne ka ek framework, ek purani parampara ka hissa, ek conversation starter. Cards tumhe wohi dikhate hain jo tumhare sawal me pehle se hai — bas ek naye angle se. Ye kam nahi hai. Kabhi-kabhi ek naya angle hi sabse zyada keemti cheez hoti hai. Usse zyada nahi, usse kam bhi nahi. Aur kabhi bhi — kabhi bhi — Tarot ke basis par shaadi, career, paisa ya sehat jaise faisle akele is par mat lo. Koi tumhe card dikha kar dara raha ho aur mehenga upay, yantra ya "correction" bech raha ho, to wahan ruk jao. Darr bechna Tarot nahi hai, business hai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Tarot card kya hai, ek line me?

Tarot 78 tasveeron wala ek card deck hai — 22 Major Arcana aur 56 Minor Arcana. Reading me kuch cards nikaal kar unki tasveeron aur paramparik arthon ko apne sawal se joda jata hai. Ye sochne aur samajhne ka ek framework hai, bhavishya batane wali tested science nahi.

Tarot sikhne ke liye pehla deck kaunsa lena chahiye?

Rider-Waite-Smith deck — 1909 me publish hua, Pamela Colman Smith ne illustrate kiya tha. Iske har card par poori scene wali tasveer hai, isiliye beginners ko yaad rakhna aasan hota hai, aur lagbhag har beginner kitaab isi deck par likhi hai.

Kya tarot card bhavishya bata sakta hai?

Nahi — iska koi controlled study me proof nahi hai. Tarot reading reflection ka kaam karti hai: cards tumhare sawal ko ek naye angle se dekhne me madad karte hain. Koi bhi card shaadi, career, paisa ya sehat ka faisla nahi kar sakta, aur aisi reading par kabhi akela faisla mat lo.

Tarot aur Kundli me kya farak hai?

Kundli ko janm tareekh, exact samay aur janm ka sheher chahiye aur wo grahon ki position se kaam karti hai. Tarot ko kuch nahi chahiye — na tareekh, na samay — sirf ek deck aur ek sawal. Kundli timing (dasha, gochar) batati hai, tarot kisi sawal par reflection deta hai.

Celtic Cross spread kya hai?

Celtic Cross tarot ka sabse famous 10-card spread hai: pehle 6 cards haal, challenge, past, future, upar aur neeche ki sthiti batate hain, aur aakhri 4 cards advice, bahari asar, ummeed/darr aur outcome dikhate hain. Beginners ke liye 1-card aur 3-card spread se shuru karna behtar hai.

Kya tarot card sikhne ke liye Hindi kitaab hai?

Haan — Dr Ishwarbhai Joshi ki "Ancient Indian Tarot Book for beginners (In Hindi)" (Google Play, 474 pages), Dr Bipin Aarya ki "Learn Tarot Cards in Hindi" (Amazon.in), aur Swati Khampariya ki "Tarot Reader Bane" Hindi me available hain. English depth ke liye Rachel Pollack ki "Seventy-Eight Degrees of Wisdom" aur Mary K. Greer ki "Tarot for Your Self" standard hain.

Final Words

Tarot seekhne ki sabse badi seekh ye hai: ye ek mirror hai, rulebook nahi. 78 cards, 4 suits, 22 bade themes — ye tumhe apne sawal ko dekhne ka ek naya tareeka dete hain. The Fool batata hai shuruaat kaisi hoti hai. The Tower batata hai ki jhoothi structure girti hai. The Star batata hai ki umeed kahan se aati hai. In sab ko jod kar dekho, to ek kahani banti hai — par wo kahani tumhari kismat nahi hai. Wo kahani ek sawal hai: "Main is situation ko kis angle se dekh raha hoon, aur kaunsa angle maine abhi tak try nahi kiya?"

Aaj ek kaam karo — ek sawal socho, jo tumhe sach me pareshan kar raha ho. Use open banao: "main kya miss kar raha hoon?" Phir ek card nikalo — koi deck nahi hai, to online free deck se bhi chalega. Card ko dekho, uski tasveer ko dekho, aur journal me likh lo: "Ye card mere sawal ke baare me kya kehta hai?" Phir sirf ek sawaal poochho: "Ye jawab mujhe pehle se pata tha, ya isne mujhe kuch naya dikhaya?" Agar jawab naya hai, to tumne Tarot ko sahi jagah par rakha hai — ek mirror ki tarah. Aur agar koi tumhe card dikha kar dara kar koi mehenga upay, yantra ya "correction" bechne ki koshish kare, to ye article yaad rakho: Tarot me upay hota hi nahi, darr bechna business hai, aur tumhara sawal tumhara hai.
