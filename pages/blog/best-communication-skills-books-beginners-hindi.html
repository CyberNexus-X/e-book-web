Communication Skills Books for Beginners in Hindi – Kaunsi Book, Kab, Aur Kyun

November 4, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: Agar tumhe sirf ek communication skills book padhni hai, to Dale Carnegie ki How to Win Friends and Influence People padho. Hindi me iska paperback milta hai, aur iska idea ek line ka hai: logon ko jeetne ke liye pehle unme sachhi dilchaspi lo. Unka naam yaad rakho, unki baat dhyan se suno, aur unhe ye ehsaas dilao ki wo zaroori hain. Baaki sab — presentation, negotiation, mushkil baat-cheet — uske baad aata hai. Aur agar tumhari sabse badi dikkat ye hai ki bolte waqt sahi shabd nahi milte, to Norman Lewis ki Word Power Made Easy se shuru karo. Wo shabd sikhati hai, aur shabd hi tumhari baat ki buniyad hain.

Honestly, communication ki kitaabon ki dikkat yehi hai. Har list me wahi 20 naam hote hain, par koi ye nahi batata ki pehle kaunsi, kis ke liye, aur kaunsi abhi mat uthao. Ek list tumhe sirf confidence ke naare de degi, doosri tumhe 92 tricks ka dher pakda degi bina ye bataye ki roz kaunsi ek trick aazmani hai. Dono adhoore hain. Is article me sirf 7 books hain. Har ek ek alag kaam karti hai — logon se judna, shabd banana, chhoti baat-cheet, mushkil baat-cheet, negotiation, aur apna idea yaadgar banana. Aur har ek ke saath ye bhi likha hai ki ye kis ke liye nahi hai.

Pehle Ye Samajh Lo: Communication Ka Matlab Zyada Bolna Nahi Hai

Ek galatfehmi pehle hi saaf kar dete hain. Achha communicator wo nahi jo sabse zyada bolta hai. Wo hai jo sabse saaf sunta hai, aur sabse saaf bolta hai. Ek meeting me tumne dekha hoga — ek insaan 20 minute bolta hai, par koi uski baat yaad nahi rakhta. Doosra insaan 2 minute bolta hai, par uski baat sabko samajh aa jaati hai. Farq awaaz ka nahi hai. Farq ye hai ki doosre insaan ne pehle suna, phir socha, phir bola.

Communication ke chaar hisse hain, aur har book is list me ek hisse par kaam karti hai. Pehla: logon se judna — pehli mulaqat me bharosa banana. Doosra: shabd — sahi shabd, sahi waqt par. Teesra: sunna — saamne wale ki baat ko poora sunna, beech me kaatna nahi. Chautha: mushkil baat — jab baat bighad rahi ho, tab bhi baat ko rasta dikhana. Tumhe chaaron chahiye, par ek saath nahi. Pehle ek, phir doosra. Isi order me ye list banayi gayi hai.

Ek baat aur. Communication seekhna koi jaadu nahi hai. Ek book padh lene se tum agle din stage par nahi chadh jaoge. Ye ek skill hai, jaise cycle chalana. Pehle ladkhadahat hogi. Phir dheere-dheere balance aayega. Jo book ye dawa kare ki usko padhte hi tum "kisi se bhi kuch bhi manwa loge", us book se door raho. Wo skill nahi, manipulation bech rahi hai.

7 Books, 7 Alag Kaam

Neeche har book ke saath teen cheezein milengi: ye book kya sikhati hai, ye kis ke liye hai, aur isme dhyan rakhne wali baat kya hai. List ko upar se neeche tak padhna zaroori nahi. Apni zaroorat wali book chuno, wahi se shuru karo.

1. How to Win Friends and Influence People — Dale Carnegie (1936)

Ye book 1936 me aayi thi, aur aaj bhi isliye chalti hai kyunki iska subject nahi badla: insaan. Carnegie kehte hain ki logon ko apne baare me baat karna achha lagta hai, apna naam sunna achha lagta hai, aur ye mehsoos karna achha lagta hai ki saamne wala sach me sun raha hai. Isliye is book ka pehla rule ye nahi hai ki "achha bolo". Pehla rule ye hai ki "sachhi dilchaspi lo".

Isme teen aadat sabse kaam ki hain. Pehli: kisi ka naam yaad rakhna, aur usko naam se bulana. Ye chhoti baat lagti hai, par kisi ko apna naam kisi aur ke muh se sunna hamesha achha lagta hai. Doosri: saamne wale ko apne baare me bolne do, aur tum suno — beech me apni kahani mat ghusao. Teesri: kisi ki galti par seedha hamla mat karo. Pehle uski achhi baat kaho, phir dheere se apni baat rakho. Ye chaaplusi nahi hai. Ye tareeka hai baat ko aage badhane ka, bina rishte ko tode.

Kis ke liye? Un logon ke liye jo logon se milne me jhijhakte hain. Student ho, job karte ho, ya apna kaam chalate ho — agar tumhe lagta hai ki log tumhari baat ko seriously nahi lete, to ye book tumhari pehli book hai.

Dhyan rakhne wali baat: Is book ka Hindi paperback Amazon.in par listed hai, par uske anuvaad par buyers ki mili-juli rai hai — kuch ko achha laga, kuch ko kamzor. Isliye khareedne se pehle edition aur publisher check karo. Aur ek aur baat: is book ke rules ko natak mat banao. Agar tum sirf faida uthane ke liye dilchaspi dikhaoge, to log bahut jaldi pehchan lenge. Dilchaspi sachhi honi chahiye, warna ye book ulta asar karegi.

2. Word Power Made Easy — Norman Lewis (1949)

Ab ek alag dikkat. Tumhare dimaag me baat saaf hai, par muh se nikalte waqt shabd atak jaate hain. Ya tum wahi 50 shabd baar-baar istemal karte ho, aur tumhari baat wahi ghumti rehti hai. Lewis ki book isi dikkat ka ilaaj hai. Ye book tumhe shabd ratwati nahi. Ye tumhe shabd banane ka tareeka sikhati hai — word roots se. Jab tumhe pata chal jaata hai ki ek root ka matlab kya hai, to us root se bane 10 shabd apne aap samajh aane lagte hain.

Is book ki khaasiyat ye hai ki ye sessions me bati hai. Har session me pehle ek test, phir shabd, phir unka istemal. Tum isko novel ki tarah ek baithak me padh kar khatam nahi kar sakte — aur karna bhi nahi chahiye. Roz ek session. Us session ke shabd din bhar apni baat-cheet me istemal karo. Jo shabd tumne bola nahi, wo tumhara hua nahi.

Kis ke liye best hai? Students ke liye, job interview ki taiyaari karne walon ke liye, aur un logon ke liye jo English me bolte waqt atak jaate hain aur wahi ghise-pite shabd dohrate hain.

Ek imaandaar baat: Ye ek English book hai. Iska Hindi edition hone ka dawa is article me nahi kiya ja raha. Par iski English seedhi hai, aur ye book India me aasani se listed hai. Agar English padhna tumhare liye bilkul mushkil hai, to pehle Carnegie se shuru karo, phir is par aao — jab tumhe roz English ke 15-20 minute padhne ki aadat ho jaaye.

3. How to Talk to Anyone — Leil Lowndes

Ye book pehli mulaqat aur chhoti baat-cheet ki book hai. Lowndes kehti hain ki log tumhe pehle 10 second me hi judge kar lete hain — tumhare chehre, tumhari body language, tumhari awaaz se. Isliye is book ki shuruaat shabdon se nahi, chehre se hoti hai. Jaise "Flooding Smile": milte hi turant muskurao mat. Ek second saamne wale ko dekho, phir dheere se muskurahat aane do. Turant wali muskurahat sabko milti hai. Ruk kar aayi muskurahat khaas lagti hai.

Phir aati hai chhoti baat-cheet — small talk. Bahut log small talk ko bekaar samajhte hain. Lowndes kehti hain ki small talk bekaar nahi hai, wo bharose ki seedhi hai. Tum seedha kisi se uski salary ya uske plan ke baare me nahi pooch sakte. Pehle mausam, jagah, ya saamne wale ki kisi khaas baat par baat karo. Aur sawaal aise poochho jinka jawab sirf "haan" ya "naa" me na ho. "Film kaisi lagi?" ki jagah "Film ka kaunsa scene tumhe sabse zyada yaad rahega?" — ye ek badlav poori baat-cheet ki shakal badal deta hai.

Kis ke liye? Un logon ke liye jo naye logon se milne me atak jaate hain. Party me, office me, ya kisi naye group me — agar tumhe shuru ke 2 minute sabse mushkil lagte hain, to ye book tumhare liye hai.

Dhyan rakhne wali baat: Isme 92 tricks hain. 92 yaad rakhne ki koshish mat karo. Sirf 3 chuno: ek muskurahat wali, ek sawaal wali, ek sunne wali. Un teen ko ek hafte tak roz aazmao. Jab wo aadat ban jaayen, tab agli teen. Jo book ko trick ki list samajh kar padhega, wo kuch nahi seekhega. Jo ek trick ko aadat banayega, wo bahut kuch seekh lega.

4. Crucial Conversations — Patterson, Grenny, McMillan, Switzler (2002)

Ab mushkil waali baat. Jab daanv par bahut kuch laga ho, rai alag ho, aur jazbaat tez hon — tab baat-cheet sabse zyada bigadti hai. Boss se salary ki baat, ghar par kisi badi baat par ikhtilaaf, team me kisi ki galti par baat — ye sab crucial conversations hain. Aur inme hum aksar do galtiyan karte hain: ya to chup ho jaate hain, ya phat padte hain. Is book ka kehna hai ki teesra rasta hai: baat-cheet ko jaari rakhna, bina rishte ko tode.

Iske do tools sabse kaam ke hain. Pehla: STATE. Apne facts share karo, phir apni story batao, phir doosre ka rasta poochho, dheere aur aazmaish ke saath bolo. Matlab, "tum hamesha late aate ho" kehne ki jagah "pichhle hafte meeting 3 baar late shuru hui, aur mujhe laga ki hamara waqt seriously nahi liya ja raha — tumhara nazariya kya hai?" — ye wahi baat hai, par isme hamla nahi hai, isliye saamne wala sun sakta hai. Doosra: AMPP se sunna — Ask, Mirror, Paraphrase, Prime. Saamne wale ki baat ko uske shabdon me wapas do, taaki use lage ki tumne suna hai, sirf apni baari ka intezaar nahi kiya.

Ek chhota sa test: agar kisi baat-cheet ke baad tumhe lagta hai ki tumne "jeet" liya, par saamne wala chup ho gaya, to tumne jeeta nahi hai. Tumne sirf uski asli baat ko dabaa diya hai. Aur dabi hui baat baad me badi keemat par wapas aati hai.

Kis ke liye nahi? Un logon ke liye nahi jo abhi logon se milne me hi jhijhakte hain. Pehle Carnegie aur Lowndes se aam baat-cheet ki aadat banao, phir is book se mushkil baat-cheet sambhalo. Ulta order me tum mushkil baat ki technique to seekh loge, par aam baat me hi atke rahoge.

5. Never Split the Difference — Chris Voss aur Tahl Raz (2016)

Voss FBI ke lead international kidnapping negotiator rahe hain. Unki book ka kehna hai ki negotiation sirf logic ka khel nahi hai. Log jazbaat se faisle lete hain, phir unko logic se justify karte hain. Isliye jo insaan sirf numbers aur logic par baat karta hai, wo aksar haar jaata hai — kyunki saamne wala insaan hai, calculator nahi.

Iske teen tools rozmarra ki zindagi me turant kaam aate hain. Pehla: mirroring — saamne wale ke aakhri 1-3 shabd wapas do, sawaal wali awaaz me, phir chup raho. "Timing thoda mushkil hai" par "mushkil hai?" kehna, aur rukna — aksar saamne wala khud asli dikkat bata deta hai. Doosra: labelling — "It seems like…" ya "Lagta hai ki…" se saamne wale ke jazbaat ka naam lo. Naam liya hua jazbaat halka pad jaata hai, aur nazarandaaz kiya hua badhta hai. Teesra: calibrated sawaal — "How" aur "What" se shuru hone wale khule sawaal, jaise "Is problem ka hal aapke hisaab se kaise nikal sakta hai?" — ye saamne wale ko problem ka hissa bana deta hai, dushman nahi.

Kis ke liye best hai? Salary ki baat, fees ki baat, client se deal, ya ghar par kisi bade faisle ki baat — jahan tumhe "aadha-aadha" samjhauta nahi, ek achha natija chahiye. Ye book khaas kar un logon ke liye hai jo negotiation ko jhagda samajh kar usse bhaagte hain.

Dhyan rakhne wali baat: In techniques ko chaalaki mat banao. Agar tum sirf isliye sun rahe ho taaki saamne wale ko apni baat manwa sako, to wo sunna nahi, jaal hai. Aur jaal ek baar pakda gaya, to bharosa dobara nahi banta. Tactical empathy ka matlab hai saamne wale ko samajhna, usko istemal karna nahi.

6. Nonviolent Communication — Marshall B. Rosenberg

Ye list ki sabse narm, aur shayad sabse mushkil, book hai. Rosenberg ka kehna hai ki hum aksar apni baat me faisla mila dete hain. "Tum kabhi meri baat nahi sunte" — ye observation nahi hai, ye ilzaam hai. Observation ye hai: "Pichhli 3 baar jab maine apni baat shuru ki, tumne beech me kaat diya." Pehla jumla ladai shuru karta hai. Doosra jumla baat-cheet shuru karta hai.

Is book ka tareeka chaar kadam ka hai: pehle bina faisle ke dekho kya hua, phir apna feeling batao, phir apni need batao, phir ek saaf request karo. "Mujhe bura lagta hai jab meri baat beech me kat jaati hai, kyunki mujhe lagta hai ki meri baat zaroori nahi samjhi gayi. Kya tum is baar meri poori baat sun sakte ho?" — isme na ilzaam hai, na dhamki. Isliye isko sunna aasaan hai.

Kis ke liye? Ghar aur rishton ki baat-cheet ke liye, aur un logon ke liye jo kehte to bahut kuch hain, par unki baat hamesha jhagde me badal jaati hai. Agar tumhari dikkat ye hai ki tum sahi baat galat tareeke se keh dete ho, to ye book tumhare liye hai.

7. Made to Stick — Chip Heath aur Dan Heath

Aakhri book ek alag sawaal poochhti hai. Tumne achhi baat kah di, par kya log usko yaad rakhenge? Heath brothers kehte hain ki yaad rehne wale ideas me kuch khaas baatein hoti hain: wo simple hote hain, concrete hote hain, aur unme ek kahani hoti hai. "Hamari company customer ko value deti hai" — ye koi yaad nahi rakhta. "Ek customer ne hamare product se apna 3 ghante ka kaam 20 minute me kar liya" — ye yaad rehta hai. Farq idea ka nahi hai. Farq ye hai ki doosra idea ek tasveer banata hai.

Isliye ye book un logon ke liye hai jo apna idea rakhte hain — teacher, presenter, team lead, ya apna kaam dikhane wala koi bhi insaan. Tumhari baat me ek example hona chahiye, ek kahani honi chahiye, aur ek line aisi honi chahiye jo sunne wale ke dimaag me atak jaaye.

Kis ke liye nahi? Ye pehli book nahi hai. Pehle Carnegie se judna seekh lo, phir is book se apna idea chipkana seekh lo. Ulta order me tum ek achha idea to bana loge, par usko sunne wala koi nahi hoga.

Ye Books Abhi Mat Lo — Aur Kyun

Ab teen galtiyan jo har beginner karta hai, aur jinse ye list tumhe bachana chahti hai.

Pehli: ek saath saari books. 7 books khareed kar shelf par sajana padhna nahi kehlata. Ek book lo, uski ek aadat ek hafte tak chalao, phir agli. Communication ki book ka test ye nahi hai ki tumne kitne page padhe. Test ye hai ki tumne kitni baar kisi se behtar baat ki.

Doosri: trick ko jaadoo samajhna. Flooding Smile, mirroring, labelling — ye sab tools hain, jaadoo nahi. Agar tum inko sirf isliye istemal karoge taaki log tumhari baat maan len, to tum communicator nahi, manipulator ban jaoge. Aur log manipulator ko bahut jaldi pehchan lete hain. Technique tabhi kaam karti hai jab uske peeche sachhi dilchaspi ho.

Teesri: sirf padhna, practice nahi karna. Communication ki book ko padh kar rakh dena, gym ki membership le kar kabhi na jaane jaisa hai. Har chapter ke baad ek asli baat-cheet me us idea ko aazmao. Ek dukaan par, ek dost se, ek meeting me. Chhoti baat-cheet se shuru karo. Badi baat-cheet apne aap aasaan ho jaayegi.

Hindi Me Communication Books Kaise Padhein — Seedha Rasta

Ab wo sawaal jo sabse zyada poochha jaata hai: ye books Hindi me milengi?

Imaandaar jawab ye hai: is list me ek pakka Hindi naam hai. Dale Carnegie ki How to Win Friends and Influence People ka Hindi paperback listed hai, par uske anuvaad par mili-juli rai hai — khareedne se pehle edition aur publisher check karo. Baaki books ke liye — Word Power Made Easy, How to Talk to Anyone, Crucial Conversations, Never Split the Difference, Nonviolent Communication, Made to Stick — khareedne se pehle edition, publisher aur anuvaadak ka naam zaroor check karo. Koi list ye dawa kar ke banana galat hoga ki saari 7 books Hindi me uplabdh hain.

To Hindi reader kya kare? Ek rasta ye hai. Book ko English me dheere padho — roz 10-15 page, jaldi ke bina. Har chapter ke baad uska idea apne shabdon me Hindi me likh lo. Phir us idea ko usi din kisi se baat karte waqt aazmao. Jaise Carnegie ka naam yaad rakhne wala idea tum Hindi me hi to aazmaoge. Book English me hai, par uska kaam tumhari bhasha me hi hoga.

Aur agar English bilkul mushkil lagti hai, to pehle Hindi me us book ke basic summary aur video se idea samjho, phir original book par aao. Ulta rasta — sirf summary padh kar book padhne ka dawa karna — lambi race me kaam nahi aata. Summary darwaza hai, ghar nahi.

4 Hafton Ka Plan — Padhna + Bolna

Book khareedna aasaan hai. Us par amal karna mushkil hai. Isliye ek chhota plan:

  1. Hafta 1 — Judna: Carnegie padho. Roz ek naye ya purane insaan se baat me uska naam lo, aur uski baat beech me kaate bina poori suno. Din ke aakhir me likho: aaj kis se baat achhi hui, aur kyun.
  2. Hafta 2 — Shabd + chhoti baat: Word Power Made Easy ka roz ek session karo, aur Lowndes ki 3 tricks chuno — ek muskurahat wali, ek sawaal wali, ek sunne wali. Roz ek chhoti baat-cheet me unko aazmao.
  3. Hafta 3 — Mushkil baat: Crucial Conversations padho. Is hafte ek aisi baat ko taalna band karo jo tum taal rahe the — chhoti wali se shuru karo. STATE wale tareeke se apni baat rakho, aur AMPP se doosre ki baat suno.
  4. Hafta 4 — Negotiation + idea: Voss ki mirroring aur labelling ko ek asli baat-cheet me aazmao — fees, salary, ya kisi deal par. Aur Made to Stick ke hisaab se apna ek idea ek kahani aur ek example ke saath kisi ko samjhao. Agar wo tumhari baat agle din bhi yaad rakhe, to tumhara idea chipak gaya.

Baaki book — Nonviolent Communication — uske baad, jab tumhe apni baat-cheet ka experience ho jaaye. Kyunki us waqt tumhe pata hoga ki tumhari baat kahan bigadti hai, aur ye book tumhe wahi jagah sudhaarne me madad karegi, sirf theory nahi lagegi.

Kya Sirf Books Kaafi Hain? Nahi.

Ye article ka sabse zaroori hissa shayad yehi hai, isliye isko seedha keh raha hoon. Books tumhe tareeka dengi, par wo tumhari jagah baat nahi kar sakti. Baat tumhe khud hi karni hogi. Aur agar tumhari dikkat sirf skill nahi hai — jaise bolne me bahut zyada ghabrahat, ya kisi baat ka darr jo roz tumhe rokta hai — to book padhna pehla kadam ho sakta hai, par aakhri kadam nahi. Kisi bharosemand insaan se baat karna, ya zaroorat ho to kisi professional se madad lena — ye bhi communication seekhne ka hissa hai, usse bahar nahi.

Aur ek baat. Communication seekh kar kuch log har baat-cheet ko ek test bana lete hain — har smile ka hisaab, har shabd ka hisaab. Mat karo. Ye kitaabein pehle tumhari baat-cheet ko aasaan banane ke liye hain, tumhari zindagi ko stage banane ke liye nahi. Jo insaan doosre ko sach me sunta hai, wo apne aap behtar bolne lagta hai. Ulta rasta sirf thakan deta hai.

Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

Communication skills seekhne ke liye sabse pehli book kaunsi padhni chahiye?

Agar sirf ek book padhni hai to Dale Carnegie ki How to Win Friends and Influence People se shuru karo — iska Hindi paperback listed hai, par edition aur anuvaadak check karke khareedo. Agar tumhari sabse badi dikkat shabd hain, to Norman Lewis ki Word Power Made Easy se shuru karo.

Communication skills ki best book in Hindi kaunsi hai?

Poori Hindi me uplabdh hone ke sabse pakke naam ke liye Carnegie ki book sabse seedha jawab hai — par uske anuvaad par mili-juli rai hai, isliye edition check karna zaroori hai. Baaki books ke liye khareedne se pehle edition aur anuvaadak check karo — har book ka bharosemand Hindi edition aasani se nahi milta.

Students ke liye communication skills books in Hindi me kaunsi sabse kaam ki hai?

Students ke liye Carnegie (logon se judne ke liye), Word Power Made Easy (shabd ke liye) aur How to Talk to Anyone (pehli mulaqat aur small talk ke liye) — ye teen sabse kaam ki hain. Inme se kisi ek se shuru karo, teenon ek saath mat uthao.

Public speaking books for beginners in Hindi me kaunsi book se shuru karein?

Stage par bolne se pehle logon se baat karna seekhna zaroori hai, isliye Carnegie aur How to Talk to Anyone se shuru karo. Phir Made to Stick se apna idea yaadgar banana seekho. Seedha stage ki book uthane se pehle ye buniyad bana lo, warna tum achhi takneek ke saath bhi khaali baat doge.

Kya sirf communication book padh lene se bolna achha ho jayega?

Nahi. Book sirf tareeka batati hai, baat tumhe khud hi karni hogi. Ek book padho, uski ek trick ek hafte tak roz aazmao, phir agli book uthao. Sirf padh kar shelf par sajana bolna seekhna nahi hai.

Ek saath saari books khareedni chahiye ya ek-ek karke?

Ek-ek karke. Pehle ek book padho, uska ek idea ek hafte tak aazmao, phir agli uthao. Saari khareed kar shelf par sajana padhna nahi kehlata — aur ye galti communication ki kitaabon ke saath sabse zyada hoti hai, kyunki har book dekhne me aasaan lagti hai.

Aaj Se Shuru Karo

To aaj ka kaam bahut chhota hai. Aaj kisi ek insaan se baat karte waqt uska naam lo, aur uski baat beech me kaate bina poori suno. Bas. Na koi trick, na koi lambi taiyaari. Kal phir wahi karo, kisi aur insaan se. Ek hafte tak ye ek aadat chalao. Bas itna hi kaafi hai shuruaat ke liye.
