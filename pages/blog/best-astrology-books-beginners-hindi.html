Best Astrology Books for Beginners in Hindi (2026): Jyotish Sikhne Ki Complete Guide

November 1, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: Jyotish sikhne ki best book ek nahi hai, ek order hai. Pehle B.V. Raman ki Astrology for Beginners se buniyad banao, uske saath Light on Life padho, phir K.S. Charak ki Elements of Vedic Astrology se kundli practice karo, aur tab ja kar Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra uthao. Seedha granth se shuru karne wale aksar do hafte me haar maan lete hain. Granth galat nahi hai. Order galat hai.

Honestly, jyotish ki kitaabon ki dikkat yehi hai: har list me sirf naam hote hain, ya ek book ko "best" bata diya jaata hai. Koi ye nahi batata ki beginner ko pehle kya chahiye, Hindi reader ka rasta kya hai, aur kaunsi book abhi mat uthao. Is article me books level ke hisaab se hain, aur har book ke saath ye bhi likha hai ki wo kis kaam ki hai — aur kis kaam ki nahi.

Ek baat shuru me hi saaf kar dete hain. Jyotish ek paramparik adhyayan pranali hai: isse grah, rashi, bhav aur dasha ka dhancha samajh sakte ho, apne faislon par chintan kar sakte ho. Par koi book, koi upay aur koi ratna future ki guarantee nahi de sakta. Jo list ye dawa kare, usse door raho.

Books on Jyotish: Pehle Ye Samajh Lo Ki Tum Kya Seekh Rahe Ho

Jyotish sikhna matlab sirf apni rashi padhna nahi hai. Asli kaam ye hai: grah kahan hai, kis rashi me hai, kis bhav me baitha hai — aur uske upar dasha, samay ka chakra. Ye chaar cheezein — grah, rashi, bhav, dasha — poori vidya ki buniyad hain. Jo book in chaaron ko seedhe shabdon me samjha de, wahi beginner ki book hai.

Ek chhota sa test rakh lo: koi bhi book kholo, aur dekho ki pehle 20 page me lagna, chandra rashi aur surya rashi ka farq saaf samajh aa raha hai ya nahi. Aa raha hai to book kaam ki hai. Nahi aa raha to use baad ke liye rakh do.

Level 1: Basic Astrology Books — Buniyad, Bina Bhaari Bhasha Ke

Basic astrology books ka kaam ek hi hai: darwaza kholna. Best astrology books for beginners in Hindi ki list ka matlab bhi yehi hai — pehle dar khatam ho, phir gehrai aaye. In books ko padh kar tumhe ye lagna chahiye ki ab kundli ka naksha tumhe dara nahi raha.

1. Astrology for Beginners — B.V. Raman

B.V. Raman ka naam isliye bada hai kyunki unhone mushkil vidya ko aam reader tak pahunchaya. Unki Astrology for Beginners shuruaat grah aur rashi ke matlab se karti hai, niyam ratne se nahi. Pehle ye samajhte ho ki Surya, Chandra, Mangal kis cheez ke prateek maane jaate hain, phir bhav ka matlab aata hai, phir chart dekhne ka tareeka.

Kis ke liye? Us insaan ke liye jisne aaj tak koi jyotish ki book nahi padhi.

Dhyan rakhne wali baat: Raman ki bhasha purani kitaabon wali hai. Dheere padho — ek chapter, phir ek din ka waqt us idea ko baithne do.

2. Light on Life — Hart de Fouw aur Robert Svoboda

Light on Life ko aksar Vedic astrology ka sabse dostana introduction kaha jaata hai, aur ye sahi hai. Ye Western reader ko dhyan me rakh kar likhi gayi thi, isliye har niyam ke saath misaal hai — aur ye bhi bataya gaya hai ki is niyam ko chart par kahan dekhna hai.

Is book ki khaasiyat ye hai ki wo sirf niyam nahi, nazariya deti hai. Grah ko sirf "achha" ya "bura" kehne ki aadat se bachati hai — apni rashi me, neech rashi me, ya mitra-shatru grah ke saath, har sthiti ka matlab alag hota hai. Raman ke baad ye padhne se buniyad pakki hoti hai, kyunki tum wahi cheezein doosre tareeke se dobara dekhte ho.

Kis ke liye nahi? Sirf apni kundli ka ek-line jawab chahne walon ke liye nahi. Ye book tareeka sikhati hai, aur tareeke me waqt lagta hai.

3. Hindu Predictive Astrology — B.V. Raman

Jab buniyad ban jaaye, tab Raman ki Hindu Predictive Astrology agli seedhi hai — dasha, gochar aur yogon ki taraf. Par dhyan rakhna: "predictive" yahan paramparik niyamon ka matlab hai, guarantee ka nahi. Us varnan ko apni zindagi par seedha chipka dena galat tareeka hai.

Ise Level 1 ke aakhir me rakho. Pehle dono upar wali books khatam karo, warna ye bhi classical granth jaisi bhaari lagegi.

Level 2: Kundli Practice — Chart Par Haath Saaf Karo

Buniyad ke baad sabse badi galti ye hoti hai ki log seedha classical granth khol lete hain. Mat karo. Beech me practice ka level hai: asli chart uthana, us par grah aur bhav dekhna, aur dheere-dheere combination banana.

4. Elements of Vedic Astrology — K.S. Charak

K.S. Charak ki Elements of Vedic Astrology do hisson me hai, aur iska kaam saaf hai: chart ko tukdon me tod kar samjhana. Pehla hissa grah, rashi aur bhav ki gehrai me jaata hai; doosra unko jodna sikhata hai — koi grah kisi bhav me kya rang dikhata hai, yog kaise banta hai, dasha chart par kahan dikhti hai.

Is book ko padhne ka sahi tareeka: ek chapter padho, phir kisi jaani-pehchani kundli ka chart saamne rakho aur wahi niyam us par dhoondho. Mil gaya to samajh pakki, nahi mila to chapter dobara padho. Chart ke bina ye book aadhi hai.

Ek seedha niyam: ek grah ko dekh kar kabhi faisla mat lo. Grah, rashi, bhav aur dasha — chaaron ko ek saath dekho.

Kis ke liye? Us reader ke liye jo buniyad samajh chuka hai aur ab khud chart dekhna chahta hai. Level 1 kachcha ho to Charak bhaari lagega — wapas jao, buniyad pakki karo.

Level 3: Classical Texts — Jab Tum Taiyaar Ho, Tabhi

Classical granth jyotish ki jad hain, par inko pehle padhna tairna seekhe bina gehri nadi me utar jaane jaisa hai. Isliye ye Level 3 me hain — yahan sabr aur ek achhe anuvaad ki zaroorat hai.

5. Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra — Maharishi Parashar (Hindi translation)

Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra ko Vedic astrology ka sabse bada mool granth maana jaata hai — grah, rashi, bhav, dasha, yog aur upayon ka vistrit varnan. Iske Hindi translations alag-alag prakashakon aur anuvaadakon se milte hain, har edition ek jaisa nahi hota. Isliye khareedne se pehle prakashak aur anuvaadak ka naam check karo; is article me kisi ek prakashak ka naam isliye nahi hai kyunki ekmatra bharosemand edition batana galat hoga.

Is granth ko reference ki tarah use karo, novel ki tarah nahi. Jo topic Level 2 se samajh aa raha hai — jaise dasha — uska hissa padho. Poora granth ek baar me padhne ki koshish karne wale hi beech me chhod dete hain.

6. Phaladeepika — Mantreswara

Phaladeepika ek classical granth hai jo phal — grahon ke prabhav ke varnan — par kendrit hai. Ye tab kaam aata hai jab tum chart dekhna jaan chuke ho. Iske English translations milte hain; Hindi me uplabdhata edition par nirbhar karti hai, isliye edition yahan bhi check karo.

Ek imaandaar chetavni: Phaladeepika ke varnan ko seedha apni zindagi par lagu karna galat hai. Uska varnan samajhna seekhna hai, usko har faisle ka aakhri jawab banana nahi.

Level 4: Vedic Astrology Remedies Book — Upay Samajhna, Darna Nahi

Remedies wali kitaabein sabse zyada galat samjhi jaati hain, kyunki log unhe mushkil ka turant hal samajh kar uthate hain — aur wahi sabse zyada galat daave milte hain. Isliye ye level sabse aakhir me hai: buniyad aur chart samajh chuke ho, tabhi upay ka matlab samajh aayega.

Ek achhi vedic astrology remedies book me paramparik upayon ka varnan milega: mantra jaap, daan, vrat aur graha-shanti se judi parampara. Ye sab padhna aur samajhna theek hai.

Jo cheez theek nahi hai: koi upay jo paisa, naukri, sehat ya shaadi ki guarantee de; koi ratna jo bina kundli dekhe sirf daam dekh kar becha ja raha ho; koi puja jiska daam dar ke hisaab se badh raha ho. In teenon se door raho. Parampara me upay ka matlab discipline tha — roz ka niyam, daan ki aadat, apne vyavhaar par dhyan.

ज्योतिष सीखने की श्रेष्ठ किताब — Aur Hindi Me Jyotish Ki Pustak Ka Sach

Ab wo sawaal jo sabse zyada poochha jaata hai. ज्योतिष सीखने की श्रेष्ठ किताब kaunsi hai, aur kya ye Hindi me milegi?

Pehli baat: "shreshth" ka jawab har reader ke liye ek nahi hai. Shuru karne wale ke liye shreshth wo hai jo use daraye nahi; chart dekhna jaan gaya ho to shreshth wo hai jo gehrai de. Isliye is article ne ek book ka naam nahi, ek order diya hai. Order hi jawab hai.

Doosri baat, Hindi ka sach. Astrology books in Hindi dhoondhne wale ko ye jaanna chahiye: kuch kitaabon ke Hindi edition sach me milte hain — khaas kar B.V. Raman jaise lekhakon ke aur kuch classical granthon ke Hindi translations. Par har book ka ek hi official, aasani se milne wala Hindi edition hone ka dawa galat hoga.

To Hindi reader kya kare? Jyotish ki pustak ko English me dheere padho — roz 10-15 page. Har chapter ke baad uska idea Hindi me apne shabdon me likh lo. Grah, bhav aur dasha ka matlab tum Hindi me hi to samjhoge, chahe book kisi bhasha me ho. Aur khareedne se pehle edition, prakashak aur anuvaadak ka naam check karo — ye teenon na milen to wo edition mat lo.

Agar English bilkul mushkil lagti hai, to pehle Hindi me basic lekh aur video se idea samjho, phir original book par aao. Sirf summary padhna darwaza hai, ghar nahi.

Kaunsi Book Kaise Chune — 5 Seedhe Sawaal

Book khareedne se pehle apne aap se ye paanch sawaal poochh lo. Inme se teen ka jawab "haan" ho to book lo, warna chhod do.

  1. Main kis level par hoon? Buniyad kachchi ho to Level 1, chart aata ho to Level 2, dono pakke hon to hi Level 3.
  2. Ye book tareeka sikhati hai ya sirf jawab deti hai? Tareeka wali lo.
  3. Edition saaf hai? Prakashak, anuvaadak, edition — teenon na dikhen to mat lo.
  4. Kya ye book mujhe dara rahi hai? "Ye dosh hai, turant ye karo" wali bhasha se door raho.
  5. Kya main is book ka ek idea is hafte aazma sakta hoon? Nahi to wo abhi shelf ke liye hai.

Beginners Ki 5 Sabse Badi Galtiyan

  • Seedha classical granth se shuru karna. Granth galat nahi, waqt galat hai.
  • Sirf apni rashi padhna. Surya rashi ek hissa hai, poori kundli nahi.
  • Ek grah se poora faisla lena. Chaaron cheezein ek saath dekho.
  • Saari books ek saath khareedna. Shelf sajana padhna nahi hai.
  • Upay ko shortcut samajhna. Jo upay guarantee de, usse door raho.

Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

Jyotish sikhne ki best book kaunsi hai — ekdum beginner ke liye?

B.V. Raman ki Astrology for Beginners se shuru karo, saath me Light on Life padho. Dono buniyad bana dete hain: rashi, grah, bhav aur unka matlab. Classical granth uske baad.

Jyotish sikhne ke liye best book in Hindi kaunsi hai?

Kuch Hindi edition aur translations milte hain, par har classic ka ek hi bharosemand Hindi edition hone ka dawa galat hoga. English original dheere padho, idea Hindi me samjho, edition aur anuvaadak check karo.

Kya jyotish ki books se bhavishya ki sahi prediction ki ja sakti hai?

Nahi. Jyotish ek paramparik adhyayan pranali hai — isse sochne ka dhancha aur chintan mil sakta hai, par future ki guarantee kisi book ya upay se nahi milti. Zaroori faisle asli jaankari aur apni samajh se lo.

Vedic astrology remedies book me kya hota hai, aur kya savdhani rakhni chahiye?

Remedies books me mantra, daan, vrat aur graha-shanti jaise paramparik upayon ka varnan milta hai. Savdhani: koi upay paisa, swasthya ya kanooni faisle ki guarantee nahi deta, aur mehenga ratna ya puja ka dabav banaya jaaye to ruk jao.

Basic astrology books ke baad agli step kya hai?

Basic books ke baad kundli practice ki books aati hain — jaise K.S. Charak ki Elements of Vedic Astrology — jahan asli chart par grah, bhav aur dasha jodna seekhte ho. Uske baad hi Brihat Parashara aur Phaladeepika uthao.

Jyotish books PDF format me padhna theek hai?

Format theek hai — phone par padhna aasan hota hai — par srot ka dhyan rakho. Bina publisher aur edition ke scan par bharosa mat karo. PDF list ke liye is blog ka astrology books PDF wala article dekh sakte ho.
