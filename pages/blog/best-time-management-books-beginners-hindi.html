Time Management Books for Beginners in Hindi – Kaunsi Book, Kab, Aur Kyun

November 3, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: Agar tumhe sirf ek time management book padhni hai, to Brian Tracy ki Eat That Frog! padho. Hindi me iska edition Sabse Mushkil Kaam Sabse Pehle ke naam se milta hai. Iska idea ek line ka hai, par us ek line ne laakhon logon ka din badal diya hai: din ka sabse mushkil aur sabse zaroori kaam sabse pehle karo. Uske baad jo bache, wo apne aap halka lagne lagta hai. Aur agar tumhe poori Hindi me likhi hui book chahiye, to Sudhir Dixit ki Time Management se shuru karo. Wo Hindi me hi likhi gayi hai, students ke liye seedhi hai, aur usme plan banane ke liye exercise bhi hain.

Honestly, time management ki kitaabon ki dikkat yehi hai. Har list me wahi 25 naam hote hain, par koi ye nahi batata ki pehle kaunsi, kis ke liye, aur kaunsi abhi mat uthao. Ek list tumhe ek bhaari system pakda degi, doosri tumhe ye keh degi ki bas ye ek trick apna lo, zindagi set ho jayegi. Dono adhoore hain. Is article me sirf 7 books hain. Har ek ek alag kaam karti hai. Aur har ek ke saath ye bhi likha hai ki ye kis ke liye nahi hai.

Pehle Ye Samajh Lo: Time Management Ka Matlab Kya Nahi Hai

Ek galatfehmi pehle hi saaf kar dete hain. Time management ka matlab poora din busy rehna nahi hai. Busy rehna aasaan hai. Phone uthao, 40 notification dekh lo, 10 kaam adhoore chhod do — din to bhar gaya, par kaam kya hua? Kuch khaas nahi. Asli time management teen kaam karta hai. Pehla: ye tay karna ki aaj sabse zaroori kaam kaunsa hai. Doosra: us kaam ke liye bina distraction ke waqt nikaalna. Teesra: baaki sab kaam ek system me daal dena, taaki wo dimaag me ghoomte na rahen.

Ek baat aur. Time management ka matlab aaram khatam kar dena bhi nahi hai. Jo book tumhe ye sikhaye ki sona, parivaar ke saath waqt, ya bas khaali baithna — ye sab "waste" hai, us book se door raho. Thaka hua dimaag koi kaam dhang se nahi karta. Aaram bhi plan ka hissa hai, plan se bhaag nahi.

To phir ye books deti kya hain? Ek tareeka. Koi book tumhe priority chunna sikhati hai, koi focus, koi poora system. Tumhe teenon chahiye, par ek saath nahi. Pehle ek tareeka, phir doosra. Isi order me ye list banayi gayi hai.

7 Books, 7 Alag Kaam

Neeche har book ke saath teen cheezein milengi: ye book kya sikhati hai, ye kis ke liye hai, aur isme dhyan rakhne wali baat kya hai. List ko upar se neeche tak padhna zaroori nahi. Apni zaroorat wali book chuno, wahi se shuru karo.

1. Eat That Frog! — Brian Tracy

Is book ka naam ajeeb lagta hai, par uska matlab bahut seedha hai. "Frog" wo kaam hai jisko tum sabse zyada taal rahe ho — aur jo sabse zyada zaroori bhi hai. Tracy kehte hain: agar tumhe din me ek frog khana hi hai, to subah sabse pehle kha lo. Baaki din uske baad behtar hi lagega. Aur agar do frog hain, to pehle bada wala, ugly wala khao.

Is book me 21 tareeke hain, par tumhe 21 yaad rakhne ki zaroorat nahi. Teen kaafi hain. Pehla: roz ka kaam ek din pehle likh lo. Doosra: 80/20 rule — tumhare 20% kaam hi 80% result laate hain, to un 20% ko pehchano. Teesra: ABCDE method — har kaam ko A, B, C, D, E me baanto. A matlab aaj hi karna hai, warna nuksaan. E matlab chhoda bhi ja sakta hai. Jab tum ye teen kar lete ho, to din ki shakal hi badal jaati hai.

Kis ke liye? Un logon ke liye jo kaam taal dete hain. Student ho, job karte ho, ya apna kaam chalate ho — agar tumhara din "kal kar lunga" par chalta hai, to ye book tumhari pehli book hai.

Dhyan rakhne wali baat: Ye book chhoti aur halki hai, jaldi khatam ho jaati hai. Par uska matlab ye nahi ki uska kaam bhi jaldi khatam ho gaya. Padhne ke agle din se ek frog chuno aur usko subah pehle karo. Book padhna aasaan hai. Frog khana mushkil. Wahi asli test hai.

2. Time Management — Sudhir Dixit (Hindi)

Ye list ki ek khaas book hai, kyunki ye Hindi me hi likhi gayi hai. Isme desi scenario hain — office, ghar, school/college — aur har chapter ke saath exercise aur planner hain, jinko tum turant apni routine me laga sakte ho. Isliye ye book un logon ke liye bahut kaam ki hai jinhe English me lambi book padhna abhi bhaari lagta hai.

Dixit ka zor sirf schedule banane par nahi hai. Unka kehna hai ki pehle apni priority pehchano, phir distraction sambhalo, phir apni energy ka khayal rakho. Ek student ke liye iska matlab ye hai: exam se pehle poora saal ka planner nahi, roz ka chhota plan. Ek working insaan ke liye iska matlab ye hai: har kaam ko "haan" kehna band karo, warna tumhara waqt doosron ke kaam me hi nikal jayega.

Kis ke liye best hai? Hindi readers ke liye, students ke liye, aur un logon ke liye jo pehli baar time management ki book utha rahe hain aur chahte hain ki book unki bhasha me baat kare.

Ek imaandaar baat: Ye book chhoti hai, aur jaldi khatam ho jaati hai. Isliye isko padh kar rukna mat. Iske planner ko ek hafte tak roz bharo. Jo book tumhare din me kuch badle nahi, wo sirf padhi hui book hai, seekhi hui nahi.

3. Deep Work — Cal Newport

Ab ek alag sawaal. Tum din bhar kaam karte ho, par kya tum kabhi poori tarah ek kaam par dhyan de paate ho? Newport kehte hain ki aaj ki duniya me yehi sabse mushkil aur sabse keemti skill ban gayi hai. Deep work matlab: bina distraction ke, lambi der tak, ek mushkil kaam par poora dhyan. Shallow work matlab: email, message, meeting, chhote-chhote kaam — jo zaroori lagte hain, par tumhe aage nahi badhate.

Is book ki sabse kaam ki baat ye hai ki wo sirf "phone band kar do" nahi kehti. Wo batati hai ki deep work ke liye ritual chahiye: ek fixed jagah, ek fixed waqt, aur ek rule ki us waqt me kya allowed hai, kya nahi. Jaise, subah ke pehle 90 minute phone doosre kamre me, aur sirf ek kaam. Pehle hafte me ye ajeeb lagega. Doosre hafte me tumhe khud farq dikhega: jo kaam pehle poora din kha jaata tha, wo 2 ghante me ho jaata hai.

Ek chhota sa test: agar tum apne din ka sabse zaroori kaam phone ke saath, notification ke saath, adhoore dhyan se kar rahe ho, to tum wo kaam nahi kar rahe. Tum sirf us kaam ke aas paas ghoom rahe ho.

Kis ke liye nahi? Un logon ke liye nahi jo abhi apni priority hi nahi tay kar paaye hain. Pehle Eat That Frog se ye tay karo ki kaunsa kaam zaroori hai, phir Deep Work se us kaam par dhyan lagao. Ulta order me tum galat kaam par bhi bahut achha focus kar loge — aur wo to aur bura hai.

4. Getting Things Done — David Allen (2001)

Ye list ki sabse poori system wali book hai. Allen ka idea ek line me hai: dimaag idea rakhne ke liye hai, kaam yaad rakhne ke liye nahi. Jab tak har chhota kaam tumhare dimaag me ghoom raha hai — "ye bhi karna hai, wo bhi karna hai, usko bhi message karna hai" — tab tak tumhara dimaag khaali hi nahi hota. Aur bhara hua dimaag kisi ek kaam par dhyan nahi de pata.

Iska system paanch kadam ka hai. Capture: sab kuch likh lo, dimaag se bahar. Clarify: har cheez ka matlab tay karo — ye kaam hai, to agla kadam kya hai? Organize: har kaam ko uski jagah par rakho. Reflect: hafte me ek baar poori list ko dobara dekho. Engage: phir kaam chuno aur karo. Aur ek chhota rule jo turant kaam aata hai: two-minute rule. Jo kaam 2 minute me ho jaaye, usko list me mat daalo. Usi waqt kar do.

Kis ke liye? Un logon ke liye jinke paas kaam bahut zyada hai — job, padhai, ghar, side project, sab ek saath. Agar tumhara din isliye kharab hota hai kyunki tum bhool jaate ho ki kya karna tha, to ye book tumhare liye hai.

Dhyan rakhne wali baat: Ye book poora system mangti hai, sirf ek trick nahi. Isliye isko pehli book mat banao. Pehle Eat That Frog se priority seekh lo, phir GTD se system banao. Aur system ko app ke peeche mat chhupao. Ek notebook aur ek list se shuru karo. App baad me bhi aa sakta hai, system pehle aana chahiye.

5. The One Thing — Gary Keller aur Jay Papasan

Ye book ek sawaal par tiki hai, aur wo sawaal tumhe roz poochhna hai: "Aisi ek cheez kaunsi hai, jisko karne se baaki sab kuch aasaan ya bekaar ho jayega?" Is sawaal ka jawab tumhara One Thing hai. Aur us One Thing ko domino ki tarah samjho. Pehla chhota domino girayo, to wo doosre ko girayega, wo teesre ko — aur aakhir me bada domino bhi gir jaata hai.

Is book ki doosri badi baat ye hai ki ye multitasking ki pol kholti hai. Ek saath 3 kaam karne ka matlab ye nahi hai ki tum 3 guna tez ho. Matlab ye hai ki tum 3 kaam adhoore kar rahe ho, aur har baar ek kaam se doosre par jaane me tumhara dhyan aur waqt dono kharch ho rahe hain. Ek waqt me ek kaam. Bas.

Kis ke liye best hai? Un logon ke liye jo har cheez ko "zaroori" keh dete hain. Agar tumhari to-do list 20 kaam ki hai aur tum roz 19 chhod dete ho, to tumhari dikkat mehnat nahi hai. Dikkat ye hai ki tumne apna One Thing kabhi chuna hi nahi.

6. Essentialism — Greg McKeown

Essentialism ka matlab hai: kam, par behtar. McKeown kehte hain ki hum zyada tar waqt isliye barbaad karte hain kyunki hum har cheez ko "haan" keh dete hain. Har meeting, har kaam, har request. Aur jab sab kuch zaroori ho jaata hai, to kuch bhi zaroori nahi rehta. Trade-off se bhaagna band karo. Har "haan" ke peeche ek "naa" chhupi hoti hai — wo "naa" aksar tumhare apne kaam ko, apni sehat ko, ya apne parivaar ko milti hai.

Is book ka sabse kaam ka hissa "naa" kehna seekhna hai. Bina rude hue, bina sorry ka lecture diye. Ek seedha tareeka: "Main is waqt is par dhyan de raha hoon, isliye ye kaam abhi nahi le sakta." Bas. Tumhe har request ka jawaab turant dena zaroori nahi. Aur jo kaam tumne liya hi nahi, uska bojh tumhare dimaag par bhi nahi aayega.

Ek side note. Essentialism ka matlab ye nahi hai ki tum sirf ek kaam karo aur baaki sab chhod do. Matlab ye hai ki tum pehle ye tay karo ki tumhare liye "essential" kya hai, phir baaki sab ko dheere-dheere hatao. Ye ek din ka kaam nahi hai. Ye har hafte ka chhota sa saaf-safai ka kaam hai.

Kis ke liye? Un logon ke liye jo hamesha busy hain, par khud se poochhen to bata nahi sakte ki unhone aaj kya zaroori kaam kiya. Agar tumhara calendar bhara hua hai aur dil khaali hai, to ye book tumhare liye hai.

7. Make Time — Jake Knapp aur John Zeratsky

Ye list ki sabse nayi soch wali book hai, aur students aur working logon ke liye shayad sabse turant kaam ki bhi. Knapp aur Zeratsky kehte hain ki din ko chaar hisson me baanto. Highlight: aaj ka ek kaam, jo din ko kaamyaab banayega. Laser: distraction se bachne ke tareeke — phone ko door rakhna, notification band karna, social media ke liye fixed waqt. Energize: neend, khana, harkat — kyunki thaka hua insaan koi plan nahi chala sakta. Reflect: raat ko 2 minute, ki aaj kya kaam kiya, kya nahi.

Is book ki khaasiyat ye hai ki ye lambi theory nahi deti. Ye 80 se zyada chhoti tactics deti hai, aur tumhe kehti hai ki inme se ek aazmao, dekho, phir agli. Jaise, subah phone ko charge par doosre kamre me rakh do. Ya, din ka highlight subah likh lo, taaki din tumhe na khinche, tum din ko khincho.

Kis ke liye best hai? Un logon ke liye jinka sabse bada dushman phone hai. Agar tum padhne baithte ho aur 10 minute me phone haath me aa jaata hai, to pehle ye book padho. System baad me, distraction pehle sambhalo.

Ye Books Abhi Mat Lo — Aur Kyun

Ab teen galtiyan jo har beginner karta hai, aur jinse ye list tumhe bachana chahti hai.

Pehli: ek saath 3-4 system. GTD bhi, planner bhi, ek app bhi, ek book ka method bhi. Koi ek system poora nahi chalta, aur phir lagta hai ki "time management mere bas ka nahi". Ek system chuno. Usko 4 hafte chalao. Phir faisla karo.

Doosri: planner aur app ki khareedari ko progress samajhna. Naya planner khareedna achha lagta hai. Nayi app download karna bhi. Par wo progress nahi hai, wo taiyaari hai. Aur taiyaari me hi poora saal nikal jaata hai, agar tumne us par dhyan nahi diya. Pehle ek notebook se shuru karo. Jab system chal padega, tab tool chuno — ulta nahi.

Teesri: productivity guilt. Jo book ya video tumhe ye sikhaye ki har minute productive hona chahiye, har ghanta count hona chahiye, warna tum peeche reh jaoge — usse door raho. Ye soch tumhe zyada kaam nahi karwati, ye tumhe thaka deti hai. Aur thaka hua insaan agle din aur kam kar pata hai. Aaram, neend, aur parivaar ke saath waqt — ye bhi tumhare plan ka hissa hain, usse bahar nahi.

Hindi Me Time Management Books Kaise Padhein — Seedha Rasta

Ab wo sawaal jo sabse zyada poochha jaata hai: ye books Hindi me milengi?

Imaandaar jawab ye hai: kuch hi books ka bharosemand Hindi edition aasani se milta hai. Is list me do pakke naam hain. Sudhir Dixit ki Time Management Hindi me hi likhi gayi hai. Brian Tracy ki Eat That Frog! ka Hindi edition Sabse Mushkil Kaam Sabse Pehle ke naam se milta hai. Baaki books ke liye — Deep Work, GTD, The One Thing, Essentialism, Make Time — khareedne se pehle edition, publisher aur anuvaadak ka naam zaroor check karo. Koi list ye dawa kar ke banana galat hoga ki saari 7 books Hindi me uplabdh hain.

To Hindi reader kya kare? Ek rasta ye hai. Book ko English me dheere padho — roz 10-15 page, jaldi ke bina. Har chapter ke baad uska idea apne shabdon me Hindi me likh lo. Phir us idea ko apni rozmarra ki zindagi me lagao. Jaise Eat That Frog ka frog wala idea tum apne din me Hindi me hi to aazmaoge. Book English me hai, par uska kaam tumhari bhasha me hi hoga.

Aur agar English bilkul mushkil lagti hai, to pehle Hindi me us book ke basic summary aur video se idea samjho, phir original book par aao. Ulta rasta — sirf summary padh kar book padhne ka dawa karna — lambi race me kaam nahi aata. Summary darwaza hai, ghar nahi.

4 Hafton Ka Plan — Padhna + Karna

Book khareedna aasaan hai. Us par amal karna mushkil hai. Isliye ek chhota plan:

  1. Hafta 1 — Shuru karo: Eat That Frog! padho. Roz subah ek frog chuno — sabse mushkil, sabse zaroori kaam — aur usko sabse pehle karo. Baaki kaam uske baad.
  2. Hafta 2 — Plan banao: Sudhir Dixit ki Time Management ke hisaab se roz ka chhota plan likho. Ek din pehle, agle din ke 3 zaroori kaam. Bas 3. Zyada nahi.
  3. Hafta 3 — Focus karo: Deep Work ke hisaab se din ka ek 90-minute block banao — phone doosre kamre me, sirf ek kaam. Is hafte ka kaam ye block roz bachana hai, chahe jo ho.
  4. Hafta 4 — System banao: Getting Things Done ke hisaab se apne dimaag ka poora bojh ek list me utaaro — sab kuch likh lo. Phir har kaam ka agla kadam likho. Aur The One Thing wala sawaal roz poochho: aaj ki ek cheez kaunsi hai?

Baaki 2 books — Essentialism aur Make Time — uske baad, jab ye chaar hafton ka kaam ho jaaye. Kyunki us waqt tumhare paas apna experience hoga, aur ye books tumhe apne experience ko samajhne me madad karengi, sirf theory nahi lagengi.

Kya Sirf Books Kaafi Hain? Nahi.

Ye article ka sabse zaroori hissa shayad yehi hai, isliye isko seedha keh raha hoon. Books tumhe tareeka dengi, par wo tumhara waqt tumhare liye nahi bacha sakti. Waqt tumhe khud hi dena hoga. Aur agar tumhara din isliye kharab hai kyunki tum par kaam ka bojh bahut zyada hai, ya tum kisi mushkil daur se guzar rahe ho, to book padhna pehla kadam ho sakta hai, par aakhri kadam nahi. Apne kaam ka bojh kam karna, madad mangna, ya kisi bharosemand insaan se baat karna — ye bhi time management ka hissa hai, usse bahar nahi.

Aur ek baat. Time management seekh kar kuch log har minute ka hisaab rakhne lagte hain — har ghanta, har minute, har break. Mat karo. Ye kitaabein pehle tumhare din ko halka karne ke liye hain, tumhare din ko jail banane ke liye nahi. Jo insaan apne din par kaboo pa leta hai, wo apne aap behtar kaam karne lagta hai. Ulta rasta sirf thakan deta hai.

Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

Time management seekhne ke liye sabse pehli book kaunsi padhni chahiye?

Agar sirf ek book padhni hai to Brian Tracy ki Eat That Frog! se shuru karo — Hindi edition Sabse Mushkil Kaam Sabse Pehle ke naam se milta hai. Agar tumhe poori Hindi me likhi hui book chahiye, to Sudhir Dixit ki Time Management se shuru karo.

Time management ki best book in Hindi kaunsi hai?

Poori Hindi me likhi hui practical book ke liye Sudhir Dixit ki Time Management sabse seedha jawab hai. Baaki duniya-bhar ki mashhoor books ke liye khareedne se pehle edition aur anuvaadak check karo — har book ka bharosemand Hindi edition aasani se nahi milta.

Students ke liye time management books in Hindi me kaunsi sabse kaam ki hai?

Students ke liye Eat That Frog (talna band karne ke liye), Sudhir Dixit ki Time Management (Hindi me seedha plan) aur Make Time (phone distraction se bachne ke liye) — ye teen sabse kaam ki hain. Inme se kisi ek se shuru karo, teenon ek saath mat uthao.

Kya sirf time management book padh lene se time manage ho jayega?

Nahi. Book sirf tareeka batati hai, waqt tumhe khud hi dena hoga. Ek book padho, uska ek idea ek hafte tak roz aazmao, phir agli book uthao.

Productivity books padh kar bhi kaam kyu nahi hota?

Aksar isliye kyunki hum ek saath 3-4 system chala lete hain aur koi ek system poora nahi chalta. Ek system chuno, usko 4 hafte chalao, phir faisla karo ki wo tumhare liye kaam karta hai ya nahi.

Ek saath saari books khareedni chahiye ya ek-ek karke?

Ek-ek karke. Pehle ek book padho, uska ek idea ek hafte tak aazmao, phir agli uthao. Saari khareed kar shelf par sajana padhna nahi kehlata.
