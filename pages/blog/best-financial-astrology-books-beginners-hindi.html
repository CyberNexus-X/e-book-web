Financial Astrology Books: Grah-Chakra Se Market Timing Sikhne Ki Best Kitaabein — Beginner Guide

November 10, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: Financial astrology sikhne ki pehli kitaab Christeen H. Skinner ki The Beginner's Guide to the Financial Universe hai — ye is vishay ki sabse seedhi beginner kitaab hai, aur iski sabse badi khaasiyat ye hai ki iski preface me khud likha hai ki ye kitaab trading nahi sikhati. Par usse pehle ek shart hai: birth chart ki buniyad pakki honi chahiye. Lagna, 12 bhav aur grah samajh lo, phir ye kitaab uthao. Seedha isse shuru karoge, to cycles bhi bhaari lagenge aur ephemeris bhi. Is article me wahi order hai — pehle framework, phir kitaabein, aur sabse pehle ek seedha niyam jo poori article me baar-baar aayega.

Honestly, financial astrology sabse zyada galat samjha jaane wala hissa hai. Log ek YouTube video dekhte hain, koi "Shani ka transit = market crash" wali line sunte hain, aur agle din apna paisa us par laga dete hain. Ya koi "guaranteed returns" wala course ₹10,000 me bech deta hai. Dono galat hain. Financial astrology ek paramparik timing study hai — grah-chakra ke patterns ko market ke utaar-chadhav se jodne ka tareeka. Ye trading signal nahi hai. Ye investment advice nahi hai. Aur ye risk management ki jagah kabhi nahi le sakta.

Ye baat isliye pehle likh raha hoon, kyunki is vishay me galti ki keemat seedhi paisa hoti hai. Sehat wali galti se zindagi kharab hoti hai, career wali se saal — yahan galti se account khaali hota hai. Isliye is article ki har kitaab ke saath ye bhi likha hai ki wo kis kaam ki hai — aur kis kaam ki nahi.

Pehle Ye Samajh Lo: Financial Astrology Kya Hai, Kya Nahi Hai

Financial astrology, ya geocosmic studies, grah-chakra ke patterns ko market ki harkat se jodne ki koshish hai. Is vishay ke sabse bade naam, Raymond Merriman, isko market timing ka ek hissa kehte hain — sahi market analysis ke teen hisse hote hain: trend analysis, market timing, aur technical analysis. Geocosmic studies usi timing wale hisse me aati hain. Matlab, ye poora trading system nahi hai. Ye ek hissa hai.

Merriman ki philosophy teen cheezon par khadi hai: (1) sahi analysis, (2) ek trading plan jo signals ko risk management ke saath jode, aur (3) us plan par chalne ki discipline. Dhyaan do — teeno me se ek bhi astrology nahi hai. Astrology sirf pehle hisse, analysis, ka ek tool hai. Jo vyakti astrology ko poora plan samajh le, wo pehle kadam par hi galat hai.

Ek example se samjho. Socho Jupiter aur Saturn ka ek bada cycle badal raha hai. Paramparik padhne me iska matlab ye hota hai ki bazaar ke mood me badlav ka samay aa sakta hai — lambi avadhi ke cycles ke hisaab se. Iska matlab ye nahi hai ki kal subah Nifty girega. Aur ye to bilkul nahi hai ki tumhe kal subah koi stock bechna ya khareedna chahiye. Cycle ka kaam hai samay ki taraf ishara karna, faisla dena nahi.

To financial astrology kya nahi hai? Ye trading signal nahi hai. Ye "sure-shot tip" nahi hai. Ye SEBI-registered advice nahi hai. Ye ek adhyayan hai — jaise cycles padhna. Ise usi nazariye se padho, to ye kaam ka hai. Paisa banane ki jaldi se padho, to yehi vishay tumhe nuksan pahuncha dega.

Framework: 2nd House, Jupiter-Saturn Cycle Aur Grah — Kitaab Se Pehle Ye Samjho

Kitaab uthane se pehle teen cheezein samajh lo. Ye teenon is article ki kitaabon me baar-baar aayengi, isliye yahin se shuruaat karte hain.

Pehla: bhav. Paisa ka mukhya bhav 2nd house hai — isko dhan bhav kehte hain. Uske saath 11th house labh (gains, income) se judta hai, aur 5th house speculation aur risk se. Ek vyakti ki chart me ye teenon bhav mil kar batate hain ki wo paisa kaise dekhta hai, kaise kamata hai, aur risk kaise leta hai. Par ek bhav se koi faisla nahi hota — parampara me grah, rashi, drishti aur dasha ko saath me padha jata hai.

Doosra: Jupiter-Saturn cycle. Jupiter aur Saturn ka sangam (conjunction) lagbhag har 20 saal me hota hai, aur Skinner isko ek pehchana hua 20-saal ka business cycle manti hain — iske alag-alag phases ka bazaar aur arthvyavastha par apna asar hota hai. Ye is vishay ka sabse bada aur sabse zyada padha jaane wala cycle hai. Iske alawa Surya ke sunspot cycle aur Chandra ke phases bhi market ke mood se jode jate hain — Skinner ki Beginner's Guide inhi se shuru hoti hai.

Teesra: geocosmic timing. Financial astrology me grahon ki sthiti (ephemeris) aur unke beech ke angles (aspects) ko padha jata hai — kab do grah ek saath aate hain, kab aamne-saamne hote hain, kab ek doosre se 90 degree par hote hain. Merriman ki kitaab ka aadha hissa yahi sikhata hai: ephemeris kaise padhe, aspect kaise nikale, aur computer se ye sab kaise kare. Ye hisaab-kitab hai, bhavishyavani nahi.

Ab jab ye teenon dimaag me hain, to kitaabein samajh aayengi. Aur agar ye teenon abhi bhi naye lag rahe hain, to pehle hamara Birth chart reading guide padh lo — usme Lagna, 12 bhav aur dasha detail me hain. Uske bina financial astrology ki kitaab uthana, bina map ke sheher me ghumne jaisa hai.

Level 1: Pehli Kitaab — The Beginner's Guide to the Financial Universe, Christeen Skinner

Agar sirf ek kitaab leni ho, to yehi lo. Christeen H. Skinner ki The Beginner's Guide to the Financial Universe (Ibis Press, 2017) is vishay ki sabse seedhi beginner kitaab hai — aur iski sabse badi khaasiyat ye hai ki ye kitaab imandari se likhi gayi hai. Iski preface me saaf likha hai: "THE CONTENTS OF THIS BOOK WILL NOT teach you how to trade." Matlab, lekhika pehle hi bata deti hai ki ye kitaab kya nahi hai. Jo kitaab apni limit khud bata de, wahi bharosemand hoti hai.

Kitaab kya sikhati hai? Step by step, kaise Surya, Chandra, grah aur bade planet cycles market ki harkat se judte hain. Chapters is order me hain: sunspot cycle aur market; seasonality charts aur key dates; Mercury aur Venus ke saath trading; commodities me Mars ki bhoomika; Jupiter-Saturn 20-saal business cycle; aur bade markets aur indices ki charts. Skinner 30 saal se zyada se traders ke saath kaam kar rahi hain, aur kitaab me unhi ke sawaalon ke jawab hain — isliye ye kitaab ek trader ke dimaag se likhi gayi lagti hai, na ki sirf ek astrologer ke.

Ye kitaab kiske liye hai? Do tarah ke readers ke liye — ek trader jo astrology nahi jaanta, aur ek astrologer jo market nahi jaanta. Skinner kitaab ki shuruaat me ye sawaal khud poochti hain: "WHAT KIND OF BEGINNER ARE You?" Agar tum trader ho, to tumhe grah aur rashi ki bhasha sikhni hogi. Agar tum astrologer ho, to tumhe market ki bhasha sikhni hogi. Dono ke liye kitaab ke peeche glossary aur symbols ki guide di gayi hai.

Ek aur baat. Ye kitaab Tropical zodiac par hai — Western system. Vedic (sidereal) system se iska hisaab alag hota hai. Ye koi galti nahi hai, bas do alag paramparaen hain. Pehle ek ko seekho, phir doosri ko compare karo. Dono ko ek saath mila kar mat padho.

Level 2: Gehrai — Merriman Ki Basic Principles Aur Skinner Ki Exploring

Jab Beginner's Guide haath me aa jaye, tab do raaste khulte hain. Dono ek saath mat uthao — ek ko chuno.

Basic Principles of Geocosmic Studies for Financial Market Timing — Raymond A. Merriman

Ye Raymond A. Merriman ki Basic Principles of Geocosmic Studies for Financial Market Timing (1997) is vishay ki standard beginner textbook mani jati hai. Merriman ne 1980 me mashhoor cycle analyst Walter Bressert se milne ke baad ye kaam shuru kiya tha, aur usi saal unki pehli kitaab, The Gold Book, aayi — gold ke price cycles ko planetary cycles se jodne par pehli quantitative kitaab.

Kitaab kya sikhati hai? Bilkul buniyad se: geocosmic studies kya hain, kaunse tools chahiye, ephemeris kaise padhe (do hisson me), grah kya hain, rashi kya hain, aspects kaise nikale, aspectarian kya hota hai, aur computer se ye sab kaise kare. Merriman khud likhte hain ki ye kitaab "the basic beginning principles necessary to get started" sikhati hai — matlab, ye shuruaat ki kitaab hai, poori kitaab nahi. Aur yehi iski taakat hai. Ye kitaab tumhe hisaab-kitab sikhata hai, sapne nahi dikhata.

Ye kitaab kiske liye hai? Uske liye jo Beginner's Guide padh chuka hai aur ab khud chart banana aur cycle nikaalna seekhna chahta hai. Agar tumne abhi tak grah aur rashi ki bhasha nahi samjhi, to ye kitaab bhaari lagegi — aur tab sahi raasta ye hai ki pehle hamare Astrology books guide se buniyad banao, phir yahan aao.

Exploring the Financial Universe — Christeen Skinner

Doosra raasta hai Skinner ki Exploring the Financial Universe (2016). Ye Beginner's Guide se pehle aayi thi, par gehrai me usse aage hai. Ye kitaab solar activity aur planet cycles se shuru hoti hai, phir time aur price ke rishte par jati hai — matlab, kab aur kitna — aur iske aakhri hisse me tumhari apni chart aur personal finance par ek chapter hai.

Kaunsi chunein — Merriman ya Exploring? Agar tumhe hisaab-kitab aur method chahiye — ephemeris, aspects, geocosmic signatures — to Merriman. Agar tumhe bade cycles aur market ke mood ko samajhna hai, to Exploring. Dono sahi hain. Dono ek saath nahi.

Gann Ka Raasta: Originals Bhaari Hain, Ye Shortcut Hai

Ab ek naam jo is vishay me har jagah milega: W.D. Gann. Gann beeswi sadi ke sabse mashhoor trader the, aur unka maanna tha ki market number, time aur vibration ke natural laws se chalti hai. Unki kitaabein hain: Truth of the Stock Tape (1923), The Tunnel Thru the Air (1927), How to Make Profits in Commodities (1941), aur 45 Years in Wall Street (1949).

Par sach ye hai ki Gann ki original kitaabein beginners ke liye nahi hain. The Tunnel Thru the Air ek coded novel hai — isme unke astrological ideas code me chhupe hain. Aur Gann ke jaan-ne wale khud kehte hain ki unki kitaabein akele padhna mushkil hai. 1946 me Gann ne apne serious students ke liye lagbhag 76 kitaabon ki ek reading list banayi thi — numerology, astrology aur science, teen hisson me — kyunki unka maanna tha ki market ko samajhne ke liye pehle number aur time ko samajhna hoga.

To beginner Gann ko kaise padhe? Shortcut se. Daniel T. Ferrera ki Gann for the Active Trader Gann ke tools ka ek chhota, seedha introduction hai, aur Gann ke jaan-ne wale isko "the first and best place to begin" kehte hain. Uske saath Michael S. Jenkins ki Stock Trading Using Planetary Time Cycles — The Gann Method, Volume I hai — Jenkins khud kehte hain ki ye ek "basic" kitaab hai jo batati hai ki Gann ne grahon se market ke turning points kaise nikale. Jenkins ki kitaab me chaar computer programs bhi hain, jinse Gann ke hisaab khud karke dekhe ja sakte hain.

Ek baat yaad rakhna: Gann ke baare me internet par bahut si kahaniyan hain — kuch sach, kuch badha-chadha kar. Kitaab se seekho, kahaniyon se nahi. Aur Gann ko tabhi uthao jab Skinner aur Merriman ki buniyad pakki ho. Gann pehla kadam nahi hai, teesra ya chauthe kadam hai.

Bharatiya Nazariya: Desh Ki Kundli Se Market — Mundane Astrology

Ab Bharat ke liye sabse zaroori hissa. Vedic parampara me financial astrology ka raasta mundane astrology se jata hai — kisi vyakti ki nahi, desh ki kundli padhi jati hai. Desh ki swatantrata ki kundli, ganatantra ki kundli, grah-mandal aur grahan — in sab se desh ki arthvyavastha aur bazaar ke mood ko samajhne ki koshish ki jati hai.

Is raaste ki standard kitaab hai Time Tested Techniques of Mundane Astrology — M.S. Mehta aur A. Radhika Rao, guide aur editor K.N. Rao. Isme Bharat ki swatantrata aur Republic Day ki kundli, deshon ke ruling signs, Koorma Chakra, Sanghatta Chakra, grahon aur bhavon ke significations, grahan, aur Shani/Rahu ke Rohini nakshatra me pravesh ka Bharat par asar — ye sab hai. K.N. Rao Bharat ke sabse bade Vedic astrologers me se ek hain, aur ye kitaab unki dekh-rekh me likhi gayi hai.

Ye kitaab kiske liye hai? Uske liye jo Vedic astrology jaanta hai aur ab use desh aur bazaar par lagana chahta hai. Beginner ke taur par isse shuru mat karo — ye advanced kitaab hai. Pehle Skinner ya Merriman se buniyad banao, phir hamara Birth chart guide se Vedic bhav aur dasha pakki karo, tab yahan aao.

Ek aur Bharatiya kitaab, short me. The Financial Markets and The Karmic Law (Volume 1) (2012) ek Indian kitaab hai jo Hindu calendar ke factors — Hindu saal aur mahine, tithi aur karana — ko market sectors se jodti hai, aur intraday, short-term aur long-term buying-selling ko sun signs ke hisaab se samjhati hai. Ye ek self-published kitaab hai, isliye isko ek vikalp ki tarah padho, standard ki tarah nahi.

Advanced, Abhi Nahi — Merriman Ki 5-Volume Kitaab Aur Gold Book

Kuch kitaabein aisi hain jo is vishay me sabse upar mani jati hain — par wo abhi tumhari nahi hain.

The Ultimate Book on Stock Market Timing — Raymond Merriman. Paanch volumes, 1,600 se zyada pages, aur 16 saal ka research — market cycles aur unke planetary cycles se rishte par. Ye kitaab 2012 me poori hui thi. Ye is vishay ki sabse badi kitaab hai, aur isiliye ye beginner ki kitaab nahi hai. Isko tab uthao jab tumne Skinner, Merriman ki Basic Principles aur Gann ka raasta — teeno — poora kar liya ho.

The Gold Book: Geocosmic Correlations to Gold Price Cycles — Raymond Merriman (1982). Gold ke price cycles ko planetary cycles se jodne par pehli quantitative kitaab. Ye tab padho jab tumhe ek hi market — gold — ki gehrai me jana ho. Pehle din se nahi.

Aur Merriman ki saalana Forecast books bhi hain — har saal ke liye market timing ki kitaab. Ye bhi pehli kitaab nahi hain. Forecast tab samajh aata hai jab tumhe cycles ki bhasha aati hai. Pehle bhasha seekho, phir forecast padho.

Abhi Mat Uthao — Pirated PDF, Guaranteed Returns Aur Galat Order

Ek cheez jo har beginner karta hai: Google par "financial astrology books pdf" search karna. Free PDF mil bhi jaye, to uske teen nuksan hain. Pehla, wo aam taur par pirated hoti hai — lekhak ki mehnat ki chori. Doosra, wo purani ya adhoori hoti hai — beech ke pages gayab. Teesra, aisi sites par malware bhi milta hai. Free me padhna hai, to sahi raasta hai: Merriman ka free weekly column (mmacycles.com par) aur Skinner ka free Full Moon letter (financialuniverse.co.uk par) — dono free, legal, aur current hain.

Doosri cheez jo abhi mat karo: "guaranteed returns" wala course lena. Jo bhi ye dawa kare — chahe wo astrology ke naam par ho, ya trading ke naam par — usse door raho. Market me guarantee naam ki koi cheez nahi hoti. Aur teesri cheez: classical granth ya Gann ke originals ko seedha uthana. Granth galat nahi hai. Order galat hai. Pehle Skinner, phir Merriman, phir Gann ya Mundane. Isi order me chaloge, to har kitaab samajh aayegi.

Aur agar tumhe share market ki kitaabon ki poori list chahiye — bina astrology ke — to hamara Stock Market books guide agla achha kadam hai. Astrology aur market, dono ko alag-alag seekhna, phir jodna — yehi sahi order hai.

Ye 5 Galtiyan Mat Karo

Ab wo galtiyan, jo is vishay me sabse zyada hoti hain. Inse bach gaye, to aadha kaam ho gaya — aur aadha paisa bhi bach gaya.

Galti 1: Astrology ko trading signal samajhna. Ek transit dekh kar "kal khareedo" ya "kal becho" kehna galat hai. Astrology timing ki taraf ishara karti hai, signal nahi deti. Signal ke liye trend, technical analysis aur risk management chahiye — Merriman ke teen factors yaad rakho.

Galti 2: Risk management chhodna. "Cycle achha hai, isliye poora paisa lagao" — ye sabse khatarnak soch hai. Cycle chahe kitna bhi achha lage, position sizing aur stop-loss pehle aata hai. Jo kitaab ye nahi sikhati, wo kitaab adhoori hai.

Galti 3: Ek transit = ek trade. "Jupiter badal raha hai to market badhega" — aisa ek line me kehna galat hai. Ek transit ke saath dasha, bhav, grah ki sthiti aur market ka trend — ye sab dekhe bina koi faisla nahi banta.

Galti 4: "Sure-shot tip" walon par bharosa karna. Jo vyakti ya channel astrology ke naam par "sure-shot" stock tips beche, wo bechne wala hai, sikhaane wala nahi. SEBI-registered advice alag cheez hai — aur wo bhi guarantee nahi deta.

Galti 5: Poori capital ek idea par lagana. Chahe idea kitna bhi achha lage, chahe cycle kitna bhi saaf dikhe. Ek idea par poora paisa lagana trading nahi, jua hai. Seekhne ke liye paper trading karo — asli paisa tab lagao jab tumhe apni galtiyon ka hisaab ho.

4 Hafte Ka Seedha Plan

Hafta 1: Framework pakka karo. 2nd/11th/5th bhav, Jupiter-Saturn 20-saal cycle aur geocosmic timing ko ek kagaz par likh lo. Hamara Birth chart guide dobara padho — Lagna, bhav aur dasha revise karo. Saath me Merriman ka free weekly column aur Skinner ka Full Moon letter padhna shuru karo. Maqsad: kitaab kholne se pehle bhasha samajhna.

Hafta 2: Skinner ki The Beginner's Guide to the Financial Universe ke pehle chapters shuru karo — sunspot cycle aur seasonality. Har cycle ko pichhle saal ki market chart ke saath milakar dekho, par koi trade mat karo. Maqsad: cycle ko market se jodna.

Hafta 3: Merriman ki Basic Principles se ephemeris padhna seekho — aaj ke grahon ki sthiti nikalo, aur unke aspects dekho. Paper par ek chhota sa cycle diary banao: kaunsa aspect kab bana, market ne kya kiya. Maqsad: hisaab-kitab haath se karna.

Hafta 4: Ab ek raasta chuno — Gann (Ferrera ya Jenkins) ya Mundane (Mehta/Rao). Ek ko chuno, dono ko nahi. Aur agar Hindi me padhna ho to hamare Astrology books guide se Vedic buniyad aur pakki karo. Maqsad: ek raasta chunna aur usi par chalna.

Is plan ke baad tumhe khud pata chal jayega ki tumhe aage kya chahiye — cycles ki gehrai, ya desh ki kundli — aur tabhi aage ki kitaab chunna.
