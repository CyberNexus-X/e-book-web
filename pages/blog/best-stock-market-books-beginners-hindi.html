Stock Market Books for Beginners in Hindi – Kaunsi Book, Kab, Aur Kyun

November 5, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: Agar tum bilkul zero se stock market seekhna chahte ho, to pehle Peter Lynch ki Learn to Earn padho — wo ye samjhati hai ki share hota kya hai, company share kyun bechti hai, aur ek aam aadmi investor kaise banta hai. Uske baad Indian market ke liye Pranjal Kamra ki Investonomy (Hindi edition) ya Jitendra Gala ki Share Market Guide uthao. Aur usse bhi pehle, ek kaam free me karo: SEBI ya NSE ke investor education portal se Hindi booklet padh lo. Book khareedne se pehle buniyad free me pakki kar lena sabse samajhdaari wala kadam hai.

Honestly, stock market ki kitaabon ki dikkat communication ki kitaabon jaisi hi hai. Har list me wahi 10 naam hote hain, par koi ye nahi batata ki pehle kaunsi, kis ke liye, aur kaunsi abhi mat uthao. Ek list tumhe seedha The Intelligent Investor pakda degi — jo achhi book hai, par pehli book nahi. Doosri list tumhe trading ki kitaabon me dhakel degi, jab tumhe abhi ye bhi nahi pata ki delivery aur intraday me farq kya hai. Dono adhoore hain. Is article me 8 books hain, ek seedhe order me: buniyad, phir Indian market, phir classic, phir ek zaroori counterpoint. Aur har ek ke saath ye bhi likha hai ki ye kis ke liye nahi hai.

Ek baat pehle hi saaf kar dete hain, kyunki isme dhoka sabse zyada hai. Ye article education ke liye hai, investment advice nahi hai. Koi book, koi list, koi YouTube video tumhe ye guarantee nahi de sakta ki tumhara paisa badhega. Market me risk hota hai, aur loss bhi ho sakta hai. Jo book ya course "guaranteed return" ya "secret formula" ka dawa kare, usse door raho — chahe uska cover kitna bhi chamakdaar ho.

Pehle Ye Samajh Lo: Share Ka Matlab Kya Hai

Share ka matlab bahut seedha hai, par aksar log isko ulta samajh lete hain. Share khareedna matlab kisi company ka ek chhota sa hissa khareedna hai. Tum koi number ya line nahi khareed rahe. Tum ek asli business ka hissa khareed rahe ho — uski dukaan, uski factory, uske customer, uska munafa, uska karz, sab kuch thoda-thoda tumhara ho jaata hai. Isliye achhe investor share ka daam nahi, company ko dekhte hain.

Ek chhota sa example. Socho tumhare mohalle me ek chai ki dukaan hai jo roz bheed se chalti hai. Tum us dukaan ka hissa lena chahte ho. Tum kya dekhoge? Ki chai achhi hai ya nahi, customer wapas aate hain ya nahi, dukaan wale ka hisaab saaf hai ya nahi, aur us par kitna karz hai. Share market me bhi wahi kaam hai, bas scale bada hai. Jo insaan sirf daam ka graph dekh kar khareedta hai, wo dukaan dekhe bina uska hissa khareed raha hai. Kabhi-kabhi wo daam badh jaata hai, par ye samajhna zaroori hai ki wo kismat thi, strategy nahi.

Isi liye is list ka order aisa hai. Pehle buniyad — share kya hai. Phir Indian market ki mechanics — demat account kya hai, IPO kya hai, delivery aur intraday me farq kya hai, mutual fund share se kaise alag hai. Phir company ko samajhna — achhi company kaise pehchanein. Aur aakhir me ek counterpoint — ki kabhi-kabhi seedha index fund rakhna, stock chunne se behtar ho sakta hai. Ye aakhri baat bahut se beginners ko pasand nahi aati, par isi liye ye list me hai.

Free Me Shuru Karo: SEBI Aur NSE Ki Hindi Booklets

Book khareedne se pehle ek hafta free material par lagao. SEBI ka investor education portal aur NSE ka investor education section Hindi me chhoti-chhoti booklets aur modules dete hain — securities market ka intro, KYC, IPO, rights issue, corporate action, investor protection. Ye material boring lag sakta hai. Par yehi material sabse saaf hai, kyunki isme koi kuch bech nahi raha.

Ek side note, jo kaam aayega. Internet par "stock market books in Hindi free download" likh kar jo pehli PDF mile, usko mat uthao. Copyrighted kitaabon ki random PDF aksar illegal hoti hain, aur kabhi-kabhi adhoori ya galat bhi. Free chahiye to regulator ka free lo — SEBI, NSE, CBSE jaisi official jagah se. Unki site ka address .gov.in ya sebi.gov.in / nseindia.com jaisa hoga. Ye chhota sa check tumhe galat material se bacha lega.

8 Books, Ek Seedha Order

Neeche har book ke saath teen cheezein milengi: ye book kya sikhati hai, ye kis ke liye hai, aur isme dhyan rakhne wali baat kya hai. Order ko ulta mat karo, khaas kar pehli teen books ka.

1. Learn to Earn — Peter Lynch aur John Rothchild

Ye list ki sabse halki, aur isi liye pehli, book hai. Lynch batate hain ki capitalism kaise kaam karta hai, company kaise banti hai, wo share kyun jaari karti hai, aur ek aam insaan usme hissa kaise le sakta hai. Isme koi bhaari formula nahi hai. Isme wahi baat hai jo tumhe school me sikhani chahiye thi, par nahi sikhayi gayi.

Kis ke liye? Un logon ke liye jinhe share market ka "s" bhi nahi pata. Student ho, job karte ho, ya pehli baar invest karne ka soch rahe ho — agar tumhe lagta hai ki market sirf "tezi-mandi" ka khel hai, to ye book tumhari pehli book hai.

Dhyan rakhne wali baat: Ye ek English book hai, aur iska maqsad tumhe investor banana nahi, market ka dar khatam karna hai. Isko padh kar seedha paise lagane ki jaldi mat karo. Pehle doosri book se Indian mechanics samjho.

2. Investonomy — Pranjal Kamra (Hindi edition)

Ab Indian zameen par aao. Kamra ki book Indian reader ke liye likhi gayi hai — Indian market ke basics, aam galatfehmiyan, aur samajhdaari se shuru karne ka tareeka, seedhi bhasha me. Iska Hindi edition bhi milta hai, jo is list me isko khaas banata hai: tumhe concept samajhne ke liye English se ladna nahi padega.

Kis ke liye best hai? Un Hindi readers ke liye jo market ke baare me suna to bahut kuch hai, par abhi tak koi saaf raasta nahi mila. Agar tumhare ghar me koi kehta hai ki "share market jua hai", to is book ke baad tum us baat ka jawab samajh ke de paoge — na jhagde se, na dar se.

Ek imaandaar baat: Ye book shuruaat hai, aakhri manzil nahi. Iske baad tumhe company ko parakhna seekhna hoga, jo agle number ki kitaabon ka kaam hai. Bas Investonomy padh kar expert banne ka dawa mat karo.

3. Share Market Guide / Bhartiya Share Bazaar Ki Pehchan — Jitendra Gala (Hindi)

Ye book mechanics ki book hai. Primary aur secondary market kya hai, demat account kaise kaam karta hai, IPO kya hai, mutual fund kya hai, basic fundamental aur technical analysis kya hai, tax ka kya scene hai, aur agar koi dikkat aaye to shikayat kahan karein — ye sab ek jagah, Hindi me. Isi liye ye book doosre number ki saathi hai: Investonomy tumhe soch deti hai, Gala ki book tumhe tareeka deti hai.

Kis ke liye? Un logon ke liye jo abhi account kholne ya pehla kadam lene se pehle ye jaanna chahte hain ki system kaise chalta hai. Agar tumhe demat, delivery, intraday jaise shabd abhi bhi confuse karte hain, to ye book tumhare liye hai.

Dhyan rakhne wali baat: Khareedne se pehle edition check karo — Gala ki alag-alag kitaabon ke naam ek jaise lagte hain (Share Market Guide, Intraday Trading Ki Pehchan). Tumhe is stage par wahi chahiye jo basics par hai, intraday wali nahi. Naam dhyan se padho.

4. Stocks to Riches — Parag Parikh

Ab Indian investor ki psychology par aao. Parikh Indian investor ke liye likhte hain — Indian companies ko kaise dekhein, promoter ke behaviour ka kya matlab hai, regulatory badlav ka asar kya hota hai, aur bheed ke peeche bhaagne ki aadat se kaise bachein. Ye book tumhe ye sikhati hai ki market me sabse bada dushman bahar nahi, andar hai: hamari apni jaldi, hamara dar, aur hamari tip par bharosa karne ki aadat.

Ek chhota sa test: agar kisi share ko khareedne ki wajah sirf ye hai ki "sab khareed rahe hain" ya "ek video me bola tha", to wo wajah nahi hai, wo bheed hai. Aur bheed ke peeche bhaagne wala aksar sabse aakhir me pahunchta hai, jab daam upar ja chuka hota hai.

Kis ke liye nahi? Un logon ke liye nahi jo abhi share ka matlab bhi nahi jaante. Pehli teen kitaabon ke baad hi is par aao, warna iski baatein tumhe bhaari lagengi.

5. The Intelligent Investor — Benjamin Graham (1949)

Ye book 1949 me aayi thi, aur aaj bhi isliye chalti hai kyunki iska subject nahi badla: insaan ka behaviour. Graham ka idea ek line ka hai — share ko business ki tarah dekho, uski keemat se zyada uski asli value par dhyan do, aur khareedte waqt "margin of safety" rakho, matlab itni chhoot par khareedo ki agar tumhara andaaza thoda galat bhi ho, to bhi nuksaan seemit rahe. Aur sabse bada farq: investing aur speculation alag cheezein hain. Investing me tum business dekhte ho. Speculation me tum sirf daam ke upar-neeche par daav lagate ho.

Kis ke liye best hai? Un logon ke liye jo ab serious ho gaye hain aur lambi race ke liye sochna chahte hain. Iska Hindi edition bhi milta hai, par ek baat saaf rahe: ye pehli book nahi hai. Isko pehle padhne wale aksar beech me chhod dete hain, kyunki ye dheemi aur gehri book hai. Isko teesre ya chauthe number par padho, jab tumhe buniyad aa jaaye.

Dhyan rakhne wali baat: Is book ko cover-to-cover padhna zaroori nahi hai pehli baar me. Shuru ke chapters aur margin of safety wale hisse par dhyan do. Baaki baad me, jab tumhe company dekhne ka tajurba ho jaaye.

6. One Up on Wall Street — Peter Lynch

Lynch ka kehna hai ki aam aadmi ke paas ek faida hai jo professional ke paas nahi: wo roz duniya ko dekh raha hai. Tum jis dukaan par roz jaate ho, jis product ko log baar-baar khareedte hain, jis company ki service tum khud istemal karte ho — wahan se idea aa sakta hai. Par dhyan rahe: idea aana aur khareedna, dono alag cheezein hain. Idea ke baad company ka hisaab, karz, aur competition dekhna zaroori hai. Lynch ki book isi farq ko samjhati hai.

Kis ke liye? Un logon ke liye jo ab company ko samajhna chahte hain, na ki sirf tip sunna chahte hain. Agar tumhe lagta hai ki achha stock sirf TV par bataya jaata hai, to ye book tumhara nazariya badal degi.

7. Coffee Can Investing — Saurabh Mukherjea, Rakshit Ranjan, Pranab Uniyal

Ab Indian lambi race ki book. Coffee Can ka idea seedha hai: achhi quality ki, kam karz wali Indian companies chuno, aur unko lambi muddat tak pakde raho, baar-baar khareedne-bechne ke chakkar me mat pado. "Coffee can" ka naam isliye hai, kyunki purane zamane me log keemti cheezein coffee ke dibbe me rakh kar bhool jaate the. Ye book kehti hai ki portfolio ke saath bhi kabhi-kabhi wahi karna behtar hai — baar-baar haath mat lagao.

Kis ke liye best hai? Un logon ke liye jo job ya business me busy hain aur roz market dekhne ka waqt nahi nikaal sakte. Iska Hindi edition bhi milta hai.

Ek imaandaar baat: Is book ka idea lamba hai, aur lambi race me patience chahiye. Agar tumhe har hafte kuch naya karne ki aadat hai, to ye book tumhe boring lagegi. Par boring hi is book ki taakat hai — kam faisle, kam galtiyan.

8. A Random Walk Down Wall Street — Burton Malkiel (Counterpoint)

Aakhri book is list ki sabse zaroori hai, kyunki ye baaki saari kitaabon se alag baat kehti hai. Malkiel ka kehna hai ki market kaafi had tak efficient hai, aur zyada tar active fund manager apne benchmark ko lagatar hara nahi paate. Isliye ek aam investor ke liye kabhi-kabhi sabse samajhdaari wala rasta ye hai ki wo index fund jaise seedhe option par tik jaaye, har share ko chunne ki koshish me apna waqt aur paisa na gavaye.

Ye book is list me isliye hai, taaki tum ek taraf ki kahani na suno. Pehli 7 kitaabon se tumhe lagega ki stock chunna seekhna hi sab kuch hai. Ye 8vi book tumhe ruk kar sochne par majboor karti hai: kya mujhe stock chunna hai, ya mujhe bas market ke saath lambi race me tikna hai? Dono raaste galat nahi hain. Par faisla tumhara hona chahiye, kisi list ka nahi. Aur haan, jo raasta tum chuno, uske baad bhi ye article ki baat yaad rakhna: ye education hai, investment advice nahi.

Ye Books Abhi Mat Lo — Aur Kyun

Ab teen galtiyan jo har beginner karta hai, aur jinse ye list tumhe bachana chahti hai.

Pehli: pehle din se trading ki book. Intraday Trading Ki Pehchan, Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets, Trading in the Zone — ye sab apni jagah achhi kitaabein ho sakti hain, par pehli kitaabein nahi. Trading me waqt, anushasan aur nuksaan sehne ki taakat chahiye. Buniyad ke bina trading ki book tumhe sirf chart ke pattern degi, faisle lene ki samajh nahi.

Doosri: tip aur guaranteed return wali kitaabein. Jo cover par likhe ki "30 din me double" ya "secret formula", usko mat uthao. Market me koi secret formula nahi hai. Jo hai, wo sab in 8 kitaabon me khula likha hai: business samjho, risk samjho, patience rakho.

Teesri: ek saath saari kitaabein. 8 kitaabein khareed kar shelf par sajana padhna nahi kehlata. Ek book lo, uska ek idea samjho, phir agli. Stock market ki book ka test ye nahi hai ki tumne kitne page padhe. Test ye hai ki tumne kitni baar tip par bhaagne ki jagah ruk kar socha.

4 Hafte Ka Seedha Plan

Hafta 1: SEBI/NSE ki Hindi booklet + Learn to Earn. Maqsad: dar khatam karna, share ka matlab samajhna.

Hafta 2: Investonomy ya Share Market Guide. Maqsad: Indian mechanics — demat, IPO, delivery vs intraday, mutual fund ka farq.

Hafta 3: Stocks to Riches. Maqsad: apni psychology — bheed, dar, jaldi — ko pehchanna.

Hafta 4: The Intelligent Investor ke shuru ke chapters, aur phir Malkiel ka counterpoint. Maqsad: faisla lena — stock chunna hai ya index ke saath tikna hai?

Is plan ke baad One Up on Wall Street aur Coffee Can Investing apne aap sahi jagah par aa jaayengi, kyunki tab tumhe pata hoga ki tum kis raaste par ho.

Hindi reader ke liye sabse seedha rasta ye hai: Investonomy (Hindi edition) aur Gala ki Hindi kitaabon se shuru karo, aur classic kitaabon ke Hindi edition tab uthao jab tumhe buniyad aa jaaye. Khareedne se pehle hamesha edition aur anuvaadak check karo — naam ek jaise hote hain, anuvaad alag-alag hota hai. Aur agar kisi book ka Hindi edition na mile, to usko chhod kar aisi book uthao jo Hindi me saaf mile, bajaye iske ki tum English se ladte raho aur book adhoori chhod do.

To aaj ka kaam bahut chhota hai. Aaj SEBI ya NSE ke portal se ek Hindi booklet kholo, aur sirf demat aur IPO wale hisse padh lo. Bas. Na koi account kholna hai, na koi share khareedna hai. Kal phir wahi karo, agle hisse ke saath. Ek hafte tak ye aadat chalao. Bas itna hi kaafi hai shuruaat ke liye.
