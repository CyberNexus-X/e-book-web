Medical Astrology Books: Rog Jyotish Sikhne Ki Best Kitaabein — Beginner Guide

November 9, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: Medical astrology sikhne ki pehli kitaab Dr. K.S. Charak ki Essentials of Medical Astrology hai — ye is vishay ki standard kitaab mani jati hai, aur iske lekhak ek surgeon bhi hain. Par usse pehle ek shart hai: birth chart ki buniyad pakki honi chahiye. Lagna, 12 bhav aur dasha samajh lo, phir ye kitaab uthao. Seedha isse shuru karoge, to bhasha bhi bhaari lagegi aur niyam bhi. Is article me wahi order hai — pehle framework, phir kitaabein, aur sabse pehle ek seedha niyam jo poori article me baar-baar aayega.

Honestly, Rog Jyotish sabse zyada galat samjha jaane wala hissa hai. Log kundli kholte hain, 6th house me Shani dekhte hain, aur dar jaate hain. Ya koi "upay" bechne wala ek placement sun kar ₹5,000 ki report thama deta hai. Dono galat hain. Medical astrology ek paramparik adhyayan pranali hai — isse grah, rashi, bhav aur dasha ka dhancha samajh sakte ho, apne swasthya par chintan kar sakte ho. Par ye vigyan se siddh diagnosis nahi hai. Koi kitaab, koi upay, koi ratna bimari ka ilaaj nahi kar sakta. Asli sehat ki chinta ho, to doctor — chart nahi.

Ye baat isliye pehle likh raha hoon, kyunki is vishay me galti ki keemat zyada hoti hai. Career wali galti se ek saal kharab hota hai. Sehat wali galti se zindagi. Isliye is article ki har kitaab ke saath ye bhi likha hai ki wo kis kaam ki hai — aur kis kaam ki nahi.

Pehle Ye Samajh Lo: Rog Jyotish Kya Hai, Kya Nahi Hai

Medical astrology, ya Rog Jyotish, Vedic astrology ki wo shakha hai jo sehat ko chart se padhti hai — kaunse bhav, grah, rashi aur dasha shareer se judte hain, aur sehat kab kamzor pad sakti hai ya kab sudhar sakta hai. Iska kaam ek naksha dena hai, faisla dena nahi.

Ek example se samjho. Socho tumhari chart me 6th house ka swami kamzor hai. Paramparik padhne me iska matlab ye hota hai ki sehat par dhyaan dena chahiye — neend, khana, roz ki aadat. Iska matlab ye nahi hai ki tumhe koi khaas bimari hogi. Aur ye to bilkul nahi hai ki tum doctor ko chhod kar koi ratna pehen lo. Framework ka kaam hai sawal uthana, jawab dena nahi.

Aur ek baat jo beginners aksar miss karte hain: medical astrology me "ek grah = ek bimari" wali soch nahi chalti. Parampara me grah ko rashi, bhav, drishti aur dasha ke saath mil kar padha jata hai — aur tab bhi nateeja ek sambhavna hoti hai, diagnosis nahi. Jo kitaab ya vyakti ek placement se bimari ka naam bata de, usse door raho.

To Rog Jyotish kya nahi hai? Ye doctor nahi hai. Ye lab report nahi hai. Ye ilaaj nahi hai. Ye ek adhyayan hai — jaise itihaas padhna. Ise usi nazariye se padho, to ye kaam ka hai. Dar ya lalach se padho, to yehi vishay tumhe uljha dega.

Framework: 6th House, Kalapurusha Aur Grah — Kitaab Se Pehle Ye Samjho

Kitaab uthane se pehle teen cheezein samajh lo. Ye teenon is article ki kitaabon me baar-baar aayengi, isliye yahin se shuruaat karte hain.

Pehla: bhav. Sehat ka mukhya bhav 6th house hai — isko Rog sthan kehte hain. Uske saath 8th house lambi umar aur gambhir ya lambi bimari se judta hai, aur 12th house aspatal, kharch aur neend se. Lagna (1st house) aur uska swami poori vitality dikhate hain — shareer ki buniyadi taakat. In teen bhavon ko Trika bhav (6, 8, 12) kehte hain, aur medical astrology me inka sabse zyada zikr hota hai.

Doosra: Kalapurusha. Rashi chakra ko ek shareer ki tarah dekha jata hai — har rashi shareer ke ek hisse se judti hai. Mesha (Aries) — sir; Vrishabh (Taurus) — gala aur gardan; Mithun (Gemini) — kandhe aur baahein; Karka (Cancer) — chhati; Simha (Leo) — dil aur peeth; Kanya (Virgo) — pet aur pachan; Tula (Libra) — kidney aur kamar; Vrishchik (Scorpio) — reproductive organs; Dhanu (Sagittarius) — jangh aur liver; Makar (Capricorn) — ghutne aur haddiyan; Kumbh (Aquarius) — takhne aur circulation; Meen (Pisces) — pair. Ye table yaad rakhna — kitaab padhte waqt har doosre page par ye kaam aayega.

Teesra: grah. Har grah ka apna shareer se rishta hai: Surya — dil, aankhein, vitality; Chandra — mann aur shareer ke fluids; Mangal — khoon aur muscles; Budha — nerves aur skin; Guru — liver aur fat; Shukra — reproductive system; Shani — haddiyan, joints aur lambi bimari; Rahu-Ketu — ajeeb, lambi ya mushkil se samajh aane wali sthiti. Ye paramparik significations hain — inko medical facts ki tarah mat padho, framework ki tarah padho.

Ab jab ye teenon dimaag me hain, to kitaabein samajh aayengi. Aur agar ye teenon abhi bhi naye lag rahe hain, to pehle hamara Birth chart reading guide padh lo — usme Lagna, 12 bhav aur dasha detail me hain. Uske bina medical astrology ki kitaab uthana, bina map ke sheher me ghumne jaisa hai.

Level 1: Pehli Kitaab — Essentials of Medical Astrology, Dr. K.S. Charak

Agar sirf ek kitaab leni ho, to yehi lo. Dr. K.S. Charak ki Essentials of Medical Astrology is vishay ki standard kitaab mani jati hai — ab apne chauthe edition me — aur iski sabse badi khaasiyat ye hai ki iske lekhak ek surgeon hain. Matlab, ye kitaab ek aise vyakti ne likhi hai jo shareer ko bhi jaanta hai aur chart ko bhi.

Kitaab kya sikhati hai? Chart ko bimari ke nazariye se kaise padha jaye — iske vistrit siddhant. Isme kuch aise sookshm tools bhi hain jo aam astrology kitaabon me nahi milte: 22nd Drekkana, 64th Navamsha, Sarpa Drekkana, aur Gulika ya Mandi — ye bimari ke shuru hone aur uske nateeje ko samajhne ke liye istemal hote hain. Pehli baar padhne par ye naam ajeeb lagenge. Darna mat — kitaab inko dheere-dheere, case ke saath samjhati hai.

Ye kitaab kiske liye hai? Us reader ke liye jo birth chart padhna jaanta hai aur ab sehat ke hisse ko gehrai se samajhna chahta hai. Agar tumne abhi tak Lagna aur bhav nahi samjhe, to ye kitaab bhaari lagegi — aur tab sahi raasta ye hai ki pehle hamare Astrology books guide se buniyad banao, phir yahan aao.

Ek aur baat. Charak ki kitaab padhte waqt ek niyam yaad rakhna: kitaab me jo case studies hain, wo pehle se jaani hui bimari wale logon ki charts hain — unko peeche se padha gaya hai. Iska matlab ye nahi hai ki tum aage se kisi ki chart dekh kar bimari bata sakte ho. Peeche se samajhna aur aage se bolna, do alag cheezein hain.

Level 2: Gehrai — Subtleties of Medical Astrology Aur Choudhry Ki Case-Study Kitaab

Jab Essentials haath me aa jaye, tab do raaste khulte hain. Dono ek saath mat uthao — ek ko chuno.

Subtleties of Medical Astrology — Dr. K.S. Charak

Ye Charak ki doosri, zyada advanced kitaab hai — ab apne doosre edition me. Isme wo cheezein hain jo pehli kitaab me nahi thi: vakri (retrograde) grahon ka sehat par asar, malefic yog, Trika bhavon (6/8/12) aur unke swamiyon ki bhoomika, 22nd Drekkana aur 64th Navamsha ki gehrai, aur Mrityu Bhaga naam ka ek kam jaana jaane wala hissa. Isme ek hi ghatna ko confirm karne ke liye teen dasha ek saath istemal ki gayi hain — Parashari system se Vimshottari aur Yogini, aur Jaimini system se Chara dasha.

Ye kitaab kiske liye hai? Uske liye jo Essentials padh chuka hai aur ab gehrai chahta hai. Beginner ke taur par isse shuru mat karo — ye kitaab isliye likhi gayi hai ki pehli kitaab ke baad padhi jaye.

A Complete Book on Medical Astrology — V.K. Choudhry aur K. Rajesh Chaudhary

Doosra raasta hai V.K. Choudhry aur K. Rajesh Chaudhary ki A Complete Book on Medical Astrology: Principles and Case Studies (Sagar Publications, 2023 edition). Ye Choudhry ke Systems' Approach par likhi gayi hai — ek step-by-step tareeka jo chart ko padhne me confusion kam karta hai.

Is kitaab ki khaasiyat iski case studies hain: grah, rashi aur bhav ke significations ke baad, gambhir bimari aur recovery ka samay kaise nikala jaye, aur preventive astral remedies kaise istemal kiye jayein — ye sab case ke saath samjhaya gaya hai. Kitaab ke chapters is order me hain: concepts, rashiyon ke significations, bhavon ke significations, grahon ke significations, advanced techniques, health problem pehchanna, gambhir bimari aur recovery ka samay, upay, aur phir lambi case-study section.

Kaunsi chunein — Subtleties ya Choudhry? Agar tumhe classical niyamon ki gehrai chahiye, to Subtleties. Agar tumhe step-by-step system aur case studies se seekhna pasand hai, to Choudhry. Dono sahi hain. Dono ek saath nahi.

Alag Nazariya: Western Medical Astrology — Eileen Nauman Ki Kitaab

Ab ek alag nazariya. Eileen Nauman ki Medical Astrology (pehli baar July 1982 me, phir 1993 aur 1996 me revised) Western medical astrology ki sabse jaani-mani kitaab hai. Ye Vedic system se alag hai — tropical system par hai — aur isme do hisse hain.

Pehla hissa astrology guide hai: grah, rashi, crosses, bhav, hard aspects, midpoints, aur Nauman ki apni Med-Scan technique — chart se sehat ka ek structured tareeka. Saath me saat case histories hain aur medical transits par detail hai. Doosra hissa nutrition guide hai: vitamins, minerals, homeopathy, Bach Flower Remedies, endocrine system, herbs aur cell salts.

Is kitaab ki ek baat khaas taur par yaad rakhne layak hai: Nauman ne apni preface me khud likha hai ki is kitaab ki jaankari competent medical advice ki jagah nahi le sakti. Yehi imandari is kitaab ko bharosemand banati hai. Vedic kitaabein padhne ke baad isko padho — do alag paramparaen compare karne se dono zyada samajh aati hain. Par pehle Vedic, phir Western. Dono ko ek saath mila kar mat padho.

Hindi Me Padhna Ho To — Rog Jyotish Ki Hindi Kitaabein

Ab Hindi readers ke liye. Agar tumhe English kitaab bhaari lagti hai, to ye do vikalp hain.

Rog Jyotish Upchar Sahit (रोग ज्योतिष उपचार सहित) — Acharya Vivekashri Kaushik 'Vishwamitra'. Ye Hindi me medical astrology ki kitaab hai, aur Hindi beginners ke liye sabse seedha raasta. Isme rog aur upay dono hain — par upay ko hamesha usi nazariye se padho jo is article me likha hai: upay ek paramparik abhyas hai, ilaaj nahi.

Medical Astrology — Dr. Deepak Singla. Ye Vedic guide shareerik aur mansik sehat dono par hai, aur isme upay bhi diye gaye hain. Hindi readers ke liye likhi gayi hai, isliye bhasha aasan hai.

Do aur naam, short me. Prof. N.E. Muthuswamy ki Medical Astrology, Volume 1: Principles and Case Studies (CBH Publications, 2009) — ek aur case-study wali kitaab, jo medical astrology ki badi bibliography me darj hai. Aur Poonam Sainani ki Medical Astrology — ek practical guide jo chart se shareer ko padhne par hai, aur khud ko textbook nahi, working reference kehti hai. Ye dono tab uthao jab pehli kitaab padh li ho — shuruaat ke liye nahi.

Abhi Mat Uthao — Classical Granth Aur Ek Galat Soch

Ek cheez jo har beginner karta hai: seedha granth uthana. Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra aur Brihat Jataka / Brihat Samhita (Varahamihira) me rog aur sehat ke niyam hain — yehi wo mool granth hain jinse Charak aur Choudhry jaise lekhakon ne apne niyam liye hain. Par inko seedha padhna galat order hai. Granth galat nahi hai. Order galat hai.

Pehle Charak ki Essentials padho, phir granth. Granth tab samajh aayega jab tumhe pata hoga ki tum kya dhoondh rahe ho. Aur granthon ka poora order — kaunsi pehle, kaunsi baad me — hamare Astrology books guide me pehle se hai, isliye yahan dobara nahi likh rahe.

Aur doosri cheez jo abhi mat karo: kisi ki chart dekh kar bimari ka naam bolna. Chahe tumne Essentials ke pehle do chapter padh liye hon. Ye vishay aisa hai jisme aadha gyaan, koi gyaan na hone se zyada khatarnak hai. Seekho, practice karo, par faisla mat sunao — na apne liye, na kisi aur ke liye.

Ye 5 Galtiyan Mat Karo

Ab wo galtiyan, jo is vishay me sabse zyada hoti hain. Inse bach gaye, to aadha kaam ho gaya.

Galti 1: Chart ko diagnosis samajhna. Chart ek naksha hai, lab report nahi. Ek placement dekh kar bimari ka naam bolna galat hai — chahe wo placement kitni bhi saaf kyun na lage.

Galti 2: Doctor ki salah delay karna. Agar shareer me kuch galat lag raha hai, to pehle doctor. Chart baad me. Koi bhi upay, ratna ya kitaab doctor ki jagah nahi le sakta — aur jo ye dawa kare, usse door raho.

Galti 3: Ek grah se bimari tay karna. "Shani 6th me hai to ye bimari hogi" — aisa ek line me kehna galat hai. Grah kis rashi me hai, uska swami kahan hai, drishti kya hai, dasha kaunsi chal rahi hai — ye sab dekhe bina koi faisla nahi banta.

Galti 4: Upay ya ratna ko ilaaj samajhna. Upay ek paramparik abhyas hai — jaise kisi ki aadat. Ye ilaaj nahi hai, aur ye bimari ko rok nahi sakta. Jo upay ko guarantee ki tarah beche, wo bechne wala hai, sikhaane wala nahi.

Galti 5: Doosron ki chart par prediction dena. Apni chart par seekhna ek baat hai. Kisi aur ko uski sehat ke baare me bolna doosri baat hai — aur isme galti ki keemat bahut zyada hoti hai. Jab tak tumhe saalon ka abhyas na ho, ye mat karo.

4 Hafte Ka Seedha Plan

Hafta 1: Framework pakka karo. 6th/8th/12th bhav, Kalapurusha table aur grah-significations ko ek kagaz par likh lo. Hamara Birth chart guide dobara padho — Lagna, bhav aur dasha revise karo. Maqsad: kitaab kholne se pehle bhasha samajhna.

Hafta 2: Charak ki Essentials of Medical Astrology ke pehle chapters shuru karo — introduction aur basic principles. Har niyam ko apni chart par lagakar dekho, par koi faisla mat sunao. Maqsad: niyam ko chart se jodna.

Hafta 3: Case studies padho — pehle Choudhry ki kitaab ki, ya Essentials ki. Case ko peeche se padho: bimari pata hai, chart kya kehti thi? Maqsad: peeche se samajhna, aage se bolna nahi.

Hafta 4: Ab ek advanced kitaab chuno — Charak ki Subtleties ya Choudhry ki poori kitaab. Ek ko chuno, dono ko nahi. Aur Hindi me padhna ho to Vishwamitra ki Rog Jyotish ko saath me rakho. Maqsad: ek raasta chunna aur usi par chalna.

Is plan ke baad tumhe khud pata chal jayega ki tumhe aage kya chahiye — dasha ki gehrai, ya divisional chart — aur tabhi aage ki kitaab chunna.
