Zodiac Signs Guide: 12 Rashiyan, Unki Dates, Elements Aur Traits — Apni Rashi Sahi Tareeke Se Pehchano

November 13, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: apni rashi nikalne ke liye pehle apni janm tareekh lo aur neeche wali table me dekho — par ek baat turant samajh lo: newspaper wala sun sign aur tumhari rashi, dono ek hi cheez nahi hain. India me jab koi kehta hai "meri rashi Mesh hai", to uska matlab aam taur par Moon sign hota hai — janm ke samay Chandra kis rashi me tha. Newspaper wale horoscope me Sun sign use hota hai — janm ke samay Surya kis rashi me tha. Dono alag ho sakte hain. Aur upar se ek aur farak: Western dates aur Vedic dates me lagbhag 24 din ka antar hai. Isi liye ek hi birthday par ek jagah tum Aries dikhte ho, doosri jagah Meena. Is article ka kaam yehi hai — ye saari confusion ek hi jagah khatam karna.

Honestly, zodiac signs ko lekar sabse zyada confusion isiliye hai kyunki har jagah aadhi baat likhi hoti hai. English site Western dates deti hai, Hindi site sirf rashi ke naam deti hai, aur rashifal wale column ye batate hi nahi ki wo kis sign se padh rahe hain. To chalo, shuruaat se — ekdum seedhi bhasha me, bina kisi jargon ke — poori picture samajhte hain. Aakhir me tumhe pata hoga: 12 rashiyan kaunsi hain, unki dates kya hain (dono systems me), har rashi ka element aur ruler kaunsa hai, aur apni rashi kaise nikalni hai — sahi tareeke se.

Ek imandari wali baat pehle hi saaf kar doon. Astrology ek sanskritik parampara aur vishwas ka framework hai — studies me is baat ka koi scientific saboot nahi mila ki sun signs personality ya events predict karte hain. Is article me jo traits likhe hain, wo parampara ke hisaab se hain — ek starting sketch, khud ko samajhne ke liye. Inhe apni pehchan ka rulebook mat banao, aur inke basis par shaadi, naukri, paisa ya sehat jaise faisle kabhi mat lo. Theek hai? Ab shuru karte hain.

Rashi Kya Hai — Aur Sun Sign, Moon Sign, Lagna Me Farak

Zodiac ek wheel hai — aasmaan ka ek circle, jo 12 barabar hisson me banta hai. Har hissa 30 degree ka hota hai, aur har hisse ka ek naam hai — Mesha se Meena tak. Surya saal bhar me is wheel ka ek poora chakkar lagata hai, har rashi me lagbhag ek mahina rehta hai. Isi liye tumhari janm tareekh se pata chal jata hai ki us din Surya kis rashi me tha — wahi tumhara Sun sign hai.

Par Jyotish me kahani yahin khatam nahi hoti. Tumhare chart me teen cheezein sabse zyada dekhi jati hain — aur in teeno ko mix karna hi sabse badi galti hai:

Ye teeno alag-alag cheezein hain, aur teeno ka apna kaam hai. Sun sign sirf janm tareekh se nikal jata hai. Moon sign ke liye janm tareekh ke saath janm ka samay aur sthan chahiye — kyunki Chandra har 2.25 din me rashi badal deta hai, isliye date table se Moon sign nahi nikalta. Aur Lagna har do ghante me badalta hai — iske liye exact janm samay chahiye hota hai. Jyotish me Lagna aur Moon sign, Sun sign se zyada important mane jate hain. Isi liye hamare Birth chart reading guide me pehla kadam Lagna nikalna hai, Sun sign nahi.

Ek chhota sa example lo. Maan lo kisi ka birthday 1 April hai. Western sun sign ke hisaab se wo Aries hai — Surya us din Mesha me tha, tropical system me. Par Vedic system me us din Surya abhi Meena me tha, kyunki Vedic dates lagbhag 24 din peeche chalti hain. Aur uska Moon sign — uski asli rashi — kuch aur bhi ho sakta hai, jo sirf uske janm samay se pata chalega. To "main kaunsi rashi hoon" ka jawab ek shabd me nahi milta. Ye article tumhe wahi teenon jawab alag-alag nikalna sikhata hai.

Western vs Vedic — Dates Me 24 Din Ka Farak Kyun Hai

Ye article ka sabse important section hai, kyunki yahin se zyadatar confusion shuru hoti hai. Western astrology tropical zodiac use karti hai — ye spring equinox (vasant vishuv) se bandha hai, matlab mausam se. Vedic astrology sidereal zodiac use karti hai — ye taaron se bandha hai, matlab asli nakshatron se. Dono ke beech ka antar ayanamsa kehlata hai. 1 January 2026 ko Lahiri ayanamsa 24°13′ tha, aur ye har saal lagbhag 50 arc-seconds badhta hai. Seedhi bhasha me: Vedic dates, Western dates se lagbhag 24 din baad chalti hain.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki ek system galat hai aur doosra sahi. Dono alag tareeke hain — ek mausam se bandha, ek taaron se. India me Vedic system chalta hai, isliye tumhare ghar me jo rashi boli jati hai, wo aam taur par Vedic dates se milti hai. Newspaper aur English apps me Western dates milti hain. Dono ko ek saath samajhna ho, to neeche wali table kaam aayegi — isme har rashi ki dono dates, element, ruler aur symbol ek hi jagah hain.

12 Rashiyan, Ek-Ek Karke — Traits, Taakat Aur Dhyaan Rakhne Wali Baat

Ab har rashi ko ek-ek karke dekhte hain. Har rashi ke liye: Hindi naam, symbol, element, ruler, aur parampara me uski pehchan. Traits ko "tum aise hi ho" ki tarah mat padho — inhe "is rashi ki energy aisi mani jati hai" ki tarah padho. Ek insaan poori kundli hota hai, ek rashi nahi.

1. Mesha (मेष) — Aries ♈, The Ram

Dates: Western 21 Mar – 19 Apr; Vedic 14 Apr – 14 May. Element: Fire. Ruler: Mars (Mangal). Quality: Movable (Chara).

Mesha zodiac ki pehli rashi hai — shuruaat ki energy. Parampara me iski pehchan hai himmat, seedha bolna, aur pehle kadam uthana. Mesh rashi wale log aam taur par jaldi decide karte hain aur kaam ko shuru karne me der nahi karte. Taakat: initiative aur courage. Dhyaan rakho: jaldi gussa aur bina soche faisla — yehi iski sabse aam kamzori mani jati hai. Agar tumhara Lagna ya Moon sign Mesha hai, to tum me ye energy zyada dikhegi; sirf Sun sign Mesha hone se poori kahani nahi banti.

2. Vrishabha (वृषभ) — Taurus ♉, The Bull

Dates: Western 20 Apr – 20 May; Vedic 15 May – 14 Jun. Element: Earth. Ruler: Venus (Shukra). Quality: Fixed (Sthira).

Vrishabha stability ki rashi hai — dhyaan se, aaram se, par pakka. Parampara me iski pehchan hai patience, loyalty, aur achhi cheezon ki kadar karna — khana, music, comfort. Taakat: steadiness aur bharosa — jo kaam haath me lete hain, use chhodte nahi. Dhyaan rakho: zidd aur change se parhez. Vrishabha ko badlav pasand nahi hota, par jab badlav zaroori ho, to der karna nuksan karta hai.

3. Mithuna (मिथुन) — Gemini ♊, The Twins

Dates: Western 21 May – 20 Jun; Vedic 15 Jun – 16 Jul. Element: Air. Ruler: Mercury (Budha). Quality: Dual (Dwiswabhava).

Mithuna curiosity ki rashi hai — baat-cheet, seekhna, aur nayi jaankari. Twins ka symbol iski dual nature dikhata hai: ek hi waqt me do taraf dekhna. Taakat: communication aur adaptability — naye log, nayi jagah, nayi baat, sab me aaram se ghul-mil jana. Dhyaan rakho: ek kaam par tikna. Mithuna ke liye sabse badi mushkil yehi hai — das cheezein shuru karna, aur kisi ek ko khatam na karna.

4. Karka (कर्क) — Cancer ♋, The Crab

Dates: Western 21 Jun – 22 Jul; Vedic 17 Jul – 16 Aug. Element: Water. Ruler: Moon (Chandra). Quality: Movable (Chara).

Karka emotions aur ghar ki rashi hai — care, protection, aur apno ke liye fikr. Crab ka symbol bilkul sahi hai: bahar se sakht shell, andar se naram. Taakat: intuition aur emotional depth — logon ki feelings jaldi samajhna. Dhyaan rakho: mood swings aur purani baaton ko pakad kar rakhna. Karka ko bhoolna mushkil hota hai — achhi baat bhi, buri baat bhi.

5. Simha (सिंह) — Leo ♌, The Lion

Dates: Western 23 Jul – 22 Aug; Vedic 17 Aug – 16 Sep. Element: Fire. Ruler: Sun (Surya). Quality: Fixed (Sthira).

Simha leadership aur expression ki rashi hai — stage, creativity, aur apni pehchan. Surya iska ruler hai, aur jaise Surya centre me hota hai, waise hi Simha ko centre me rehna pasand hai. Taakat: confidence, generosity, aur bade dil se dena. Dhyaan rakho: ego aur tareef ki zaroorat. Simha ko appreciation chahiye hoti hai — aur jab nahi milti, to wo andar hi andar kha jati hai.

6. Kanya (कन्या) — Virgo ♍, The Maiden

Dates: Western 23 Aug – 22 Sep; Vedic 17 Sep – 16 Oct. Element: Earth. Ruler: Mercury (Budha). Quality: Dual (Dwiswabhava).

Kanya detail aur kaam ki rashi hai — analysis, safai, aur cheezon ko theek karna. Parampara me iski pehchan hai practical thinking aur service — jo kaam haath me aaye, use behtar banana. Taakat: observation aur discipline — chhoti galti bhi nazar aa jati hai. Dhyaan rakho: overthinking aur perfectionism. Kanya ke liye sabse badi mushkil yehi hai — kaam ko "kaafi achha" manna, "perfect" ka peeche bhaagna nahi.

7. Tula (तुला) — Libra ♎, The Scales

Dates: Western 23 Sep – 22 Oct; Vedic 17 Oct – 15 Nov. Element: Air. Ruler: Venus (Shukra). Quality: Movable (Chara).

Tula balance aur relationships ki rashi hai — tarazu ka symbol hi iski kahani hai: har cheez ko tolna, dono taraf dekhna, aur faisla lene se pehle sochna. Taakat: diplomacy aur fairness — jhagde ko suljhana, dono pakshon ko samajhna. Dhyaan rakho: faisla lene me der. Tula itna tolti hai ki kabhi-kabhi faisla hi nahi le pati — aur "sab khush rahen" ki koshish me khud ki pasand bhool jati hai.

8. Vrishchika (वृश्चिक) — Scorpio ♏, The Scorpion

Dates: Western 23 Oct – 21 Nov; Vedic 16 Nov – 15 Dec. Element: Water. Ruler: Mars (Mangal). Quality: Fixed (Sthira).

Vrishchika intensity aur transformation ki rashi hai — gehrai, research, aur chhupi hui cheezon ko dekhna. Parampara me iski pehchan hai focus aur loyalty — jo iske kareeb hain, unke liye ye poori tarah khadi rehti hai. Taakat: determination aur depth — satah par nahi, gehrai me jana. Dhyaan rakho: possessiveness aur secretiveness. Vrishchika ko sab kuch control me rakhna pasand hai — aur yehi aadat kabhi-kabhi iski sabse badi mushkil ban jati hai.

9. Dhanu (धनु) — Sagittarius ♐, The Archer

Dates: Western 22 Nov – 21 Dec; Vedic 16 Dec – 13 Jan. Element: Fire. Ruler: Jupiter (Guru). Quality: Dual (Dwiswabhava).

Dhanu exploration aur philosophy ki rashi hai — teer ka symbol bilkul sahi hai: hamesha aage, door ki taraf. Parampara me iski pehchan hai optimism, seekhne ki bhookh, aur badi soch. Taakat: honesty aur vision — chhoti baaton me nahi ulajhna, badi picture dekhna. Dhyaan rakho: blunt bolna aur commitment se bhaagna. Dhanu ko aazadi pasand hai — aur kabhi-kabhi is aazadi ke peeche, zimmedari bhool jati hai.

10. Makara (मकर) — Capricorn ♑, The Sea-Goat

Dates: Western 22 Dec – 19 Jan; Vedic 14 Jan – 12 Feb. Element: Earth. Ruler: Saturn (Shani). Quality: Movable (Chara).

Makara discipline aur long-term kaam ki rashi hai — pahad chadhne wali energy, slow par steady. Shani iska ruler hai — karma aur mehnat ka grah — isliye Makara ko shortcut pasand nahi. Taakat: patience aur ambition — saalon ka plan bana kar chalna. Dhyaan rakho: kaam me itna doob jana ki zindagi ke baaki hisse chhoot jayein. Makara ke liye sabse badi seekh yehi hai — pahad chadhna theek hai, par raaste me ruk kar saans lena bhi zaroori hai.

11. Kumbha (कुंभ) — Aquarius ♒, The Water-Bearer

Dates: Western 20 Jan – 18 Feb; Vedic 13 Feb – 13 Mar. Element: Air. Ruler: Saturn (Shani). Quality: Fixed (Sthira).

Kumbha originality aur samaj ki rashi hai — naye ideas, bade sapne, aur bheed se alag sochna. Ghada liye hue aadmi ka symbol iski kahani hai: paani sabko dena, par khud alag rehna. Taakat: independent thinking aur idealism — jo sab kehte hain, use blindly follow nahi karna. Dhyaan rakho: emotional distance. Kumbha ko ideas se pyaar hota hai, logon se utna nahi — aur kabhi-kabhi ye doori, akelepan me badal jati hai.

12. Meena (मीन) — Pisces ♓, The Fish

Dates: Western 19 Feb – 20 Mar; Vedic 14 Mar – 13 Apr. Element: Water. Ruler: Jupiter (Guru). Quality: Dual (Dwiswabhava).

Meena zodiac ki aakhri rashi hai — imagination, compassion, aur sapne. Do machhliyan, opposite disha me tairti hui, iski dual nature dikhati hain: ek taraf duniya, doosri taraf sapne. Taakat: empathy aur creativity — doosron ka dard samajhna, aur use art ya madad me badalna. Dhyaan rakho: reality se bhaagna. Meena ko sapno me rehna pasand hai — par jab zindagi mushkil hoti hai, to sapne me chhup jana, hal nahi hota.

4 Elements — 12 Rashiyon Ko Yaad Rakhne Ka Sabse Aasan Tareeka

12 rashiyan yaad rakhna mushkil lagta hai, par ek shortcut hai — 4 elements. Har element me 3 rashiyan aati hain, aur har element ka ek mood hota hai. Ye yaad rakh lo, to aadhi list yaad ho jayegi.

Fire (Agni) — Mesha, Simha, Dhanu. Energy, action, aur enthusiasm. Fire signs ko kaam karna pasand hai — shuru karna, aage badhna, aur roshni me rehna. Inki taakat hai passion; inki mushkil hai patience. Agar tumhare chart me fire zyada hai, to tumhe rukna seekhna hoga — har kaam sprint nahi hota, kuch kaam marathon hote hain.

Earth (Prithvi) — Vrishabha, Kanya, Makara. Stability, practicality, aur results. Earth signs ko real cheezein pasand hain — paisa, kaam, ghar, aur wo sab jo haath me aaye. Inki taakat hai reliability; inki mushkil hai flexibility. Earth signs ko badlav se darr lagta hai, par zindagi badlav hi hai — ye seekhna inke liye sabse zaroori hai.

Air (Vayu) — Mithuna, Tula, Kumbha. Ideas, communication, aur log. Air signs ko sochna aur bolna pasand hai — naye ideas, naye log, nayi baatein. Inki taakat hai intellect; inki mushkil hai grounding. Air signs kabhi-kabhi itna sochte hain ki kaam karna bhool jate hain — sochna achha hai, par soch ko zameen par lana bhi zaroori hai.

Water (Jal) — Karka, Vrishchika, Meena. Emotions, intuition, aur depth. Water signs ko feelings samajhna aata hai — apni bhi, doosron ki bhi. Inki taakat hai empathy; inki mushkil hai boundaries. Water signs doosron ka dard apna bana lete hain — aur kabhi-kabhi isme khud ko bhool jate hain.

Ek baat aur: har bhav (house) bhi kisi na kisi element se juda hota hai — isliye jab tum birth chart padhna seekhoge, to elements ka yehi shortcut wahan bhi kaam aayega. Astrology houses ka framework — kaunsa bhav zindagi ke kis area ko dikhata hai — elements ke saath mil kar hi poora samajh aata hai.

3 Qualities — Movable, Fixed, Dual

Elements ke baad doosra shortcut hai qualities — har rashi ek quality me aati hai, aur har quality ka ek tareeka hota hai.

Movable (Chara) — Mesha, Karka, Tula, Makara. Ye shuru karne wali rashiyan hain — nayi cheez, naya season, naya kadam. Inhe initiation pasand hai. Mushkil: shuru to kar deti hain, par beech me chhod bhi deti hain.

Fixed (Sthira) — Vrishabha, Simha, Vrishchika, Kumbha. Ye tikne wali rashiyan hain — jo shuru hua, use pakka karna, use banaye rakhna. Inhe stability pasand hai. Mushkil: badlav. Fixed signs ko apni raay badalna sabse mushkil lagta hai.

Dual (Dwiswabhava) — Mithuna, Kanya, Dhanu, Meena. Ye adapt karne wali rashiyan hain — dono taraf dekhna, beech ka raasta nikalna. Inhe flexibility pasand hai. Mushkil: ek jagah tikna. Dual signs ko faisla lena sabse mushkil lagta hai — kyunki dono taraf ke fayde dikhte hain.

Elements aur qualities ko mila kar dekho, to har rashi ki ek unique pehchan banti hai. Mesha = Fire + Movable — aag jo shuru karti hai. Vrishabha = Earth + Fixed — zameen jo tikti hai. Meena = Water + Dual — paani jo beh kar adapt karta hai. Ye do cheezein yaad rakh lo, to 12 rashiyon ki basic nature kabhi nahi bhoolegi.

Apni Rashi Kaise Nikale — 3 Steps

Ab practical baat. Apni rashi nikalne ke teen steps hain — aur teeno ka jawab alag hai.

Step 1: Sun sign — sirf date se. Apni janm tareekh lo aur upar wali table ke Western column me dekho. Ye tumhara Sun sign hai — newspaper wala. 30 second ka kaam hai. Par yaad rakho: ye sirf ek hissa hai, poori kahani nahi.

Step 2: Vedic sun sign — table se, par dhyaan se. Wahi janm tareekh, Vedic column me dekho. Agar tumhara birthday April ke pehle hafte me hai, to Western me tum Aries ho, par Vedic me abhi Meena — kyunki Surya Vedic Meena me 14 March se 13 April tak rehta hai. Ye step isliye zaroori hai kyunki India me jo rashi boli jati hai, wo aam taur par Vedic system se milti hai.

Step 3: Asli rashi (Moon sign) — kundli se. Ye sabse important step hai, aur iske liye sirf date kaafi nahi. Tumhe chahiye: janm tareekh, janm ka samay, aur janm ka sthan. In teeno se ek free birth chart banao — AstroSage, ya kisi bhi free chart tool se — aur dekho ki Chandra kis rashi me tha. Wahi tumhari rashi hai, Indian sense me. Aur usi chart se tumhara Lagna bhi niklega. Iska poora tareeka hamare Birth chart reading guide me hai — Lagna se lekar dasha tak, step by step. Agar tumhe chart banana nahi aata, to pehle wo guide padho, phir is article par wapas aao — dono ek doosre ko poora karte hain.

Aur haan, ek baat aur: agar tumhara birthday kisi boundary par hai — jaise 20 April, 21 June, ya 23 September — to table se faisla mat karo. Surya ek rashi se doosri me ek exact samay par jata hai, aur wo samay har saal ek din aage-peeche ho sakta hai. Sirf chart hi batayega ki us samay Surya kis taraf tha. Ise "cusp" kehte hain, aur iska jawab sirf janm samay se milta hai.

Rashifal (Daily Horoscope) Kya Hai, Kya Nahi

Ab wo sawaal jo har koi poochta hai: daily rashifal padhna chahiye ya nahi? Pehle ye samajh lo ki rashifal hota kya hai. Newspaper aur apps me jo rashifal chhapta hai, wo aam taur par Moon sign se padha jata hai — India me — aur Sun sign se — Western apps me. Isi liye do alag jagah par tumhari rashi ka rashifal alag dikh sakta hai. Dono me se koi bhi tumhari poori kundli nahi padh raha — sirf ek grah ki position se ek broad hint de raha hai.

To rashifal ko kaise lena chahiye? Ek broad guide ki tarah — din ki sochne ki disha, jaise "aaj patience rakho" ya "aaj baat-cheet ke liye achha din hai". Prediction ki tarah nahi — "aaj tumhe naukri milegi" ya "aaj paise ka nuksan hoga" — aisi baatein rashifal se nahi niklti. Aur agar koi rashifal tumhe dara raha ho, ya bada kharcha karne ko keh raha ho — koi puja, koi stone, koi "upay" — to use ignore karo. Wahi sahi faisla hai. Rashifal padhna theek hai, par us par faisla lena galat hai.

Ye 5 Galtiyan Mat Karo

Ab wo galtiyan, jo zodiac signs ko lekar sabse zyada hoti hain. Inse bach gaye, to tum aam readers se ek kadam aage ho.

Galti 1: Sun sign ko poori personality samajhna. "Main Aries hoon, isliye main aisa hoon" — aisa nahi hai. Tum ek poori kundli ho — Lagna, Moon sign, Sun sign, bhav, dasha — sab mil kar. Ek sign se poori pehchan banana, ek photo se poori film samajhne jaisa hai.

Galti 2: Boundary birthday par date table se pakka faisla. 20 April ko paida hue log har saal Aries ya Taurus — dono ho sakte hain. Sirf chart batayega. Table se pakka faisla lena, andaze par faisla lena hai.

Galti 3: Rashi se shaadi ya naukri ka faisla. "Meri rashi uski rashi se match nahi karti, isliye shaadi nahi hogi" — ye galat hai. Matching ek poori kundli ka kaam hai — guna milan, dasha, aur dono charts ko dekh kar hota hai. Sirf Sun sign ya Moon sign se matching nahi hoti. Aur naukri ka faisla to bilkul bhi rashi se nahi hota — skills aur mehnat se hota hai.

Galti 4: Daily horoscope ko prediction samajhna. Rashifal ek broad hint hai, bhavishyavani nahi. Us par paise ka faisla, ya koi bada kadam, kabhi mat lo. Jo rashifal "aaj ye mat karo, warna nuksan hoga" keh kar darata hai, wo rashifal nahi, daraane ka tareeka hai.

Galti 5: Sun sign se gemstone khareedna. Ye sabse mehngi galti hai. Gemstone kabhi Sun sign se nahi chuna jata — wo Lagna aur poori kundli se chuna jata hai, aur wo bhi ek trained astrologer ki salah se. "Main Leo hoon, isliye mujhe ruby chahiye" — aisa soch kar hazaron rupaye kharch karna, sabse aam aur sabse mehngi galti hai. Pehle kundli samjho, phir kisi expert se poochho, phir faisla lo.
