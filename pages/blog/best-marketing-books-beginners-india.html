Best Marketing Books for Beginners in India – Kaunsi Book, Kab, Aur Kyun

October 31, 2026 • Complete Guide 2026

Seedha jawab: Agar tumhe sirf ek marketing book padhni hai, to Seth Godin ki This Is Marketing padho. Aur agar tum apna chhota business, Instagram page, ya YouTube channel chala rahe ho, to uske saath Donald Miller ki Building a StoryBrand jod lo. Pehli book tumhari soch badalti hai, doosri tumhara message saaf karti hai. Baaki 5 books is article me isliye hain kyunki har ek ek alag kaam karti hai — aur yehi farq hai ek lambi list aur ek kaam ki list me.

Honestly, marketing ki kitaabon ki dikkat yehi hai. Har list me wahi 20 naam hote hain, par koi ye nahi batata ki pehle kaunsi, kis ke liye, aur kaunsi abhi mat lo. To ye article wahi karega. Chhota sa. Seedha sa. Aur India ke hisaab se.

Pehle Ye Samajh Lo: Marketing Ka Matlab Ads Nahi Hai

India me bahut se log marketing ka matlab ye samajhte hain: ad chalao, reach badhao, sale aayegi. Kabhi-kabhi aati bhi hai. Par aksar kya hota hai? Ad par paisa kharch hota hai, log dekhte hain, aur phir bhool jaate hain. Kyunki unko samajh hi nahi aaya ki tum bech kya rahe ho, aur kyun tumse hi khareedna chahiye.

Asli marketing usse pehle shuru hoti hai. Tum kis ke liye ho? Tumhara customer kya chahta hai? Tum uski problem ko ek line me bata sakte ho ya nahi? Agar ye teen sawaalon ka jawab saaf nahi hai, to ad sirf ek mehengi awaaz hai jo koi sun nahi raha.

Ye baat chhote seller ke liye aur bhi zyada zaroori hai. Bade brand ka naam hi kaafi hota hai. Tumhara naam koi nahi jaanta. Isliye tumhe trust pehle banana hoga — saaf message se, imaandaari se, aur baar-baar ek hi baat, ek hi tareeke se keh kar. Yehi is article ki poori soch hai.

7 Books, 7 Alag Kaam

Neeche har book ke saath teen cheezein milengi: ye book kya sikhati hai, ye kis ke liye hai, aur isme sabse aam galti kya hai. List ko upar se neeche tak padhna zaroori nahi. Apni zaroorat wali book chuno, wahi se shuru karo.

1. This Is Marketing — Seth Godin (2018)

Godin ka kehna simple hai, par aadat ke khilaaf hai: sab ko bechne ki koshish mat karo. Apne sabse chhote viable audience ko dhoondho — wo chhota group jisko tum sabse achhe se serve kar sakte ho — aur unki itni achhi seva karo ki wo khud tumhare baare me doosron ko batayein.

Ye book kis ke liye hai? Un logon ke liye jo marketing se thoda darte hain, ya jinko lagta hai ki marketing matlab zor-zabardasti bechna hai. Godin marketing ko service ke roop me dikhata hai: samjho, madad karo, phir kahani batao.

Ek imaandaar chetavni bhi hai. Ye book philosophy zyada hai, step-by-step checklist kam. Tum isko padh kar prerit to ho jaoge, par pehla kadam kya ho, ye tumhe khud tay karna hoga. Isliye iske saath agli book itni zaroori hai.

Ek line me: Chhote audience ko brilliantly serve karo, sab ko weakly nahi.

2. Building a StoryBrand — Donald Miller (2017)

Miller ka framework saat hisson ka hai, par uska dil ek line me hai: tumhara customer hero hai, tum guide ho. Bahut si websites aur Instagram bios isliye fail hoti hain kyunki wo apni hi kahani sunati rehti hain — hum kaun hain, humne kya banaya, hum kitne achhe hain. Customer ko isse koi matlab nahi. Usko apni problem ka hal chahiye.

Is book ka practical hissa ye hai: apne offer ko ek line me likho jo koi ajnabi 5 second me samajh le. Phir apne customer ki problem, tumhara plan, aur ek saaf call to action — ye teen cheezein har page, har post, har pitch me ek jaise rakho. Miller ka rule yaad rakhne layak hai: agar tum confuse karoge, to tum haar jaoge.

Kis ke liye nahi? Agar tumhe abhi ye bhi nahi pata ki tumhara customer kaun hai, to pehle Godin wapas padho. StoryBrand message saaf karti hai, customer dhoondh kar nahi deti.

3. Positioning — Al Ries aur Jack Trout (1981)

Ye sabse purani book hai is list me, aur shayad sabse zyada galat samjhi jaane wali bhi. Ries aur Trout kehte hain ki positioning wo nahi hai jo tum product ke saath karte ho. Positioning wo hai jo tum customer ke dimaag me karte ho.

Unka idea ye tha: log har category me brands ko dimaag ki ek seedhi par rakhte hain. Koi pehle number par hai, koi doosre par, baaki bheed me. Tumhara kaam ye tay karna hai ki tum us seedhi par kahan khade hona chahte ho — aur kis cheez ke liye jaane jaana chahte ho. Sab kuch banne ki koshish karoge, to kisi cheez ke liye yaad nahi rahoge.

Haan, iske examples purane hain. 1981 ki duniya me Instagram nahi tha. Par sawal wahi hai jo aaj bhi hai: jab koi tumhara naam sune, to uske dimaag me pehla shabd kya aaye? Agar jawab "kuch bhi" hai, to positioning ka kaam abhi baaki hai.

4. Influence — Robert Cialdini

Ye wo book hai jo batati hai ki log "haan" kyun kehte hain. Cialdini ke chhah mool siddhant — reciprocity, commitment, social proof, authority, liking, aur scarcity — baad ke edition me unity ke saath saat ho jaate hain. Ye siddhant har jagah dikhte hain: "sirf 2 seat bachi hain" scarcity hai, "10,000 log ye le chuke hain" social proof hai, ek free sample reciprocity hai.

Yahan ek zaroori baat. Ye book tumhe samajh deti hai, par ye permission nahi deti ki tum logon ko bewakoof banao. Jhoothi scarcity, nakli reviews, ya dabav wali tactic thode din chal sakti hai, par trust ek baar gaya to wapas nahi aata. India me, jahan chhota seller apne naam par hi bikta hai, ye nuksaan sabse bada hai. Principles ko imaandaari se use karo — jaise asli review dikhana, asli deadline batana — to ye book tumhara sabse bada hathiyar ban jaati hai.

Ek chhota sa test: agar tum apni marketing tactic apne customer ko khul kar bata do, aur phir bhi wo kaam kare, to wo tactic sahi hai. Agar batane me sharam aaye, to wo tactic hi galat hai.

5. Contagious — Jonah Berger (2013)

Kuch cheezein log apne aap share kyun karte hain, aur kuch achhi cheezein bhi kyun dab jaati hain? Berger ka research is sawal ka jawab deta hai. Log wo cheez share karte hain jo unko achha dikhati hai, jo unke dimaag me aasani se aati hai, aur jiske peeche koi emotion hota hai.

Ek example samjho. Ek dukaan ka naam mushkil hai, uska board padha nahi jaata, uski kahani koi bata nahi sakta. Product achha hone ke baad bhi log uska naam doosron tak pahuncha nahi paate. Doosri taraf, ek simple naam, ek yaad rehne wali line, ek chhoti kahani — ye teeno mil kar wo kaam kar dete hain jo hazaron rupaye ka ad nahi kar pata.

Is book ki limit kya hai? Ye tumhe viral hone ka formula nahi deti. Koi book nahi de sakti. Ye tumhe ye samajh deti hai ki share karne layak cheez kaisi hoti hai. Baaki kaam tumhara product aur tumhari consistency karegi.

6. The 1-Page Marketing Plan — Allan Dib

Ab tak ki books ne soch aur message de diya. Ye book plan deti hai — aur wo bhi sirf ek page par. Dib customer ke safar ko teen hisson me baantta hai: pehle (log tumhe kaise dhoondhenge), beech me (wo khareedenge kaise), aur baad me (wo wapas kaise aayenge aur doosron ko kaise laayenge).

Is book ki sabse kaam ki baat leverage wali hai. Dib kehta hai ki marketing hi kisi business ka sabse bada leverage point hai. Ek chhota sa example: agar tumhari website par 100 log aate hain aur 1 khareedta hai, to conversion 1% hai. Agar message saaf kar ke tum usko 2% kar do, to bina ek bhi naya visitor laaye, tumhari sale double ho gayi. Yehi fark hai mehnat aur sahi jagah mehnat me.

Ye book kis ke liye best hai? Us chhote business owner ke liye jiske paas ek lamba marketing plan likhne ka waqt nahi hai, par ek page par apna poora plan dekhna chahta hai.

7. Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook — Gary Vaynerchuk

Aakhri book social media ke liye hai, aur iska naam hi iska formula hai. Jab matlab value do — ek helpful post, ek jawab, ek free tip. Right hook matlab maango — jab tum kuch bechna chahte ho. Bahut se page ulta karte hain: har post me "kharido, kharido", aur phir hairan hote hain ki log unfollow kyun kar rahe hain.

Vaynerchuk ki doosri baat bhi utni hi zaroori hai: har platform par wahi post chipka mat do. Instagram par jo chalta hai, wo WhatsApp status par nahi chalega, aur YouTube par to bilkul nahi. Har platform ki apni bhasha, apni speed, apna tareeka hai. Usi ke hisaab se apni baat dhalo.

Ek side note. Vaynerchuk ki energy bahut zyada hai, aur kabhi-kabhi lagta hai ki har waqt post karna hi jawab hai. Nahi hai. Roz ek achhi post, hafte me saat bekaar post se behtar hai. Consistency ka matlab quantity nahi, bharosa hai.

Ye Books Abhi Mat Lo — Aur Kyun

Ab teen naam jo har list me hote hain, par pehli baar me lene layak nahi hain.

Philip Kotler ki kitaabein marketing ki buniyad hain, par wo textbook jaisi lagti hain. Student ya manager ke liye bahut achhi, par ek beginner jo kal se apna page ya dukaan chalana chahta hai, uske liye pehla kadam nahi. Unko baad ke liye rakh do.

David Ogilvy ki Ogilvy on Advertising advertising ki kala par classic hai. Par agar tum abhi apna message hi saaf nahi kar paaye ho, to advertising ki kala pehle seekhna ulta kadam hai. Pehle StoryBrand, phir Ogilvy.

Aur teesra: koi bhi channel manual — jaise SEO ya ads ke settings wali book. Ye manuals kaam ke hain, par unki umar chhoti hoti hai. Platform ka button badla, to aadhi book purani. Isliye pehle wo books padho jo button par nahi, insaan ke dimaag par based hain. Wo purani nahi hoti.

Hindi Readers Ke Liye Seedhi Baat

Ab wo sawaal jo sabse zyada poochha jaata hai: ye books Hindi me milengi?

Imaandaar jawab ye hai: in classic books ke official Hindi edition har book ke liye aasani se nahi milte, aur jo Hindi version mil jaaye, uska anuvaad hamesha bharosemand nahi hota. Isliye jhootha dawa kar ke koi list banana galat hoga.

To Hindi reader kya kare? Ek rasta ye hai. Book ko English me dheere padho — roz 10 page, dictionary ke saath, jaldi ke bina. Har chapter ke baad uska idea apne shabdon me Hindi me likh lo. Phir us idea ko apni Hindi ya Hinglish content me lagao. Jaise StoryBrand ka hero-guide wala idea tum apni dukaan ke board par, apni Instagram bio me, ya apne WhatsApp message me Hindi me hi to lagaaoge. Book English me hai, par uska kaam tumhari bhasha me hi hoga.

Aur agar English bilkul mushkil lagti hai, to pehle Hindi me marketing ke basic articles aur videos se idea samjho, phir original book par aao. Ulta rasta — sirf summary padh kar book padhne ka dawa karna — lambi race me kaam nahi aata.

4 Hafton Ka Plan — Padhna + Karna

Book kharidna aasaan hai. Us par amal karna mushkil hai. Isliye ek chhota plan:

  1. Hafta 1 — Soch: This Is Marketing shuru karo. Aur ek page par likho: mera sabse chhota audience kaun hai? Ek line me, naam ke saath nahi, pehchaan ke saath.
  2. Hafta 2 — Message: Building a StoryBrand padho. Apni website, bio ya dukaan ke board ka headline dobara likho — customer hero, tum guide.
  3. Hafta 3 — Plan: The 1-Page Marketing Plan ke hisaab se apna ek page ka plan banao: log kaise aayenge, kaise khareedenge, wapas kaise aayenge.
  4. Hafta 4 — Content: Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook se apne agle 7 post ka plan banao — 6 value wale, 1 maangne wala. Phir unko post karo, aur dekho kaunsa chala.

Baaki 3 books — Positioning, Influence, Contagious — uske baad, jab ye chaar hafton ka kaam ho jaaye. Kyunki us waqt tumhare paas apna experience hoga, aur ye books tumhe apne experience ko samajhne me madad karengi, sirf theory nahi lagengi.

Kya Sirf Books Kaafi Hain? Nahi.

Ye article ka sabse zaroori hissa shayad yehi hai. Books tumhe soch dengi, par platform ke rules har mahine badalte hain, customer ki aadat badalti hai, aur tumhara competitor bhi to baithe nahi hai. Jo sirf padhta hai, wo peeche reh jaata hai. Jo padh kar ek chhota experiment karta hai — ek naya headline, ek naya offer, ek naya post ka tareeka — aur uska result note karta hai, wo aage badhta hai.

Ek aur chetavni. Influence jaisi books padh kar kuch log ye soch lete hain ki psychology ka matlab logon ko phansaana hai. Nahi. Asli marketing me tum customer ki madad karte ho ek achha faisla lene me. Agar tumhara product uski problem hal nahi karta, to koi book, koi tactic, koi ad usko lambi race me nahi bacha sakta. Pehle product theek karo, phir marketing ki book uthao.

Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

Marketing seekhne ke liye sabse pehli book kaunsi padhni chahiye?

Agar sirf ek book padhni hai to Seth Godin ki This Is Marketing. Agar apna business ya page chalana hai to uske saath Building a StoryBrand jod lo.

Kya marketing books Hindi me milti hain?

In classic books ke official Hindi edition har book ke liye aasani se nahi milte. Hindi readers ke liye behtar rasta ye hai: English original ko dheere padho, idea Hindi me samjho, aur apply apni Hindi ya Hinglish content me karo.

Bina paise lagaye marketing seekhne ke liye kaunsi book best hai?

This Is Marketing aur Building a StoryBrand — dono ka zor soch aur message par hai, ad budget par nahi.

Marketing ki books padhne ke baad bhi sales kyun nahi badhti?

Aksar isliye kyunki book ka idea apply hi nahi hua. Har book ke baad ek chhota kaam karo: headline badlo, ek post ka message saaf karo, ya ek offer ka plan likho.

Digital marketing ke liye alag book chahiye ya yehi kaafi hain?

Ye 7 books buniyad hain — soch, message, position aur psychology. Channel ki detail ke liye baad me ek channel manual lo, par pehle buniyad pakki karo.
